# License, access, and provenance

- Nallino’s 1899–1907 edition is a historical public-domain source. The Google/Princeton scan is evidence, not authorship. “Digitized by Google,” the Princeton plate/barcode, UI cards, and binding matter must never enter the scholarly body.
- The PAL al-Battānī commentary transcription was acquired from exact Git commit `762614d992ac588389f10309cb180e0a34b3459c`. PAL’s exact license file states CC BY 4.0 applies to all transcriptions except one unrelated Quadripartitum edition. That excluded edition is not in the attached subtree.
- The 2009–2010 *Astrological History* publication is supplied by the Universitat de Barcelona repository under CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 ES. It is a complete source/reference witness. Do not republish or adapt the editors’ article under an incompatible license. Any new transcription of al-Battānī’s underlying public-domain text must be independently grounded, clearly attributed, and kept distinct from their translation/commentary.
- The BEA PDF and provider pages in the evidence archive establish identity, extent, and access claims; they are not production copy.
- Unknown or restricted manuscript access is recorded as a gap, not bypassed.

Attribution must name al-Battānī for his works and must separately credit Nallino, Kennedy and colleagues, Nadine Löhr/PAL, manuscript repositories, and source providers in their actual roles.
