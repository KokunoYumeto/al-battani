% Chapter 44 continued: the time of the middle of a solar eclipse: when the third parallax exceeds the second, or falls short of it, the hours are moved by a sixth (an eighth, a tenth) of an hour and the corrected motion of the Moon gives the corrected hours of the second parallax; the places of Sun and Moon carried to that time.
*laxis super secundam crescit.* — *Si tertia parallaxis est secunda minor, parallaxis tunc*
*est minor quam minuta inter Solem et Lunam posita, quantitate qua tertia parallaxis*
*a secunda superatur.* — Oportet igitur cognoscere tempus, in quo spatium inter Solem et
Lunam debet ut parallaxis lunaris longitudinis esse; id tempus enim est medium eclipsis. Hanc
cognitionem ut nunc dicam adipisceris.
