⟨19⟩ On the brothers
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.5.
@p AB03-F039v-L04..AB03-F040r-L08
As for the matter of the brothers: if one examines it in general, without going beyond the possible measure in seeking the number of the brothers accurately, one by one, he can know the matter of the brothers who are of one mother, by the natural method, from the sign that is in the middle of the sky from the place of the mother, I mean from the place of Venus by day and the place of the moon by night among the signs. Since this sign, and the sign that rises to its place, are the place from which the children of the mother are known, that must be the place of the brothers. If the beneficent planets share in a configuration with these places, we judge from that that the brothers are many, base our conjecture on the number of these planets, and look whether they are in signs of one form or in bicorporeal signs. If the maleficent planets overpower the place, or face it in opposition on the diameter, being in the house opposite it, they signify few brothers, especially when the sun enters with them. If the sun (?) is on the cardines, and especially on the ascendant, and then the planet in one of these places is Saturn, it is an indication of this among the first-born; if it is Mars, it lessens the number of the brothers by death. As for the planets that give brothers: if their condition, in their position in the world, is praiseworthy, we should suppose that the brothers will be persons of standing and noble; if the matter is the opposite, they will be lowly and obscure. If the maleficent planets overpower the giving ones, or follow them, they will be short-lived too. Those of them that give males are the planets masculine in their condition in the world, and those that give females the feminine in their condition in the world; again, the eastern ones give the first brothers and the western the last. Besides this, if the giving planets share in an agreeing configuration with the sign in charge of the matter of the brothers, they signify that the brothers love one another; if they agree with the lot of good fortune, they signify that the brothers share in their affairs; and if they are in signs between which there is no connection, or in the condition opposite to the one we have mentioned, they signify that the brothers are hostile and envious of one another and, for the most part, cunning. If one wishes to examine each of the matters of the brothers more than this, he can also conjecture about it by setting up the giving planet in the place of the ascendant and considering all their other matters as one considers a nativity.

Others than Ptolemy among the ancients looked into the matters of the brothers from the third house from the ascendant, Mars, the lot of the brothers, and their lords. If most of these significators, especially the planets that have many shares in these places, are in the signs of many offspring, and the sign of the house of the brothers likewise, they signify many brothers; if they are otherwise, they signify few. If most of them, especially those with many shares, are in the masculine signs, most of them are males; if in feminine signs, most of them are females. Speak of their safety and death, and of the soundness and corruption of their condition, from the soundness of the condition of the significators and of the house of the brothers, and from their corruption. Look at the powers of the lord (?) of the triplicity of the ascendant. If it is in the ascendant, [the native] is a first-born; if it is in the middle of the sky, he is a first-born or a fourth; if in the seventh, a first-born or a seventh; if it is in a place other than a cardine, above the earth, count from it to the ascendant: if there is a maleficent planet between them, it signifies that some of the children miscarried or died; if they are beneficent, or in some of their own dignities, strong, in agreeing signs and in praiseworthy places, they signify the number of those born before him. If the lord of the triplicity is below the earth, count from the ascendant to it, as we said first. They also looked, for the number of those born before him, at the number of the planets found between the middle of the sky and the ascendant: if no planet is found there, he is a first-born, or if one was born before him, it died; and, for the number of those born after him, at the number of the planets found between the ascendant and the fourth. They said that when the moon and the ascendant of the native are in signs of many offspring, the mother has many children. If the moon is found in Scorpio, in three degrees of it, she has no children, or if they were born they did not survive but died. If we find both lords of the triplicity of the ascendant in the places third from the cardines, he has no brothers, or if he has, they are scattered and do not come together in the land. If you find the lord of the house of the brothers in the ascendant, he is single, with no brother; if it is found in the middle of the sky, he has a brother older than he; and if it is found in [the house of] the parents, he has a brother younger than he.

If the house of the brothers is found to be a bicorporeal sign, and its lord also in a bicorporeal sign, especially when the sign in which the lot of the brothers is is so too, it signifies that he will have brothers from another father or from another mother. For their love and enmity one should look at the lot of the brothers and its lord: if they look at one another from trine or sextile, it signifies mutual love; if from quadrature, a middling friendship; if from opposition, enmity; and if they do not look at one another, it signifies estrangement and seldom meeting. The same is known from the look of the planet that prevails over the ascendant by its shares at the one that prevails over the house of the brothers, the lot of the brothers and Mars. If the lord of the lot of good fortune is with the lot of the brothers, and the lord of the lot of the brothers with the lord of the lot of good fortune, it signifies that they benefit one another, and they may get much wealth from their brothers. For the death of the brothers one looks to the lot and to the lord of the lot of the brothers, at the conjunction, quadrature and opposition of the maleficent planets, especially when the maleficent planet is in its first station or retrograde, and above all when Jupiter does not look at the lot. If one of the two maleficent planets looks at the lot while it is in the dignities of Mercury, it signifies that his brothers are killed; and if one of the two maleficent planets is in the sign in which Mercury is and looks at the lot of the brothers from opposition and quadrature, we judge likewise. For the sisters Venus is used in the place of Mercury.
