⟨3⟩ On the powers of the four seasons of the year and of the ages of animals
@n *Tetrabiblos* I.10.
@p AB03-F021r-L09..AB03-F021r-L13
In spring moisture prevails, in summer heat, in autumn dryness and in winter cold. In the same way, in the first age of animals the moist substance prevails; in the second age, up to the time of the prime, heat; in the third age, when it nears the decline and begins to decrease, dryness; and in the last of the ages, when it nears dissolution, cold: like spring, summer, autumn and winter.
