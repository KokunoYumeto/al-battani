⟨2⟩ On the powers of the planets according to the configurations they have relative to the sun
@n *Tetrabiblos* I.8.
@p AB03-F020v-L19..AB03-F021r-L03
The moon and the three upper planets receive increase and decrease in their particular powers according to the configurations they have with the sun. From the time when the moon appears as a crescent to the time of its first half in light, it produces moisture above all; from its first half to its full, when it is in opposition to the sun, heat; from its full to its second half in light, dryness; and from its second half to its disappearance, cold.
@p AB03-F021r-L03..AB03-F021r-L08
Saturn, Jupiter and Mars, from the time of their rising in the mornings and their appearing from under the rays of the sun to the time of their first station, moisten more; from their first station to their rising at the beginning of the night, which is when they are in opposition to the sun, they heat more; from their rising at the beginning of the night to their second station, they dry more; and from their second station to their setting and disappearance, they cool more. When they mix with one another, they produce in the air very many kinds of very different conditions, and these conditions are called the causes of the stars.
