⟨23⟩ On those who are not nourished and those who are not reared
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.9.
@p AB03-F042v-L12..AB03-F043r-L02
Since there remains, of what one wishes to know of what is at the time of birth, the account of those who are not nourished, one should know that this account is in some respects joined to the account of the length of life, since it is not far from each of these two kinds, and in some respects distinct from it, in that the force of this inquiry differs somewhat. For the account of the length of life concerns all who have a perceptible life, those whose life is not shorter than one of the revolutions of the sun, I mean a year; and life in potency is also what is less than this, months, days and hours. The account of those who are not nourished is the account of those who reach none of the times we have mentioned, because of the excess of evil and harm upon them; and for this reason the inquiry into the length of life has many parts, and the inquiry into those who are not nourished is general and simple.
@n «Kinds», «distinct from it», «shorter than» and «in potency» follow the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (الأجناس، متباينًا له، دون، بالقوّة).
@p AB03-F043r-L02..AB03-F043v-L07
In general, when one of the luminaries is on one of the cardines, and [one of] the two maleficent planets shares with it in longitude, either being with it degree for degree, or in opposition, or in an isosceles configuration, and none of the beneficent planets shares in a configuration with it, and the governor of the place of the luminaries is found with the maleficent planets, the child is not nourished and dies at once. If it is not in an isosceles configuration, but the casting of the rays of the maleficent planets is near the place of the luminaries, rising to them — he means that the luminaries are near them, in the parts that come after the parts of the luminaries in the signs in which they are — or if the maleficent planets are two and harm one of the luminaries or both: either by rising to their place, or to the place of one of them, as we have said, by conjunction; or by facing it, or both of them, on the diameter; or each of the maleficent planets harming one of the luminaries; or one of them in opposition to one of the luminaries and the other rising to the place of the other by way of conjunction, being then, as we have described, in the parts that follow the parts of the luminaries: then the child has no life. For the amount of harm in this matter corrupts and hides the benefit and soundness in prolonging life that come from the distance of the place that rises to the place of the luminaries. What harms the sun in particular, of what is in the place that rises to its place, [is Mars], and the moon, Saturn; but in what faces them on the diameter, or overpowers them, the matter is the reverse: Saturn harms the sun and Mars the moon, especially if they have dominion over the place of the luminaries and the ascendant by governing them. If there are two oppositions on the diameter, and the luminaries and the two maleficent planets are on the cardines in an isosceles configuration, the infants are then born either dead or in a state between life and death.
@n The words from «the place of the luminaries in prolonging life» to «rises to its place» are written in the margin; the name of Mars is missing.
@p AB03-F043v-L07..AB03-F044r-L07
When these things are so, and the luminaries are separating from one of the beneficent planets, or share a configuration with it, and the rays reach the preceding parts, the child lives for the number of the signs between the haylāj and the nearest ray of the maleficent planet, as so many months, days or hours, according to the amount of the badness of the condition and the strength of the things that are its causes. If the rays of the maleficent planets reach the preceding parts, the child lives for the number of the degrees between the haylāj and the nearest ray of the maleficent planet, as so many months, days or hours, according to the amount of the badness of the condition and the strength of the things that are its causes. If the rays of the maleficent planets reach the parts preceding the luminaries and the rays of the beneficent the parts that follow them, the child is nourished and lives. Again, if the maleficent planets prevail over the beneficent ones that share in the configuration, the child will be of a bad condition and abased; if the beneficent overpower, the child passes to others than its parents. If one of the beneficent planets rises with the moon or applies to it, while one of the maleficent is setting, the child is reared by its parent. In this way the consideration is made for multiple children: if one of the planets that share in configurations two by two or more is in the west, the child is born between dead and alive, or like a lump of flesh, or deficient and incomplete in form; and if one of the maleficent planets overpowers it, the child is not reared and has no life, as that requires.
@n «Separating» reads منصرفين for مضرّين, «harming»; «for the number», «the maleficent planet», «prevail» and «abased» follow the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (مدّة، المنحس، غالية، ذليلًا). The second sentence repeats the first with «degrees» for «signs»; Ptolemy counts degrees.
@p AB03-F044r-L07..AB03-F044v-L06
The ancients also said about the rearing: Look at the [lords of the] triplicities of the ascendant and of the sun by day, and at the lords of the triplicities of the moon and of the lot of good fortune by night. If you find the greatest of them, or the one of greatest authority among them, in good places of the sphere and of the signs — especially the middle of the sky, the eleventh and the fifth — clean of misfortunes and defects, it signifies rearing and life. If Venus is clean of defects and is in the fifteen degrees above the ascendant, and Jupiter, being clean, looks at her, it signifies rearing. If the moon is not afflicted, and none of the maleficent planets is in the cardines, and in the ascendant, the middle of the sky or the eleventh there is one of the diurnal planets by day or of the nocturnal by night, it signifies rearing. When the lords of the triplicities are in the middle of the sky, the eleventh and the fifth, they signify ease in rearing; when they are in the seventh, «والفنّ», difficulty (?) in rearing, especially when they are maleficent. When the cardines are upright, and the planets free from misfortunes, clean of defect, in soundness of their mixtures, and the lords of the houses in good places, in the terms of the beneficent planets, and their rays and the luminaries agree with one another and with the ascendant, it signifies long life. Look at the planet of greatest authority among the lords of the triplicities of the ascendant and of the sun by day, and of the moon and the lot of good fortune by night, whether it be one or two. If it happens to be one, and it is fallen and conjoined with the degree of a maleficent planet, and the degree of the ascendant is also conjoined with the moon, it is an indication of short survival. Likewise when a maleficent planet is conjoined with the degree of the ascendant, or with the degree of the luminary of the domain, and likewise with the lords of the triplicities of the luminary; and likewise when all the lords of the triplicities of the ascendant decline, and the luminaries and their lords decline, and the conjunction or the full moon and its lord decline: then there is no survival for the child. If the dominant planet has fallen from the cardines and is afflicted, but there are some degrees between it and the maleficent planet, the child survives for as many days, months or years as there are degrees between the dominant planet and the maleficent.
@n «Clean» reads نقيًّا for بقيًا; «a maleficent planet» النحس for الحسن, «the good»; «short» قلّة for فلة; and «no survival» لا إبقاء for الإبقاء, «survival».
@p AB03-F044v-L06..AB03-F045r-L13
Likewise when the maleficent planet is in the cardine and the dominant planet has fallen from the cardine, or the degree of the ascendant or the degree of the moon is looked at by a maleficent planet: he survives for the amount of those degrees, as days, months or years. When the moon is afflicted and in a cardine, especially the ascendant and the middle of the sky, and no beneficent planet looks at it, it signifies short survival; especially if the maleficent planet is with the degree of the moon or the degree of the ascendant, or in quadrature or opposition to them, in the degree and the term; and especially if it is with the degree of the ascendant and of the moon together: for when it is so, even if the beneficent planets then look at it, he does not live. If the moon is in the seventh, afflicted by bodily conjunction, quadrature or opposition with the degree of a maleficent planet, and no beneficent planet looks at it, judge with certainty that he will not live. If the moon is in the fourth cardine and the maleficent planet faces it, especially if another maleficent planet is together with the moon, the child and its mother will not both escape death on the day he is born; unless the moon is in its house, its exaltation or its triplicity, and a beneficent planet looks with strength at that maleficent one from the trine: that signifies that both escape, because a beneficent planet in the place of its dignities increases in strength and good, and a maleficent planet in the place of its dignities decreases in evil; especially when Mars is in a feminine sign and Saturn in a masculine sign. If the moon is as I have described, besieged between two maleficent planets, with no beneficent planet looking at it, and it is besides decreasing in light, then besides signifying the death of the child it signifies the loss of his sight before that. If there is a maleficent planet in the ascendant and one in the seventh, and the moon is in the cardine of the ascendant or of the middle of the sky, and the beneficent planets do not look at it, it signifies, with death, that he is put out of his parents' house before he dies; especially if the moon is in the term of the maleficent planets and the lord of that term is in a cardine, for that is worse and more harmful. If most of [the lords of] the triplicities fall, especially in the sixth and the twelfth, and above all the one of greatest authority among them, they signify short survival; and the time in that is [found] by looking at the beneficent planet, when it is in a cardine or in a good, strong place, or at the planet that applies to the degree of the ascendant, of the luminaries and of the lot of good fortune: it signifies [the life] by the amount of its period (?), in years, months or days. When the maleficent planets are in the ascendant, near it in degrees — even if there are beneficent planets there nearer in degrees to the degree of the ascendant — if the other significators are strong for rearing and life, it signifies that he will be deficient in mind and limbs, with many injuries to the end of his life, and in any case he will only be of short life. When you find the sign in which the conjunction or the full moon is, the lord of the term that looks, which is called «المعنق», the lord of the term of the luminaries and of the ascendant, and the lords of the triplicities that we have mentioned — especially the triplicity of the sun by day and of the moon by night — and the lot of good fortune, all afflicted, judge that he will come out of his mother's womb dead, or come out dead from his mother's womb.
@n «Is in» reads وهو for ونحن, «and we»; «bodily conjunction» مجاسدة for محاسدة; «dignities» حظوظه and الحظوظ for خطوطه and الخطوط, «lines»; «a maleficent planet in the place of its dignities» النحس for الشمس, «the sun»; «a feminine sign» برج for موج, «wave»; «the luminaries» النيّرين for المضيين.
