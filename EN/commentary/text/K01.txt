⟨1⟩ On knowing the power of the sun, the moon, the wandering stars and the fixed stars
@n Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos* I.4 and I.9. The chapters of the *Tetrabiblos* are cited in F. E. Robbins's numbering.
@p AB03-F020v-L03..AB03-F020v-L11
Abū ʿAbd Allāh Muḥammad ibn Jābir al-Ḥarrānī, known as al-Battānī, said: The action of the substance of the sun is to dry and to heat, the drying being slight. As for the moon, most of the action of its power is moistening, and it changes bodies visibly, because it ripens and rots most of what is in them; it also shares a little in heat with the sun, because it receives light from it. Saturn acts mostly by cooling, and it dries a little, because of its cold and dryness. Jupiter is of a temperate mixture, for it heats and moistens together, and it produces fertilizing winds. Mars dries above all, and most of the action of its power is burning, because its nature is burning, its colour being like the colour of fire. Venus is of a temperate mixture, except that it moistens much and heats a little. As for Mercury, its moistening and its drying are equal: at some times it dries and absorbs moisture, at others it moistens, and it passes quickly from one state to the other; and it generates winds.
@n «Fire» reads النار with the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (PAL's T); the manuscript has العار, «shame».
@p AB03-F020v-L11..AB03-F020v-L18
As for the powers of the fixed stars, from their mixtures, compared with the powers and natures of the wandering stars as they were found for them when they were observed: we have set them down in the tables of the positions of the fixed stars, in a book other than this, and drawn beside each star a mark by which the wandering stars that its nature resembles are known. Some resemble in their mixture the mixture of one wandering star; some resemble the mixture of two stars equally; and some resemble one of two stars much and the other a little. Their natures resemble [the wandering stars] as the marks found there show, and for that reason we can dispense with mentioning their powers here.
@n «Beside» reads إزاء for the transcription's إذا. Ptolemy gives the powers of the fixed stars in *Tetrabiblos* I.9. The star catalogue of al-Battānī's *Zīj*, as Nallino edits it from the Escorial codex, has no such marks.
