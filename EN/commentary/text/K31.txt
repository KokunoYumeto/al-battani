⟨31⟩ On the native's craft and work
@n *Tetrabiblos* IV.4.
@p AB03-F064v-L19..AB03-F065r-L12
The native's craft is known from two directions, from the sun and from the signs in the middle of the sky. For we need to look at the planet that has lately appeared in the mornings from under the sun, and at the one that is in the middle of the sky, especially when it applies to the moon. If we find both these conditions in one planet, we should use this planet alone; and likewise, if one of these two conditions belongs to a planet and the other belongs to none of the planets, we use the planet that is in the one condition only. If the one that has lately appeared in the mornings from under the sun is one planet, and the one in the middle of the sky that is familiar with the moon is another, we use them both, putting first the one that outweighs in the number of its dignities in the governing, in the manner we have mentioned before. If we find no planet that has lately appeared from under the rays in the mornings, nor any in the middle of the sky, we should take the lord of the middle of the sky, but only for occasional works, since one in whom the condition is this is for the most part idle. This is the way of distinguishing the planets that govern the matter of craft and work.
@n «The planet» follows the Berlin manuscript (الكوكب) for الكواكب. The words from «we should use this planet» to «belongs to a planet» are written in the margin; the Berlin manuscript lacks them. «Outweighs» reads أرجحهما for أن جحهما, and «dignities» حظوظ for خطوط, «lines».
@p AB03-F065r-L13..AB03-F065v-L07
The kind of craft and work we distinguish from the particular condition of three planets, I mean Mars, Venus and Mercury, and from the condition of the signs in which they happen to be. When the maker of the craft is Mercury, for instance, the native is a scribe, a person of business dealings, a calculator, a teacher, a merchant, a money-changer, a soothsayer, an astrologer and a sacrificer, and in general one who deals in writing, interpretation and taking (?). If Saturn witnesses to it, the native is a manager of affairs, an interpreter of dreams, or a frequenter of the temples for the sake of divination and *enthousiasmos*. If Jupiter witnesses to it, the native is a lawgiver (?), a person of eloquence and wisdom, moving among people of rank. If the maker of the work is Venus, the native is one who deals with the scents of flowers, or with perfume, wine, dyes, spices or things of adornment, such as a perfumer, a maker of wreaths (?), a tavern-keeper, a wine merchant, a druggist, a weaver, a seller of spices, a painter or a clothier. If Saturn witnesses to it, the native's trade is in the things used for pleasure and adornment, and the native is a deceiver, a sorcerer and the like. If Jupiter witnesses to it, the native is a wrestler, crowned, worthy of honours, and the cause of the native's rise comes through women.
@n «Taking» reads والأخذ for فالأحد; Ptolemy has giving and taking. «*Enthousiasmos*» is the Greek ἐνθουσιασμός, written والابنو سماء سيموس (the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* أنثوسيما سموس). «A lawgiver» reads واضع سنن for واضع سرّ; Ptolemy has law-makers. «Moving» follows the Berlin manuscript (متقلّبًا) for متغلّبًا, «prevailing». «Flowers» reads الزهر for الزهرة, «Venus», and «a perfumer» عطّار for عطارد, «Mercury»; «a maker of wreaths» guesses at صاحب آكلة. The Arabic *Tetrabiblos* adds «or colours» after «dyes» and «or a dyer» after «a painter».
@p AB03-F065v-L07..AB03-F065v-L16
As for Mars: if it shares a configuration with the sun, the native's craft is one of the crafts that use fire, such as a cook, a founder, a stoker, a coppersmith or a worker in mines. If it does not share with the sun, the native's craft is one of the crafts worked with iron, such as shipbuilding and other carpentry, farming, quarrying and dressing stone, cutting gems, splitting wood, and the like. If Saturn witnesses to it, the native is a seaman, a drawer of water, a toiler (?), a cook, a painter or an attendant in the baths. If Jupiter witnesses to it, the native is a soldier, a policeman, a collector of tithes, an innkeeper, a ferryman (?), a builder or a slaughterer.
@n Before «use fire» the transcription has «worked with iron, such as», from the next sentence. «A founder» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (سبّاك) for {سماك}. «Shipbuilding» and «carpentry» read نجارة for تجارة, «trade»; «gems» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (الفصوص) for الحيوص (the Berlin manuscript الحصوص), and «splitting» its وشقّ for وقصّ. «A ferryman» reads معبّرًا for {مغريًا}; Ptolemy has ferrymen.
@p AB03-F065v-L16..AB03-F066r-L18
Again, if two planets take charge of the matter of craft, and they are Mercury and Venus, the craft is music, instruments of song and amusement, poetry and the composition of melodies, especially when they have exchanged their places. When the matter is so, the native is an entertainer, a showman of images, a dealer in slaves, a maker of instruments of amusement, a twister of strings, a dancer, a painter, a weaver or a composer of songs. If Saturn too witnesses to them, the native's craft, besides these things mentioned, is that of a merchant and seller of ornaments. If Jupiter witnesses to them, the native is a pleader in lawsuits, entrusted with matters of accounts, frequenting the gatherings of people and the gates of the ruler, a teacher of children, and [in charge] of the affairs of the common people. If Mercury and Mars together take charge of the matters of craft, the native is a maker of statues and of weapons, an engraver of things in the temples, a maker of statues in animal forms, a wrestler, one who treats with the hand, a satirist, wanton, of bad deeds, and a forger of documents. If Saturn witnesses to them, the native is a killer, a snatcher of other people's clothes, a robber, a highwayman, a raider and crafty. If Jupiter witnesses to them, the native is fond of weapons or of single combat, firm, wicked in works, acting as an agent for strangers, and makes a living from such things. If Mars and Venus together take charge of the matter of craft, the native is a dyer, a perfumer, a worker in gold and silver or in tin and lead, a farmer, a player with weapons, a maker of drugs and a physician who treats with drugs. If Saturn witnesses to them, the native is in charge of the animals slaughtered to God, a shrouder of the dead, a mourner, a piper over the springs (?), a person of *enthousiasmos*, dwelling in places where there are circlings (?) and the shedding of blood. If Jupiter witnesses to them, the native is a frequenter of the temple, an augur from birds, a guardian who gives women in marriage (?), and an arranger of marriages, living from such things, in comfort and at ease.
@n «A dealer» reads نخّاس for نحاس, «copper»; «a dancer» follows the Berlin manuscript (رقّاصًا) for قاصًا, «a storyteller». «A wrestler» follows the Berlin manuscript ({مصارعًا}) for {مضاهًا}, «an imitator», and «a satirist» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (هجّاء) for هما. «Single combat» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (للمبارزة) for للزبارة, and «firm» the Berlin manuscript and the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (ضابطًا) for ظابطًا. «In charge» reads قيّمًا for فيما; for «a person of *enthousiasmos*» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has أنثوسيمًا, the transcription {أينوسنًا سيموس}. Ptolemy has pipers at funerals, and mysteries and laments where the Arabic has «circlings».
@p AB03-F066r-L18..AB03-F066v-L14
The forms proper to the signs in which the planets are also help. The signs whose figures are human figures help towards all the crafts that come from knowledge and that people need; the signs whose figures resemble four-footed beasts help towards the crafts concerned with metals, carpentry, building and trade; the signs of the solstices and the equinoxes help towards the crafts of interpretation, transport, geometry, farming and the matters of the temples; and the signs whose figures resemble land (?) animals help towards the crafts carried on in liquids and using liquids, herbs, shipbuilding, burial, and the making of salted or salt things. If the lord of the place of the craft is the moon in particular, moving after the conjunction with Mercury, and that is in Taurus, Capricorn or Cancer, the native is a soothsayer, a sacrificer, and one who looks into basins of water; if it is in Sagittarius or Pisces, one who looks into divination from the dead and one who stirs up the jinn; if in Virgo or Scorpio, a sorcerer, an astrologer, a teller of the unseen, knowing things before they come; and if in Libra, Aries or Leo, a person of revelation, an interpreter of dreams and one who makes known (?). This is the way that should be followed in knowing the condition of the craft and its kind, by way of inference.
@n For «the forms» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «the things» (أشياء). «Metals» reads المعدنيّة for العونيّة, and «shipbuilding» نجارة for تجارة. «Land» reads البرّيّ for البريء, as in chapter 26; Ptolemy has terrestrial and aquatic signs. «Basins» follows the Berlin manuscript and the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (طساس) for طساش, «Sagittarius» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (الرامي) for الرأس, and «one who makes known» its معرّفًا for مغرمًا; Ptolemy has exorcists.
@p AB03-F066v-L14..AB03-F067r-L04
Its measure of strength should be considered from the strength of the governing planets. When they are eastern or on the cardines, the crafts are crafts of leadership and power; when they are western or declining from the cardines, the crafts are under the hands of others. When the beneficent planets prevail over them, they are noble, honourable and profitable, never failing the one who practises them, and praised; when the maleficent prevail over them, they are vile, base and ruinous, and fail the one who practises them. If Saturn is the one opposing, coldness comes into them, and slowness and delay prevail in them; if Mars is the one that opposes, danger and ugliness come into them; and if both are opposing, these matters are utterly bad for the whole of life. Their increase and decrease are also known from the condition of the planets that are the cause of the event, relative to the cardines attributed to the evenings.
@n «Prevail» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (غلبت) for علّت, both times, and «the cause» (سبب) for بيت, «house».
