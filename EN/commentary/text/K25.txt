⟨25⟩ On the form of the native's body, his appearance and his temperament
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.11.
@p AB03-F050v-L09..AB03-F051r-L02
Since the conditions of the body precede the conditions of the soul by nature, because the body is grosser, we should examine, for the most part, the eastern horizon and the planet among the wandering stars that follows it, or that takes charge of its governing in the manner we have mentioned before, if there is a planet there too; and in particular one should examine the matter of the moon in the same way. For the form and the condition of bodies are known only from the form of these two places together, from the form of the planets that govern them, from the mixing of each kind of them, and also from the form of the figures of the fixed stars that rise with them. What comes first among them, in power over the power of the others, is the planets that take charge of the governing, and after them the condition proper to the places themselves. The particular account that can be given of their matters as a simple, unrestricted report is as I describe to you.
@n «One should examine» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (فينبغي).
@p AB03-F051r-L02..AB03-F051r-L18
Ptolemy says of the planets that when Saturn takes charge of the governing and is eastern, the native's appearance is dark (?) and robust of body, with black, curly and thick hair, middling eyes and moderate size; as for his temperament, cold and moisture prevail in it. When it is western, the native is dark, lean and small of body, with straight and sparse hair, well put together, the limbs in proportion to one another as they should be, and black-eyed; as for his temperament, dryness prevails in it. When Jupiter takes charge of these places mentioned and is eastern, the native is white of colour, with good hair, middling eyes, good stature and size, and dignified; as for his temperament, heat and moisture prevail in it. When it is western, the native is white, but not of as good a colour as the one we have mentioned, with straight hair, bald at the temples, with middling eyes, and small of body; as for his temperament, moisture prevails in it. If Mars too is eastern and takes charge of the governing, the appearance is between white and red, and the native is of good size, robust of body and blue-eyed, with thick, middling hair; as for the temperament, heat and dryness prevail in it. If it is western, the native is in appearance red of colour, plainly red, small of body and small of eyes, with sparse hair, straight and reddish; as for his temperament, dryness prevails in it.
@n «Dark» stands for the unclear العمل; Ptolemy has dark-skinned. «Robust» follows the Berlin manuscript (مخصب) for مخضّب, «dyed»; «curly» stands twice. «Western» reads غربيًّا for غريبًا, «foreign»; «straight» reads سبط for بسط. Ptolemy gives the western Saturn cold and dry, and the western Jupiter and Mars average height.
@p AB03-F051r-L18..AB03-F051v-L08
Venus has effects like Jupiter's, except that the one who comes from her is more beautiful and has more charm, and his beauty is more like the beauty of women; he is of better figure, of more praiseworthy character and softer of body; and it is proper to her to make the eyes grey, and plainly beautiful. When Mercury takes charge of the governing and is eastern, the native is like honey in colour, moderate of body, well put together, small of eyes, with middling hair; as for his temperament, heat prevails in it. When it is western, the native is between dark and yellow, lean and thin of voice, with sunken eyes whose pupils are like the pupils of oxen, inclining to red; as for his temperament, dryness prevails in it. Each of these planets is helped by the sun and the moon when they share in a configuration with it. The sun helps towards good looks, beauty and robustness of body; the moon helps towards moderation, slenderness and moisture of temperament, especially when it is separating from them. In particular, its help is according to the properties of the light that is in it, as we mentioned at the beginning of this book.
@n «The moon» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (القمر) for الزهرة, «Venus», and «good looks» likewise (الحسنة) for السنة; «slenderness» reads القضف for العصف. For «oxen» (البقر) the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has المغر, «reddish», and for «its help» «the knowledge of it» (معرفته). Ptolemy gives the western Mercury glancing, brilliant eyes.
@p AB03-F051v-L09..AB03-F051v-L13
Again, in general, the planets make bodies large when they are attributed to the mornings and are appearing; when they stand at the first station they make them strong and hardy; when they are retrograde (?) they make them moderate; when they stand at the second station they make them weaker; and when they set they make them base, a prey to blights and hardships. The places themselves in which they are also help towards the appearance of the body, its form and its temperament in particular, as we said just now.
@n «Retrograde» renders the doubtful reading راجعة, the motion between the two stations; the Berlin manuscript has {قواجيد} and the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* مقدّمة, «going ahead».
@p AB03-F051v-L13..AB03-F052r-L02
Again, in general, the quarter from the spring equinox to the summer solstice makes the native of good colour and size, robust of body, with beautiful eyes, moisture and heat prevailing in him. The quarter from the summer solstice to the autumn equinox makes the native of middling colour, moderate in the size of the body, robust, with large eyes and thick, curly hair, heat and dryness prevailing in him. The quarter from the autumn equinox to the winter solstice makes the native's colour like the colour of honey, and makes him spare, thin of voice, broad of shoulders, with middling hair and beautiful eyes, dryness and cold prevailing in him. The quarter from the winter solstice to the spring equinox makes the native black of colour and moderate of body, with straight, sparse hair, well put together, cold and moisture prevailing in him.
@n «Sparse» reads خفيفه for حفيفه.
@p AB03-F052r-L02..AB03-F052r-L18
In particular, the places whose figures resemble the figures of human beings, among the figures in the sphere of the signs and outside it, make bodies well put together and moderate. Those whose figure is other than this change bodies according to what suits their figures, and make the limbs resemble their own limbs with some degree of likeness, by inclining them towards largeness or smallness, towards strength or weakness, or towards good or bad composition — meaning good sense. Those that incline towards largeness are like Leo, Virgo and Sagittarius; those that incline towards smallness, like Pisces, Cancer and Capricorn. Again, of signs like Aries, Taurus and Leo, what lies next to their upper and fore parts inclines the limbs towards strength, so that the powers of the body are vigorous, and what lies next to their lower and hind parts inclines bodies towards weakness. The reverse holds for signs like Sagittarius, Aquarius and Scorpio: what lies next to their fore parts inclines bodies towards weakness, and what lies next to their hind parts towards robustness of body. Again, Virgo, Libra and Sagittarius incline bodies towards moderation, good composition and good sense; Scorpio, Pisces and Taurus incline them to departure from good composition and from moderation, and to folly. In the same way one should look at the matter of the other figures, compare all of it, and conjecture from it the properties that come together from the mixing of these things in the appearance of bodies and in their temperaments ⟦God the Exalted willing⟧.
@n «Sagittarius» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (الرامي) for الرأس, «the Head», here and twice below; Ptolemy has Gemini for Aquarius. «Upper parts» follows the Berlin manuscript (أعاليها); «inclines» reads يميل for يلي, and «conjecture» follows the Berlin manuscript (فتحدس) for فتحدث. The copyist repeated «either» and «Scorpio». The Arabic takes Ptolemy's good and bad proportion of the limbs as good sense and folly.
