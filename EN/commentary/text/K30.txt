⟨30⟩ On the native's fortune in favour
@n *Tetrabiblos* IV.3. «Favour» (ḥuẓwa) is Ptolemy's dignity: rank, honour and the power that comes with them.
@p AB03-F063v-L21..AB03-F064r-L14
The matters of favour, and of the good fortune that befits it, we should consider from the condition of the luminaries and of the planets that attend them, and with that we also consider their familiarities. For when both luminaries are in masculine signs, and both are on the cardines, or one of them, especially the one of the domain, and the wandering stars attend them, those attending the sun attributed to the mornings and those attending the moon to the evenings, the native is a king. If the attending planets are also on the cardines, and share a configuration with the cardine above the earth, the native is of great standing, powerful, a king over the world; and the good fortune increases when the attending planets are dexter — we think that «dexter» means northern in latitude — and share a configuration with the cardines above the earth. If the condition of the other planets is this, but the sun alone is in a masculine sign and the moon in a feminine sign, and one of them is on the cardine, the native is a ruler with power to kill and to let live.
@n «Attending» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (المحتفّة) for المختلفة, «differing», and «to let live» (يستحيي) for the doubtful {يسحن}.
@p AB03-F064r-L15..AB03-F064v-L05
If the planets attending them are not on the cardines, the native is merely of great standing, and the favour is to be thought of as like the favour of one who rules over a region, or of stewards, or of the commander of an army, not like the favour of the commanders who direct the supreme rule. If the luminaries are not on the cardines, and most of the planets attending them are on the cardines or share a configuration with the cardines, the native's favour is not noble and famous, but he is foremost in governing the cities and middling in managing his livelihood. If the planets attending the luminaries are not familiar with the cardines, the native is obscure in actions and unsuccessful; and when the luminaries are neither on the cardines nor in masculine signs, and are not attended, the native is in the utmost abasement and misfortune. In sum, the way followed in examining these matters is as I have described, as regards the increase and the decrease of favour.
@n «The commanders» follows the Berlin manuscript and the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (القوّاد) for القوّادين; both add the «not» (لم يكون) before «on the cardines». «The cities» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (المدن) for البدن, «the body», «obscure» its خاملًا for حافظًا, «keeping», and «favour» its الحظوة for الخطوط, «the lines».
@p AB03-F064v-L05..AB03-F064v-L18
As for the conditions between the increase and the decrease, which are very many, we should consider them from what is taken from that kind of particular variation that belongs to the luminaries and to the planets attending them, and from the planets that govern those attending them. For when the governing planets are of the domain, or are the beneficent ones, the favour is more sovereign and more lasting; when they are of the opposite domain, or are the maleficent ones, the favour comes with submission and passes away easily. The kind of favour that will come about should be considered from the particular condition of the planets attending the luminaries. When the one in charge of this matter is Saturn, the power of the favour lies in many possessions and the amassing of wealth; if it is Jupiter or Venus, its power lies in joy, gifts, honours and magnanimity; if Mars, it lies in the command of armies, in fear, and in mastery over those under him; and if Mercury, it comes from understanding, learning and care.
@n «Taken» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (تؤخذ) for توجد, «found»; «more sovereign» the Berlin manuscript and the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (أرأس) for رأس; «submission» reads خضوع for حضوع, and «amassing» جمع for جميع, «all».
