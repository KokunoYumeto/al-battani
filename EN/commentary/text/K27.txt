⟨27⟩ On the condition of the native's soul
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.13. The lists of qualities in this chapter follow Ptolemy's, mostly in the same order; the notes compare them where the Arabic is corrupt or departs from Ptolemy.
@p AB03-F055r-L11..AB03-F055r-L21
Of the conditions of the soul, those that belong to its rational, intellectual part are always known from the condition in which Mercury is seen; those that belong to its moral part, which is not rational, are known from the moon and from the planets that share a configuration with it, by applying to it and separating from it. Since the movements and conditions of the soul are of many kinds, it is right that our inquiry into them should proceed neither in an absolute and simple way nor at random, but through much examination and varied observations. For the differences of the signs in which Mercury and the moon are, and of the planets that dominate them, help much towards the properties of the soul; so too do the configurations, relative to the sun and to the cardines, of the planets that have a share in the matter we have mentioned; and the condition proper to the nature of each of the planets also helps in the movements of the soul.
@n «Intellectual» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (العقليّ) for الفعليّ, «active».
@p AB03-F055r-L21..AB03-F055v-L08
Of the signs in general, the tropical ones make souls fond of the affairs of crowds, of the common people and of cities, fond of Mars (?), attached to the things of God the Exalted, pure, of praiseworthy movement, inquisitive, doing good to others, good at conjecture and at comparison, and versed in judgement from the stars and in divination. The bicorporeal signs make souls versatile, easily changed, hard to grasp, light, easily shifting, of two conditions, amorous, of many kinds, fond of music, sluggish, clever and given to regret. The fixed signs make souls just, not deceived, steady, pure, grave, understanding, patient, fond of toil, strong, self-controlled, rancorous, hard in dispute, fond of honour, factious, rebellious and unyielding.
@n For «fond of Mars» Ptolemy has glory-seeking. «Inquisitive» reads بحّاثة for بحّامة; «rancorous» reads حقودة for حقوقة, and «factious» مشغبة for مشعبة. For «unyielding» (غير مسعدة, «not obliging») the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «not shifting» (متنقّلة).
@p AB03-F055v-L08..AB03-F056r-L02
As for the conditions and configurations of the planets: those that are eastern and rising, especially those that are in their own face, make the soul liberal, simple, self-willed — one also says «enterprising» — strong, keen, sharp, and concealing nothing (?). The stations of the planets attributed to the mornings, and their culminations, make the soul reflective, steady, of good memory, grave, understanding, of great aspiration, unchanging, unbending, not deceived, discerning, practical, given to examination and of sound knowledge. The rising of the planets at the beginning of the night, and their setting, make the soul light, flighty, weak, unable to bear toil, impressionable, wretched, cowardly, doubting, shifting between boldness and cowardice, dull, cunning and slow to move. The stations of the planets attributed to the evenings, their culmination in the part of the sky below the earth, and the evening setting of Venus and Mercury when the time is by day and their morning setting when it is by night, make the soul noble and wise, not of good memory, neither painstaking nor fond of toil, but searching into hidden matters and seeking concealed things, such as magic, the mysteries, the higher sciences, the science of instruments and devices, the science of wonders, judgement by the stars, augury from birds, the interpretation of dreams, and the like.
@n «Enterprising» follows the Berlin manuscript (ويقال وقّاعة) for وتقال واقعة; «concealing nothing» reads مورّية for مؤذيّة, «harming»; Ptolemy has open. «Practical» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (عمل) for عقل, «reason»; «wise» reads عاقلة for غافلة, «heedless». After «dreams» the Berlin manuscript adds «and philosophical» (وتكون متفلسفة).
@p AB03-F056r-L02..AB03-F056r-L21
Besides, when the planets in charge of governing the matters of the soul are in their own places and in the domains proper and suited to them, as we have explained before, they make the properties of the soul evident, with no obstacle opposing them to keep them from completion, each standing by itself and effective (?); especially when the very same planets dominate the two places together, I mean when they share a configuration with the place of Mercury, in whatever way that may be, or are separating from the moon or applying to it. If the matter with them is not as we have said, or they are in places not proper to them, their proper natures make the actions of the soul not evident, hidden, incomplete and unsuccessful. The natures of the planets that dominate them or overpower them make the actions of the soul strong and harmful to those under their hands. So with the unjust and the wicked, in whom this comes from the familiarity of the maleficent planets: when these planets dominate, they make their impulse to harm others an easy impulse, with no obstacle and no hardship in it, and notorious; and when planets of the domain opposite to theirs dominate these planets, they make them obscure, unsuccessful and exposed to punishments. And so with the good and the just, to whom this comes from the familiarity of the beneficent planets: when these are not overpowered, they rejoice in doing good to others and meet with it without harm reaching them from it; rather their justice becomes a cause of benefit to them. When planets opposed to these dominate them, the condition is the reverse of what we have mentioned: because of their calm, their gentleness, their love for people and their compassion for them, they are among those whom many people make light of and blame, or wrong. This, then, is the general way of examining the moral part.
@n «Governing» and «as we have explained» are written in the margin. «Effective» guesses at the doubtful {بحجة}; Ptolemy has effective. «Impulse» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (حركتهم) for تركتهم, and «rejoice» (يفرحون) for يعرجون.
@p AB03-F056r-L21..AB03-F056v-L02
The particular properties and conditions that arise from the nature of the planets according to their rule we shall mention in summary, until we come to the mixture that appears in the general case.
@p AB03-F056v-L02..AB03-F056v-L09
If Saturn alone governs the matter of the soul, and is the lord of Mercury and the moon, then, if its condition in the world and relative to the cardines is praiseworthy, it makes the native fond of the body, strong in opinion, deep, self-willed, toiling, domineering, aggressive, wealthy, fond of property, violent, a keeper of treasures and envious. If it is the opposite, not of praiseworthy condition, it makes the native dirty, contemptible, mean-spirited, undiscriminating, self-willed, envious, cowardly, withdrawn from people, foul of speech, fond of the desert and of lamentation, shameless, unlucky, fond of drudgery, without affection, treacherous to his friends, joyless and hating the body.
@n «The body» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (للأبدان) for للأديان, «religions», both times; «property» reads للأملاك for للملوك, «kings».
@p AB03-F056v-L09..AB03-F056v-L15
If it is allied with Jupiter, then in the praiseworthy condition it makes the native good, honouring the elders, calm, quiet, discerning, fond of possessions, magnanimous, provided for, of good choice (?), loving his friends, upright, forbearing and philosophical. If its condition is the opposite, it makes the native unused to good, out of his mind, busy with the jinn, haunting the temples, telling of the unseen, hating children, friendless, keeping to a hiding place (?), not mixing with people, untrustworthy, ignorant, bad, dark (?), with a false smile, weak, not caring for honour, given to regret, harsh, hard to meet and of bad choice.
@n «A hiding place» reads سرب for شراب, «drink»; Ptolemy has hiding within doors. For «dark» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «green» (أخضر); Ptolemy has venomous.
@p AB03-F056v-L16..AB03-F057r-L06
If it is allied with Mars, in the praiseworthy condition, it makes the native undiscriminating, toiling, abasing (?), shifting between boldness (?) and cowardice, hard in dealings, pitiless, contemptuous, rough, confrontational, rash, fond of disorder, treacherous, insidious, of bad rancour, unmoved by what is put to him, lavish to the people, unstable, rebellious, hating the leaders, fond of dispute and of victory, solitary, of bad stock, hard to bear, hard in dealings, heavy, coarse, crude, boastful (?), doing harm to people, unjust, formidable, hating people, neither changing nor moving, meddling in many things, but easily brought back, a man of work and effort, and in general successful. If it is in the opposite condition, it makes the native a robber, a highwayman, despised, of bad condition, of foul gain, not fearing God, without affection, abusive, insidious, treacherous, a killer, foul in sex, violent, a killer of men, a sorcerer, a robber of temples, dissolute and a robber of graves, and in general bad in all conditions.
@n «Makes» reads صيّر for صغير, «small». «Shifting» is written in the margin; «boldness» guesses at الخيرة, after the same phrase above (الجرأة). «Formidable» reads مهيبًا for مهينًا, «contemptible»; Ptolemy has not to be despised.
@p AB03-F057r-L06..AB03-F057r-L21
If Saturn is allied with Venus, then in the praiseworthy condition it makes the native a hater of women, fond of elders and of hackney horses (?), unpleasant to meet, not fond of finery (?), fond of its secrets, a soothsayer, devout, fond of the things of God, mighty and glorious, and holding fast to them, chaste, reserved, a walker (?), fond of wisdom, trustworthy, faithful, self-controlled, quick to take offence and jealous over women. If it is in the opposite of the praiseworthy condition, it makes the native greedy in intercourse, ill-omened, committing foul acts, undiscriminating and impure in intercourse, impious, a deceiver of women, especially of his kinswomen, feeble, blamed in all conditions, dissolute in intercourse, hating the beautiful, toiling, foul of speech, drunken, given to hidden affairs, despised, wanton in the matter of intercourse, committing foul acts and doing in them what departs from the natural course, fond of doing that with those advanced in age and with the base among people, against the law, and with animals; not fearing God, mighty and glorious, making light of sacred things and of the temples, mocking, a sorcerer, and meddling in everything.
@n «Praiseworthy» is written above the line. Ptolemy has lovers of antiquity, solitary, where the Arabic has «fond of elders and of hackney horses»; «finery» guesses at the corrupt الذين. «Devout» reads متألّهًا for متًّا لها. «Drunken» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (سكيّرًا) for متكبّرًا, «proud», and «the base» (بالأنذال) for بالاترال; «hidden» is written above the line.
@p AB03-F057r-L21..AB03-F057v-L06
If Saturn is allied with the planet Mercury, then in the praiseworthy condition it makes the native a searcher, an inquirer after news, a seeker of laws, fond of medicine, a keeper of secrets, looking into hidden and concealed matters, a worker of wonders, a deceiver, living for the day, nimble, able to manage, clever, exacting, wakeful, fond of understanding and of work, and successful. If Saturn is in the opposite of the praiseworthy condition, it makes the native rancorous, troubled of soul, gloomy, a dealer in charms and deceit, treacherous and unsuccessful.
@n «A searcher» reads مفتّشًا for مبتّشًا.
@p AB03-F057v-L06..AB03-F057v-L13
As for Jupiter: if it takes the governing of the matter of the soul alone, then in the praiseworthy condition it makes the conditions of the soul like the conditions we have mentioned, except that they are weaker and more subdued, without discernment; for example, instead of magnanimous the native is prodigal; instead of pure, of bad doctrine; instead of generous, cowardly; instead of chaste, conceited; instead of loving people, simple-natured; instead of fine in conduct, fond of pleasure; instead of high-minded, vain; instead of good, undiscriminating; and the like.
@n Something is lost here. Ptolemy lists Jupiter's qualities in honourable positions and then says that in the opposite positions they look similar but are humbler and less judicious; the Arabic passes from «in the praiseworthy condition» straight to the weaker likeness. «Undiscriminating» follows the Berlin manuscript (غير مميّز) for عن مميّز.
@p AB03-F057v-L13..AB03-F058r-L03
If Jupiter is allied with Mars, then in the praiseworthy condition it makes the native rough, quarrelsome, warlike, a manager, restless, unsubmissive, ardent, a commander of armies, bold, practical, damaging, vengeful, fond of victory, domineering, plunging into affairs, gentle, courageous, generous, victorious, magnanimous, fond of honour, passionate, discerning, and one who commands and forbids. If Jupiter is the opposite of what we have mentioned, the native is abusive, muddled, coarse, implacable (?), seditious, contentious, headstrong, a slanderer (?), conceited, rebellious, a beggar, quick to change, light, given to regret, holding to nothing, foolish, without discernment or judgement, out of his mind, boastful, a fault-finder and prodigal, and in general uneven in character and unsettled in conditions.
@n «Bold» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (دالّة) for ذااله. For «courageous» (رجلًا) the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has نازلًا. «Implacable» reads قليل الإغضاء for قليل الأعضاء, «of few limbs», and «a slanderer» ساعيًا for سعًا; for «a beggar» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «angry» (غاضبًا).
@p AB03-F058r-L03..AB03-F058r-L17
If Jupiter is allied with Venus, then in the praiseworthy condition it makes the native pious, living in comfort, fond of cleanliness, fond of playing with children and of looking at them, fond of singing and amusement, fond of food, of good character, beneficent, compassionate, sound of heart, loving God, devout, fond of striving, intelligent, affectionate, chaste, pure of soul, grateful, provided for, fond of reading books, discerning, moderate and decent in intercourse, fond of kin, of good religion, fond of justice, fond of honour and renown, and in general good and excellent. If Jupiter is the opposite of what we have mentioned, the native is luxurious, soft-living, effeminate in soul, fond of dancing, with an anger like the anger of women, a spendthrift, infatuated with women, amorous, much given to intercourse, addicted to it, wanton, fond of adorning the hair, ugly in conduct, rash, prodigal, fond of finding fault with people, quick of speech, effeminate, with a mind like a woman's, dominated by the affairs of the temples, forward in affairs and a keeper of secrets; but trustworthy, with no evil in him, cheerful, approachable, of praiseworthy ways, and very liberal in the conditions they pass through.
@n «Playing with children» follows the Berlin manuscript (للمناغات) for للمنازعات, «disputes»; the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «the arts» (للصناعات). Ptolemy has lovers of children and of spectacles. «Dancing» reads الرقص for الرفض, here and twice below. For «amorous» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «beloved» (معشوقًا), and for «quick of speech, effeminate» «quick to pain, adorned, effeminate» (الألم متزيّنًا). «Very liberal in the conditions» follows the Berlin manuscript and the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (جدًّا، الأحوال) for «liberal in the words».
@p AB03-F058r-L17..AB03-F059r-L10
If Jupiter is allied with Mercury, then in the praiseworthy condition it makes the native much given to reading books, fond of reasoning, a geometer, versed in the mathematical sciences, a poet, an orator, intelligent, chaste, of praiseworthy opinion, good in counsel ⟨…⟩ beneficent, managing, of good character, a giver of gifts, fond of company, good at conjecture, quick to succeed, a statesman, of good religion, loving God, good in possessions (?), affectionate, fond of kin, well brought up, fond of wisdom, of renown and standing. If Jupiter is the opposite of what we have mentioned, the native is clumsy, a babbler, making many mistakes, contemptible, subject to *al-ashar shams* (?), which is something that comes in the moon and brings the one who has it to loss of reason, and then to telling what will be; holding fast to the things of God, mighty and glorious, concerned with the things of the soul, thinking himself wise while devoid of reason, conceited, wily, a conjurer, unsettled in movement, much given to talk, a teacher, and full of desire.
@n The copyist wrote «much given to reading books, fond of reasoning, a geometer» twice and struck the repetition out, and repeated «opinion». @p AB03-F059r-L18..AB03-F059v-L11

@p AB03-F060v-L15..AB03-F061r-L13
This being so, one should know that the condition of the moon also helps somewhat towards the things we have mentioned. When it is in the places of bending at the southern and northern limits, it helps towards changeable character in the soul, many devices and quick shifting; when it is on the belt of the signs, it helps towards sharpness of character, effectiveness in action and quickness of movement. Again, when the moon is rising, that is, when it comes out of the rays, or when it is increasing in light, it helps the soul to be more richly endowed, more renowned, clearer and firmer; when its light is waning, or it is hidden under the rays, it helps towards the soul's dullness and weariness, and the slackness, lagging and obscurity that befall it. The sun too helps somewhat towards what we have said, when it is familiar (?) with the planet in charge of governing the matter of the soul: when it is allied with it and its condition is praiseworthy, the characters of the soul are more just, farther from injustice, more noble, more dignified and of better religion; if the matter is the opposite, the characters of the soul are baser, more troubled, harsher, more self-willed, more fearful and weaker in conduct, and in general hard to set right. In this way the matter of the soul is distinguished, according to the mixture of the governing planets and of those that look at them.
@n «The moon» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (القمر) for النفس, «the soul»; on the belt of the signs the moon is at a node. «Familiar» guesses at the incomplete كلت. «Its condition», «more dignified» and «to set right» follow the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (حاله، أجلّ، الصلاح) for «the condition», واحد and العلاج, «treatment»; «baser» reads أخسّ for أحسن, «better».
