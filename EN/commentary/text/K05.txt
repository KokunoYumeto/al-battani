⟨5⟩ On the fixed, the solstitial and the bicorporeal signs
@n *Tetrabiblos* I.11.
@p AB03-F021v-L08..AB03-F021v-L14
Of the signs, two are solstitial and two are equinoctial. The solstitial ones are Cancer and Capricorn, and the equinoctial ones Aries and Libra. These signs are given these names because when the sun comes to the point of the summer solstice it brings about summer; when it comes to the point of the winter solstice, winter; when it comes to the point of the spring equinox, spring; and when it comes to the point of the autumn equinox, autumn; and at these two points night and day are equal over the whole earth.
@p AB03-F021v-L14..AB03-F021v-L19
Of the eight remaining signs, which lie between the points of the equinoxes and of the solstices, four are fixed. They are called fixed because the season stays fixed in them in one state; they are the ones that follow the two signs of the equinoxes and the two signs of the solstices, and they are called Taurus, Scorpio, Leo and Aquarius. The bicorporeal signs are those that follow the fixed signs. They are given this name because they lie between the fixed signs and the signs of the equinox and the solstice, and share, as it were, with their beginnings in the fixed signs and with their ends in the changing signs; they are Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius and Pisces.
