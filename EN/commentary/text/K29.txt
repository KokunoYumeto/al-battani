⟨29⟩ On the native's fortune in wealth
@n *Tetrabiblos* IV.2. The commentary passes over Ptolemy's introduction to Book IV (IV.1). For «wealth» (المال) the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «possessions» (الملك) throughout the chapter.
@p AB03-F063r-L17..AB03-F063v-L11
As for wealth, and how the matters of acquisition will be, one should take [the indication] for it from the lot of fortune alone, which we always find by looking at what is between the sun and the moon and casting it from the ascendant, as we have explained in the chapter on the length of life, by night and by day alike, for the reasons mentioned before. The way of considering it is to take the planets in charge of governing the sign in which this lot is, and to see how their strength and their familiarity are, in the manner we defined before; and we also look at the planets that share a configuration with them and that overpower them, whether of that same domain or of the opposite domain. For when the governors of the lot are strong, the native has much wealth, especially if the luminaries bear them suitable witness. Saturn signifies that the native's wealth comes through building, through agriculture or through seafaring; Jupiter, through trusts, through stewardship, or through the offices of the imams (?) and of justice; Mars, through command and rule; Venus, through gifts of friends or through women; and Mercury signifies that the wealth comes from learning or from trade.
@n «Lot» reads السهم for القسم, «part». «Familiarity» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (ملائمتها) for ملازمتهما, and «defined» (حدّدنا) for حررنا; «agriculture» its الفلاحة for العلامة, «sign». «The imams» reads الأئمّة for إلّا يمنة; Ptolemy has priesthoods. Ptolemy has eloquence where the Arabic has learning.
@p AB03-F063v-L11..AB03-F063v-L21
If Saturn in particular is familiar with the lot of fortune, and Jupiter shares a configuration with it, it signifies inheritances that the native receives, especially when that is on the upper cardines and Jupiter is in a bicorporeal sign or also applies to the moon; for then it signifies that the native is adopted and inherits the inheritances of strangers. If the planets of the domain of the governing planets witness to them, what the native owns remains and is kept safe. If the planets of the opposite domain prevail over the chief places, following them and moving towards them, they signify the loss of the wealth; and the general time when that happens is known from the passage of the planets that are the cause of the action to the cardines and to the places that rise [to them].
@n «Adopted» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (يتبنّى) for سى. For «passage» (مجاز) the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «coming opposite» (محاذاة); Ptolemy has the approach of the planets to the angles and the succedent places.
