⟨21⟩ On the birth of twins
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.7.
@p AB03-F041r-L14..AB03-F041v-L05
Likewise, in the birth of twins or of more than twins, we should examine the matter of these three places, I mean the luminaries and the ascendant. It is in the nature of this matter to occur at the minglings when two of these places, or all three of them, are bicorporeal signs, especially when this same condition also befalls the planets that govern them; or when some of them are in bicorporeal signs, or some are [disposed] two by two or more than two in the governing. If the chief places are bicorporeal signs and share in a configuration, and with that many of the planets [are so too], those born will be more than two, and the planet that has this property increases their number. The kind of the planets that share in a configuration with the sun, the moon and the ascendant, which increases the generation of males or females, as mentioned before, adds to the knowledge of which of them are male and which female. If this condition does not include the sign of the ascendant together with the luminaries, but the sign of the middle of the sky is found in this condition instead, what the mothers bear is for the most part twins or more than that.
@n «Minglings» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (شيئين is its reading of the next word); Ptolemy speaks of the intermixture at conception.
@p AB03-F041v-L05..AB03-F041v-L18
In particular, for the birth of three males, complete and well formed, the matter is like that of the birth of the Anaktores, who are the kings: Saturn, Jupiter and Mars in the places we have mentioned, in bicorporeal signs. For the birth of three females, the matter is like that in the birth of the Charites: Venus and the moon with Mercury, made feminine, in the places we have mentioned. For the birth of two males and a female, the matter is like that in the birth of those called the Dioscuri: Saturn, Jupiter and Venus in these places. For [the birth of] two females and one male, the matter is like that in the birth of the one called Demeter and Kore: Venus, the moon and Mars in these places. In these conditions it mostly happens that the child is not complete, that its birth is with some injuries that befall the body, and that things arise in some of its limbs without visible cause, of an unexpected kind; or some of these accidents are on it in the manner of something that appears [as a portent].
@n The Greek names, corrupt in the transcription (نطروس، الحارطين، دياسفودا، لمنظر وفودس), are read from the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (أناقطرس، الحارطس، ديوسقور، ديميطر وقوريس): Ptolemy's Kings, Graces, Dioscuri, and Demeter and Kore.
