⟨10⟩ On the familiarity of the triplicities with the countries and the planets
@n *Tetrabiblos* II.3. In the lists of this chapter, names that the Arabic forms allow to be identified are given in English, and «(?)» marks a doubtful identification; names too corrupt to identify are given in Arabic letters as the transcription has them.
@p AB03-F023r-L20..AB03-F023v-L18
Ptolemy divided the whole inhabited earth that lies to the north of the equator by two lines, which cut it in length and in breadth into quarters, and made each quarter akin to the triplicity, among the triplicities of those four [sets of] signs, to which it is attributed. Ptolemy made the parts of each quarter that lie next to the middle of the whole inhabited earth receive familiarity from the triplicity that governs the angle opposite to that angle, and made Mercury a partner with the governing planets, because it is of a common domain; and made the familiarity of the countries to be with the planets that are the lords of the houses of those triplicities, together with the signs of the triplicities too. Each of these quarters Ptolemy divided into three parts, by which the countries in it are marked off, so that each of these three parts became akin to one sign of the triplicity, together with the planet that is the lord of that sign. Ptolemy explained the natures of the nations that live in each of these particular countries, their characters and their conditions, and treated each of the quarters as a whole according to what belongs to it, the triplicities akin to it and the planets that govern those triplicities, and according to the natures of the signs and of the planets that are their lords, as was needed, and summed it up in what we can dispense with mentioning here; then described the familiarity of each of the countries in each quarter, and of what in it lies next to the middle of the inhabited earth, and classified it separately by the signs and the planets that are the lords of those signs, so that it might be easy to consult, following what had been said before. We have confined ourselves to that and set it out in this way: we began with the countries akin to each sign and its lord, attributed to that third, and followed it with the countries that lie next to the middle of the inhabited world, and we set out in detail what makes each of them known. Then we recorded the familiarity of the cities with the signs and the planets, according to what is said to have been rising at the time of their building, or the positions of the sun and the moon, or the middle of the sky of the kings who lived at the time when they were built.
@n «Inhabited» reads المعمورة for المحمودة, «praised», and «the cities» المدن for الدرج, «the degrees».

So of the countries and districts, those akin to Aries are the lands of Britain, Bastarnia (?), Germania and Galatia, the land of the Jews and Deep Syria; by sharing with Aries these countries belong to Mars. Those akin to Taurus are the lands of Parthia, Media, Persia, the Cyclades, the coasts of Asia Minor and Cyprus; by their familiarity with Taurus they belong to Venus. Those akin to Gemini are the lands of Jurjān, Ṭabaristān, Armenia, al-Qayrawān, Marmarica, which is below the land of Egypt, and «فورينا»; by their familiarity with Gemini they belong to Mercury. Those akin to Cancer are Numidia, Carthage (?), Ifrīqiya, «سوسا» and Phrygia; by their familiarity with Cancer they belong to the moon. Those akin to Leo are Italy, «وعاليلواىولىا», Sicily, Phoenicia (?) and Chaldaea (?); by their familiarity with Leo they belong to the sun. Those akin to Virgo are Babylon, the Jazīra, the land of Āthūr, «الأمن», Achaia (?) and Crete; and they are akin to Mercury too. Those akin to Libra are «ينظر بالي», Casperia (?), Serica, Thebais (?), the Oasis (?) and Troglodytica (?); and they are akin to Venus too, with Libra. Those akin to Scorpio are Metagonitis (?), Mauritania (?), Gaetulia (?), Syria, Commagene (?), «فافا حرٯما» and the Ḥijāz; and they are akin to Mars too. Those akin to Sagittarius are Tyrrhenia (?), Celtica (?), Spain (?) and also the cultivated land of the Arabs; their familiarity is with Jupiter. Those akin to Capricorn are the land of India, Ariana, «ادىا», China, Gedrosia (?), Thrace (?), Macedonia and Illyria (?); and they are akin to Saturn. Those akin to Aquarius are Sarmatica (?), «طلساي», Sogdiana (?), «ارانياىا» and the Middle «ىىوىا», meaning the middle land of the Abyssinians; and they are akin to Saturn too. Those akin to Pisces are Phazania (?), «ٯعلاعرىىا», Nasamonitis (?), Garamantica (?), Lydia (?), Cilicia (?) and Pamphylia (?); and they are akin to Jupiter, with Pisces. All of these are «ما يميّزه» and ninety regions, of those in the four [quarters] mentioned and of those next to the middle of the inhabited world.

One must add to this as well that each of the fixed stars is akin to the countries to which the parts of those signs are akin, and they all act on them: these are the parts that the stars face and that correspond to them on one of the circles that pass through the poles of those signs; this is evident from the latitudes of the stars when their passage is above the zenith. This is the account of what we have described concerning the cities.

In this copy of the cities we found the familiarity of the districts with the signs in the form that I shall set down. You see that this copy is one whose interpretation was taken care of, and that it seems to differ from Ptolemy in some districts and to lean in part to the opinion of the Egyptians in the division of the countries. Of the districts, those akin to Aries are Fārs, Ādharbayjān, Britain, «طعنه», Germania, Palestine and part of al-Balqāʾ; and they are akin, with Aries, to Mars. Those akin to Taurus are all the land of Māh, Iṣfahān, the small islands of the Sea of the Romans, Cyprus and «أثار»; they are akin, with Taurus, to Venus. Akin to Gemini are «حلان», al-Daylam, Jurjān, Ṭabaristān, Greater Armenia, «مرم», Pentapolis, Marāqiya and Egypt; and with Gemini, Mercury. Akin to Cancer are the land of the Berbers, Ifrīqiya, «وينبه إلى» the land of the Romans, Phrygia (?), «لاوقة» and «لاودية»; akin with Cancer is the moon. Akin to Leo are the land of the Turks, «سمر», Italy (?), Cilicia (?), «انقبيه», the land of Ḥimṣ, Damascus and the Sawād of Kufa; and with Leo, the sun. Akin to Virgo are the land of Babylon, «مواقيّة», the land of Mosul, the Jazīra, the land of «الرياس» and Crete; and with Virgo, Mercury. Akin to Libra are the land of Bukhārā⟨n⟩, «حارستان», Ḥimyar, Tibet, the island of Sardinia (?), a part of the land of the Abyssinians that is called «طراموية», Ṣanʿāʾ, the land of Najrān and the land of Kirmān; and with Libra, Venus.
