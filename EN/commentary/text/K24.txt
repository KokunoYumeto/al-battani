⟨24⟩ On the length of life
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.10. The *haylāj* is Ptolemy's prorogator, the significator of life (see the glossary of the *Zīj*); the *kadkhudāh*, below, is the planet that gives the years.
@p AB03-F045r-L13..AB03-F045v-L04
Of the accounts of the accidents that come after birth, the first is the account of the length of life. The consideration of it is not absolute and unrestricted, but is taken and known from the dominion and prevalence of the chief places, and it is something in which many ways are used. The way that agrees with our opinion and follows the natural course is the one that I shall mention, and I say about it: the consideration is from the places of the haylāj, from the places that have dominion over the places of the haylāj, and from the killing places or planets too; and each of these matters is distinguished as I shall describe to you. The first thing we should know is that the places of the haylāj are those in which the planet to which the authority of the haylāj falls must necessarily be. They are the sign of the ascendant, from the 5 degrees that precede it and rise from the horizon before it to the remaining 25 degrees that rise after it and ascend; the [thirty degrees] to the right of these thirty degrees, in sextile to them, which belong to the house of good fortune; those in quadrature to them, which belong to the middle of the sky; those in trine to them, which belong to the place called Theos (?), which is the house of religion (?) and the fortunate; and those opposite them, which belong to the setting point.
@n «Killing» reads القاتلة for المقابلة, «opposite»: Ptolemy has the destructive places or stars. Ptolemy calls the eleventh place the House of the Good Daemon and the ninth the House of the God (θεός); for the transcription's باوس the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has ثاوس, and for بيت الدين والمسعد the Berlin manuscript has بيت الذين والسعر.
@p AB03-F045v-L04..AB03-F045v-L13
Of these, what should be put first for its strength in dominion is what is in the middle of the sky; then what is in the ascendant; then what is in the house of good fortune, which is the one that rises to the middle of the sky; then what is in the setting point; then what precedes the middle of the sky, which is the ninth, of which we said that it is the house [of religion]. All that is below the earth we should leave unused in so noble a matter, except the 25 degrees that are always rising and appearing above the earth. Of what is above the earth one should [not] use the sign that has no connection with the ascendant, nor the sign that has risen before the ascendant, which is called the house of misery: for besides its declining from the cardine, the power that goes out from it to the earth is troubled by the thick, dark vapour that rises to it from the moistures of the earth before the rising of the sun; and because of that the colours of the stars in this house, and the sizes of their bodies, are seen otherwise than they are by nature.
@n «The middle of the sky» is written in the margin. Ptolemy calls the twelfth place the House of the Evil Daemon.
@p AB03-F045v-L13..AB03-F046r-L04
Then we should take as the haylājs the four chief things, I mean the sun, the moon, the ascendant and the lot of fortune, which is the lot of good fortune, and the governors of the places of these things. The lot of fortune is found by always taking the number [of degrees] from the place of the sun to the place of the moon, both by day and by night, and beginning from the ascendant and counting from it the same number, proceeding in the direction of the following signs: so we know the place of this lot. We do that so that the relation of the sun to the ascendant may be like the relation of the moon to the lot of fortune, and the lot becomes as it were the ascendant of the moon. Likely it was to this very meaning that he went who said that in nativities that occur at night one should count from the moon to the sun [what one counts] by day, and then begin from the ascendant and count from it the same number in the direction opposite to the one we counted by day, I mean towards the preceding signs. For when we do that, the place of the lot of fortune comes out for us in this way as the very place that we had, and the condition in the sharing of the configuration becomes the same condition.
@p AB03-F046r-L04..AB03-F046v-L01
Of these things one should choose, by day, first the sun, if it is in the places of the haylāj; if it is not so, the moon is chosen; otherwise the planet that has the most shares in the governing of the place of the sun, of the place of the preceding conjunction and of the ascendant — I mean when it has, of the five shares on which the governing rests, three shares in one of the places we have mentioned, or more than three, or has them in all of them; and if that cannot be, we take the degree of the ascendant. By night one should choose first the moon; then the sun, if it is in the signs that rise and ascend above the earth from the house of the ascendant; then the planet that has the most shares in the place of the moon, the place of the preceding opposition and the place of the lot of fortune. If that cannot be, we take in the last resort the ascendant, if what preceded the birth was a conjunction, and the lot of fortune, if what preceded was an opposition. If both the luminaries, and the governor according to the suitable domain, are in some of the places of the haylāj, we should take whichever of the luminaries is in the place of greater authority and strength, which is clear from the order we have set out for the places of the haylāj. The governor should be chosen over the luminaries only when it is in a place of greater authority and strength among the places [of the haylāj], in that order, and has shares in the governing according to both domains. By this procedure the haylāj that signifies the length of life is known.
@n «In the last resort» is written between the lines over سائر; «of greater authority» and «the luminaries» follow the Berlin manuscript (أكثر، النيّر).
@p AB03-F046v-L01..AB03-F046v-L17
Others than Ptolemy among the ancients agreed that none of these that we have mentioned deserves to be haylāj unless the lords of the shares in its place witness to it. The witnessing is that they share in a configuration with it by one of the sharings, whichever it may be: quadrature, opposition, trine and sextile; or looking, or equality in power; or of the commanding and obeying signs, which are called the high and the low. The one of them that has this witnessing is the haylāj, and the one among the lords of the shares in its place that shares [a configuration] with it is the giver (?) of the years. We have seen that Ptolemy mentions in these places the lords of the shares, which are those in charge of the governing of these places, but does not afterwards call them to witness for the haylāj. It may be that he hinted at it, but that it did not come out in the translation, or the like. Those who held this view put the degree of the conjunction or of the full moon that precedes the birth, in the haylāj-ship, in the place of the planet that has the shares in those places, and made no distinction between above the earth and below it in the places of the haylāj. We shall explain their opinion when we have finished with Ptolemy's opinion in this chapter.
@n «Giver» guesses at the transcription's العين. Al-Battānī reckons with the Arabic translation of the *Tetrabiblos* that he reads, and allows that it may have lost something of Ptolemy's meaning.
@p AB03-F046v-L17..AB03-F047v-L05
Ptolemy said: Once we know the haylāj, we should use the two kinds of direction, the kind that goes in the order of the signs, and the one that goes both in the order and against the order. The order of the signs alone should be used in the condition called the casting of rays, that is, when the haylāj is in the eastern places, I mean those from the middle of the sky to the ascendant. The one used both in the order of the signs and towards the preceding signs together is in the condition called the «hourly», which is when the haylāj is in the ninth place, called Theos, declining from the middle of the sky. This being so, the killing [degree], according to the haylāj [directed] by the kind towards the preceding signs, is only the degree of the setting point, because it hides the lord of life. The degrees of the planets that meet the haylāj or witness to it only decrease and increase the numbers that accumulate up to the time of the setting of the haylāj, without killing; for it is not they that come to the place of the haylāj, but it comes to their places. Those that increase are the beneficent planets and those that decrease the maleficent. Mercury goes with those of these planets that share in a configuration with it: if they increase, it increases, and if they decrease, it decreases. The number of the increase and the decrease is known from the position of the degree of each of them, or of its ray: for the number of the full years is according to the times of the hours of each degree of them, by the times of the day hours if the time is diurnal and by the times of the night hours if it is at night. This must be understood as said of them in the ascendant; [the number] then decreases according to its distance from the ascendant, until it reaches the setting point and nothing of it remains. This is found by taking the distance of the degree from the setting point, as we have explained in the first chapter of this book on the hours of the distance of the degrees from the cardines: for each seasonal hour of it take a twelfth of the times of the hours of that degree, nocturnal or diurnal according to the time, and what comes out is the amount of the increase or the decrease.
@n «Direction» follows the Berlin manuscript (التسير) for {التكيسير}; Ptolemy's «hourly» is the *horimaea*. Al-Battānī reckons the distance of a degree from the cardines in seasonal hours in chapter 55 of his *Zīj*.
@p AB03-F047v-L05..AB03-F048r-L18
According to the kind that goes towards the following side from the place of the haylāj, what kills is the places of the maleficent planets, I mean the place of Saturn and of Mars: either when they meet the haylāj with their bodies, or when they cast their rays from either of these two places, I mean the place of quadrature and the place of opposition; and sometimes this is from the sextiles that hear and obey, or that look and are equal in power. The very quadrature of the place of the haylāj that is on the following side of the signs kills too; and sometimes the sextile does so, when it is afflicted and in signs of long ascensions, and the trine does it when it is afflicted and in signs of short ascensions. When the moon is the haylāj, the place of the sun may kill too. One should not suppose that these places necessarily kill at every time; they do so only when they are afflicted. For it is prevented too if the term belongs to one of the beneficent planets, or if one of the beneficent planets casts its ray, from quadrature, trine or opposition, either upon the killing degree itself or upon the degrees that follow it, provided that it does not exceed 12 degrees in the casting of the ray of Jupiter and 8 degrees in the casting of the ray of Venus; and likewise when the haylāj and the planet that meets it meet bodily, and the latitude of both is not one and the same. When the planets that release and help, or those that kill, are two planets or more on each of these sides, one should look at which of the two kinds prevails, by the number of those that help it and by their strength: by number, when one of the two kinds is more numerous than the other by something perceptible; by strength, when the planets that help or those that kill are in the places suited to them and the others are not in such places, and especially when the planets of one kind are eastern and those of the other kind western. In general, none of them that is under the rays should be used, either for killing or for helping and releasing; except that, when the moon is the haylāj, the place of the sun itself is the killer, and it is used with the maleficent planet that is with it, when it corrupts and none of the beneficent planets releases it; and when a beneficent planet is with the sun, in the place of that maleficent one, as we said, it releases it.
@n «Killing degree» reads القاتلة for المقابلة; «not one and the same» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (لم يكن) for وليكن; «kill» reads تقتل for يقبل.
@p AB03-F048r-L18..AB03-F048v-L16
The number of the years that come from the distances between the place of the haylāj and the killing place should not be taken absolutely, nor however it happens, as many people took it, who claimed that it always comes from the times of the ascensions of the degrees of that distance in the clime; and we should not use that at any time, unless the ascendant itself is the place of the haylāj, or one of the places that come to the ascendant, when the degree of the ascendant is the haylāj. When it is in the middle of the sky, I mean the degree of the middle of the sky, we use for the amounts of the distances the ascensions of the right sphere. When the place of the haylāj inclines from the cardines to one of the sides, we use what we described in our first book, where we mentioned the ascensions of the signs between the quarters of the sphere, so as to know after how many times of the equator the following killing place comes to the place of the haylāj, which is the preceding place; and when it reaches the setting point, the amounts of the distances are according to the settings of the degree of the setting point, when the haylāj is in it. That is something we have explained, and we can do without mentioning it here, since we finished with it in the book, because it is one of the geometrical things whose working had to be explained there.
@n «Our first book» is al-Battānī's *Zīj*, chapter 55, «On knowing the ascensions of the signs between the cardines, in the quarters of the sphere». The words «from the times of the ascensions» stand twice.
@p AB03-F048v-L16..AB03-F049r-L18
It remains for us to distinguish each of the kinds of things that meet the haylāj and its setting: of the things we have mentioned before, those that are shortest in time, those that kill, those that are called climacterics, which are the [critical] times, and the other things that meet it. Distinguishing this comes from knowing the bad, harmful meeting and the good, beneficial, helping one, which occur ⟨in⟩ the way we mentioned just now; and from the ingress of the coming years, which are the times of the revolutions afterwards that the place that the haylāj meets signifies. For when the places of meeting are bad and afflicted, and the positions of the planets at the ingress of the following years harm the chief places, we should suppose that what happens then is death in truth; when one of these two matters is sound, we should suppose that what happens is injuries of great danger; and when both these matters are sound, we should suppose that what happens is sluggishness, weakness, harms and exile, by departure or travel. Instead of his word «exile» one may say «decline of rank, life abroad and journeys». The particular kinds that arise from these things are known from the places of the things that meet [the haylāj], for the matters of the native. Nothing prevents us, when we are in doubt at some times about the places that must have the matter of killing, from reflecting on and considering the things that meet the haylāj in each of the matters of the native: one should [look] in what comes in them for what agrees with the accidents that have already occurred and appeared, and examine the matter in all of them, when the matter, in the completion of what comes from them, is equal in strength in all of them, and so make our examination of their increase and decrease accordingly. One should correct the amounts of the applications and the distances between the haylājs and the planets according to the latitudes of the planets, as we have explained in the chapter on establishing the applications and the distances between the planets, before directing from the haylājs.

The sentence «when one of these two matters is sound … death in truth» stands twice, the first time with the wrong conclusion. @p AB03-F049r-L18..AB03-F050r-L07
Those who followed the doctrine we have described said about the giver who gives the years, the kadkhudāh — I mean the lord of the house, the exaltation, the triplicity [and the term] of the haylāj, putting the lord of the term first — that whichever of them looks at the haylāj is the kadkhudāh; or if two look at it together, the nearer of them in degrees, before or after, is the kadkhudāh. This is when each of them has a share in the ascendant, the lot of good fortune and the sign of the conjunction or the full moon; then it goes to the one in charge (?), whether its look is near or far, after you have looked at the degrees. If their testimonies are equal, the strongest of them in position; if they are equal in position, the nearest of them to the cardine or to what follows the cardine. If some of them are foreign and some in their dignities, the one in its dignities has the better claim, and the eastern, the farther from the sun, comes first. Know that when the sun is in Aries or Leo, it takes over both the haylāj-ship and the kadkhudāh-ship, and likewise the moon in Taurus or Cancer, because each of them has two dignities in these two signs — when there is no kadkhudāh looking at it. When you know the kadkhudāh: if it is in the ascendant or in the middle of the sky, eastern, in its dignities or in its suitability of masculine and feminine and of night and day, free from retrogradation and burning, [it signifies its greater years]; if it is in the eleventh or the fifth, exceeding what I have mentioned by one quality, it signifies its middle years; if it is in the second or the eighth, as I have described for the ascendant and the middle of the sky, or in the sixth or the twelfth, as I have described for the eleventh and the fifth, it signifies its lesser years. Whenever a quality is lacking, the rank of what the kadkhudāh signifies, from whatever place it may be, comes down one step. For example, what I have described of its being in the ascendant or the middle of the sky: if it is not eastern, it comes down one step, and its greater years go back to the middle ones; if besides it is not in some of its dignities or its suitability, it goes back to its lesser years; if besides it is retrograde, it goes back to its lesser years as months; and if besides it is burnt, to its lesser years as days. Speak of all these places by this analogy. They said that when the Head is with the kadkhudāh within 12 degrees, before or after, and the kadkhudāh is beneficent, it adds a quarter of the years of the kadkhudāh that it gives; if the Tail is in the place of the Head, it takes away as much; and if the kadkhudāh is maleficent, the Head takes away from its years and the Tail adds to them by the same analogy. The nearer the degrees, the more certain it is in both cases. If the kadkhudāh is in the condition that signifies nothing, and Jupiter and Venus, or one of them, are in the ascendant or the middle of the sky, the native may hope for as many years as the lesser years of whichever of them happens to be in the cardine we have mentioned, provided that it is not burnt (?) by the maleficent planets, and the moon afflicted (?).

@p AB03-F050r-L07..AB03-F050v-L08
When you know the years of the kadkhudāh, look at the planet that looks at the kadkhudāh. If a beneficent planet looks at it, free from the maleficent, from retrogradation and from burning, it adds as much as its lesser years. If a maleficent planet looks [at it] from opposition or quadrature, it takes away as much as its lesser years; if the maleficent planet has shares in the nativity and is eastern in some of its dignities, it takes away as many months as its lesser years; and if besides its look is one of trine or sextile, it takes away days only. Mercury, when it is with the beneficent planets and the givers, eastern or western, gives as much as its lesser years; and when with the maleficent and the diminishers, it takes away as much. The sun, in opposition and conjunction, takes away like the maleficent planets, and in quadrature it adds nothing but a little. The sun is most cutting and most maleficent for the kadkhudāh when it is with Mars, because their heats come together. When the kadkhudāh is under the rays, it cannot receive what the beneficent planets add, unless it is in the heart [of the sun], for then it is at its most fortunate, whether it is maleficent or beneficent. When you know the amount of the indication of the kadkhudāh, direct the haylāj by the degrees of ascension for its place in the sphere, in that way, to the bodies of the maleficent planets and their rays, to the Tail, to the quadrature, the opposition and the conjunction of the moon, and to the degree of the setting point, if it has passed (?) what is below the earth: a year for each degree. If those years agree with the years of the kadkhudāh, or are near them, and no beneficent planet casts its ray to that term in which the haylāj reached the ray of the maleficent planet, the native perishes in that year; especially when the lord of the native's year is afflicted, or the lord of the ascendant afflicted by that maleficent planet. If it reaches another maleficent planet that does not agree with the years of the kadkhudāh, there is a calamity; and whenever the haylāj reaches the body or the ray of a maleficent planet, whether a beneficent planet casts its ray to that degree or not, it is a year of calamity; and that is worse when the maleficent planet is in the course of the degrees of the haylāj, he means in latitude.
