⟨36⟩ On the native's death
@n *Tetrabiblos* IV.9.
@p AB03-F073r-L15..AB03-F073v-L08
Since what remains of the matters of nativities, after what we have mentioned, is the inquiry into the condition of death, we should first know, by the things we said on the length of life, whether the killing comes from a meeting and the casting of rays, or from the descent of the one in charge of the matter of life to the setting cardine. If the killing comes from the casting of rays and from meeting what it meets, one must examine the place where the haylāj and the ray, or the maleficent planet, meet, wherever they meet; if it comes from its reaching the degree of the setting point, one should examine the place of the setting point itself. For the condition of the death follows the planets that follow the places we have mentioned, if a planet follows them, or else the planets that come to them before any others. That is, when the killing comes from the casting of rays and from meeting what it meets of the maleficent planets, [it follows] the planets that follow the places of the meeting, which are those that rise after those places and also set after them; and when it comes at the arrival of the haylāj, its descent to the degree of the setting point, [it follows] the planets that set before the setting degree sets, which are the planets that precede the setting degree. With their natures one takes, in knowing the accidents and their variety, the properties of the planets that share in the configuration, if the killing comes from meeting them; and if it comes from the casting of their rays, the particular condition of the killing places that belong to them, in the place of the signs, by way of the natures of the terms.
@n «If» reads إن for ن; «share in» reads تشارك في for تشبه, «resemble».
@p AB03-F073v-L08..AB03-F073v-L21
When Saturn is the lord of death, it comes from chronic diseases, consumption, catarrhs, wasting, fever with shivering, pains of the spleen, dropsy and diarrhoea, or from illnesses of the womb, and all the illnesses that come from the prevalence and excess of cold. When the lord of death is Jupiter, death comes from quinsy, pneumonia, apoplexy, cramp, headache, illnesses of the heart, and all the illnesses that come from excessive wind or a foul smell. When its lord is Mars, it comes from continual fevers and the fevers called *hēmitritaios* (?), which is the half-tertian; from a sudden stroke, pains of the kidneys, illnesses of spitting blood and haemorrhage, miscarriage, childbirth, erysipelas, deadly illnesses, and all the diseases that bring a burning fever and excess of bile. When its lord is Venus, it comes from illness of the stomach, the liver and the heart, from bloody flux, from spreading tumours and fistulas, from the drinking of drugs, and from all the things in which accidents arise from excess or corruption of moisture. When the lord of death is Mercury, death comes from madness, loss of reason, melancholic obsession, the falling sickness, epilepsy, illnesses of coughing and spitting, and all the illnesses that come from excess or corruption of dryness.
@n «Diarrhoea» reads الذرب for الدرف. The Greek ἡμιτριταῖος is written {انمرطرلطا} (the Berlin manuscript امطر طاوس, the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* طرناوس). «Fistulas» reads النواصير for النواصيّة.
@p AB03-F073v-L21..AB03-F074r-L09
Those who die their own death depart from life in these ways mentioned, through all of them or through some, when the planets to which the matter of death belongs are in their proper or familiar natural condition, and none of the planets from which a death becomes a bad and hard death overpowers them. The death is one of violence and turmoil — the death that comes by killing, or by other accidents of calamity — when the two maleficent planets together dominate the place of killing, either by being in those places, or in quadrature to them, or opposite them on the diameter; or when one of them, whichever it may be, dominates the sun, or the moon, or both. In another copy we have found: «when it looks at the luminaries or at either of them». The bad death comes from the meeting of the two maleficent planets; the magnitude of the death comes from the witness of the luminaries; and the kind of death comes from the regard of the other planets and from the signs in which the two maleficent planets are.
@n «Their own death» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (ميتة) for موته; «or familiar» follows the Berlin manuscript (أو الملائمة), and «a bad death» reads سوء for متوء. The copyist wrote «befalls» (يعرض) before «other» and struck it out.
@p AB03-F074r-L09..AB03-F074v-L04
When Saturn is in quadrature or opposition to the sun from the opposite domain: if it is in the fixed signs, death comes from it through stoning by the common people, through strangling or through torment; and likewise when it is western and the moon follows it, that is, if Saturn is in the seventh and the moon after it in the eighth, or setting after it. If it is in the signs whose figures resemble animals, death comes through being devoured by wild beasts; if Jupiter witnesses — that is, looks — and is afflicted, the native is thrown to the beasts in the places where people gather, or on famous days; and if it is opposite one of the luminaries, whichever it may be, and is opposite the ascendant or in the ascendant, the death is in prison. If it shares a configuration with Mercury, especially in the place of the figure of the Serpent in the sphere, or in the signs whose figures are those of land animals, death comes from the stings and bites of venomous creatures; if Venus is with them, from drinking deadly poisons and from the plots of women. If the place of killing is in Virgo or Pisces or in the moist signs, and the moon shares in the configuration, it comes from drowning in water; if it is in the place called the Ship, death comes from the terrors of the sea. If it is with the sun in the signs of the solstices, or in the signs whose figures are those of four-footed beasts, or is opposite Mars in place of the sun, it is through [a house] falling on him. In another copy: «or Mars is opposite the place of the sun or the moon, or in quadrature to them, outside their domain». If it is in the middle of the sky or in the cardine of the earth, death comes from a fall from a high place, such as a raised place of the ground, or [into] a well, a pit and the like, when it is in the cardine of the earth.
@n «Strangling» reads خنق for حنق, «rage», and «western» follows the Berlin manuscript (غربيًّا) for غاريبًا. Ptolemy has trampling in a mob and indurations for stoning and torment. «Famous» reads مشهورة for شهورة; Ptolemy has days of celebration. «The Serpent» reads الحيّة for الحياة, «life»; «land» reads البرّيّ for البريء. «A well» reads البئر for النيّر, «the luminary».
@p AB03-F074v-L04..AB03-F074v-L16
As for Mars: if it is in quadrature or opposition to the sun or the moon, against the domain, then if it is in the signs whose figures are human figures, death comes from slaughter that happens in a tumult among a people and their tribe, or from wars, or from a man's killing himself because of women, or from killing women, which is when Venus witnesses. If Mercury shares a configuration with it, destruction comes at the hands of robbers, highwaymen at sea and scoundrels. If it is in the signs with severed limbs, whose figures are not complete, or is with the Ghoul's Head — the one who is called Perseus, that is, the knight who holds the Ghoul's Head — or, in another copy, «in the signs with severed limbs, or rising with the Ghoul's Head», death is by beheading or by the cutting off of limbs. If it is in Scorpio and Taurus, death is by burning, by cutting, by the lancing (?) of physicians, or by cramp. If it is in the middle of the sky, or in what is opposite the middle of the sky, that is, the cardine of the earth, death is by crucifixion, especially when it is with Cepheus, who is the Inflamed One, and Andromeda. If it is in the setting sign or the ascendant, the man is burned alive; if it is in four-footed signs, it comes from a fall, breaking and crushing; and if Jupiter witnesses to it and is afflicted, destruction comes by a public sentence and the anger of rulers and kings.
@n «Or the moon» follows the Berlin manuscript (أو القمر) for «and the moon». «Scoundrels» reads الدعّار; the editor supplies ⟨ة⟩, «debauchery». «Lancing» reads ببطّ for the doubtful {ببطء}, «slowness»; Ptolemy has amputation by physicians. «Cepheus» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (قيفوس) for {فيغاوس}; al-Multahib, «the Inflamed One», is an Arabic name of the constellation. Ptolemy has opposition to the ascendant for «the ascendant». «Crushing» reads سحق for سحب, «dragging».
@p AB03-F074v-L16..AB03-F074v-L23
If the two maleficent planets come together, or one of them is opposite the other, in one of the conditions we have mentioned as causes of death, they help especially towards a bad death. The lord of the matter in the kind of death is the lord of the killing place, and the accidents of the death are doubled, either in quality or in quantity, when both maleficent planets have shares in the killing places. One in whom the planets are in this condition is not buried or covered: beasts of prey and birds with talons devour him, when the two maleficent planets are in signs whose figures are those of the things we have mentioned and none of the beneficent planets witnesses to the killing places while below the earth — the beneficent planet is meant. Death occurs abroad when the planets that dominate the killing places fall in the declining places, especially when the moon is in the places we have mentioned, or in quadrature to them, or opposite them.
@n «A bad death» and «the kind» follow the Berlin manuscript (المنيّة، حال) for المايّة and الحال; «doubled» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (مثنّاة) for مثنى. «Declining» reads الساقطة for القاتلة, «killing», which the transcription repeats; Ptolemy has the declining places.
