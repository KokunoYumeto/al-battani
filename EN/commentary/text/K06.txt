⟨6⟩ On the sharing of the signs in the configurations
@n *Tetrabiblos* I.13–17.
@p AB03-F021v-L20..AB03-F022r-L09
As for the matters of the sharings in the configurations, which are the conjunction, the opposition, the quadrature, the sextile and the trine; of the signs that look at one another and are equal in power; of the commanding and the obeying signs; and of the signs that are not joined to one another and do not share in the configurations: we have explained them earlier in this book, since [there was given] the cause that shows why these distances alone are used and none other of the geometrical things. So too the others: the contrary and associated signs are those opposite one another; the hostile signs are those in quadrature to one another; and the friendly signs are those in trine to one another, and also those in sextile, since these two configurations fall between signs of one kind, either masculine or feminine, while the first two configurations are otherwise. Moreover, the houses of the two maleficent planets are in opposition and in quadrature to the houses of the luminaries, and the houses of the two beneficent planets in trine and in sextile to the houses of the luminaries.
@n Ptolemy explains why only these distances are used in *Tetrabiblos* I.13, al-Battānī in chapter 54 of the *Zīj*. «The two maleficent planets» reads النحسين for the transcription's الجنسين: the houses of Saturn, Capricorn and Aquarius, are opposite those of the moon and the sun, Cancer and Leo, and the houses of Mars, Aries and Scorpio, in quadrature to them (*Tetrabiblos* I.17).
