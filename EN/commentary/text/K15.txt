⟨15⟩ On knowing the matters of nativities and what is inferred in them
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.1.
@p AB03-F034v-L13..AB03-F034v-L15
The first thing we should consider among the matters of nativities: since people have a temporal beginning of their coming to be, and this beginning belongs to this very kind of consideration, for whoever wishes to look closely and to delve into the examination of the properties that are in the composition itself alone.
@n The sentence is incomplete. Ptolemy's introduction to the nativities (*Tetrabiblos* III.1) takes as the starting point of a human being the time of conception or, failing it, of birth.
