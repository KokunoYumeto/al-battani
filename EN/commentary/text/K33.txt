⟨33⟩ On children
@n *Tetrabiblos* IV.6.
@p AB03-F070r-L16..AB03-F070v-L02
We should look at the planets that are in the place over our heads, or in the place that follows it, which is the place of good fortune, or in a place that shares a configuration with it. If we do not find any there, we should take the planets that are opposite them on the diameter. We use the moon, Jupiter and Venus for the giving of children, and the sun, Mars and Saturn for their absence or their fewness; and we put Mercury with one of the two, I mean with whichever of the two kinds shares a configuration with it: when it is eastern it gives children, and when it is western their absence comes from it.
@n «The place» follows the Berlin manuscript (الموضع) for المواضع. The copyist repeated «or in the place that follows it, which is the place of good fortune» and struck it out. «The moon» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (القمر); «absence» reads عدمهم for عددهم, «their number».
@p AB03-F070v-L03..AB03-F070v-L17
When the giving planets are, in general, these planets, and they are alone, the child is single; when they are in bicorporeal and feminine signs, the children are two (?); and likewise when they are in signs of many offspring, like Pisces, Cancer and Scorpio, what is born is two or more than two. If they are masculine in the signs and in their configurations relative to the sun, what is born is male; if they are feminine in the signs, what is born is female. If the maleficent planets prevail over them, or they are in barren signs like Leo and Virgo, children are born, but not for good, and what is born does not last. If the sun and the maleficent planets dominate these places mentioned before, then, if they are in masculine or barren signs and the maleficent prevail over them, they signify no children at all; if they are in feminine signs, or in signs of many offspring, or the beneficent planets witness to them, they signify a child with an injury, or a child of short life. If both domains have a share in the begetting signs, loss of children comes of it, of some or of all of them, and its measure follows the predominance of the planets that witness to each of the two domains over the other: whether their predominance is found in the number of the planets, or happens to lie in their strength, because they are more eastern, or more firmly placed on the cardine, or more overpowering, or rising to the cardine.
@n «Feminine» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (مؤنّثة) for مرتبة; «two» guesses at دون إثنين, «less than two»; Ptolemy has two or more. The copyist wrote «small» (صغورًا) before «male» and struck it out. «Not for good» reads لا لخير for لخير, «for good». Ptolemy has «not overcome by the beneficent planets» where the Arabic has the maleficent prevailing. «Domains» reads الحيّزين for الجزئين, «the two parts», both times, and «begetting» المولّدة for المولوده; «more eastern» follows the Berlin manuscript and the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (تشريقًا) for تشريفًا.
@p AB03-F070v-L17..AB03-F071r-L07
If the planets that are the lords of the signs mentioned, and that give children, are eastern in their own places, the child is noble and famous; if they are western, or in the places of the domain opposite to them, the child is lowly and unnoted. If they agree with the ascendant and with the lot of fortune, the child is loved by his parents, and lives to marry and to inherit his parents' wealth; if they are disjunct or opposed, the child is quarrelsome and hostile to his parents and does not inherit their property. Again, if the planets that give children share in an agreeing configuration, the brothers love one another and honour one another; if they are disjunct or opposite on the diameter, the child loves enmity with his brothers and plots against them. The particular matters of the children one can know only by using, for each of them, the planet that is the cause of the giving of children, putting it in the place of the ascendant, and so knowing from the other conditions found there the particular matters of the children, in the main, as we know them from nativities.
@n «Lowly» reads ذليلًا for دليلًا, «a guide». For «the ascendant» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «the hour» (للساعة). «The planet» follows the Berlin manuscript, where the copyist struck out the letter that makes it plural; «the cause of the giving» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (سبب إعطاء) for سبيل عطاء, «the way of giving».
