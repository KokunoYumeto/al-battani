⟨40⟩ [Other divisions of the year]
@p AB03-F079v-L03..AB03-F079v-L10
Others have divided the year into twelve months by the course of the sun. They made each month begin when the sun, moving from the sign it has reached, comes to the same [degree] of the signs in which it was in the root nativity, from the sign in which it was; and they said: see by how many degrees the sun has moved from its place in the root nativity, add that to the ascendant of the nativity and to the other haylājs, and see which degree of the signs it falls on: that is the place of that day. We should do the like with the signs of the things (?) in the years too. Others said: take [the distance] from the sun to the moon on each day, cast it from the ascendant of the root nativity, and put it for the native, on that day, in the place of the lot of good fortune.
@n «Course» reads مسير for مصيّر; «put it» reads فتقيمه for the doubtful {تقميّة}.
@p AB03-F079v-L10..AB03-F079v-L12
The Book of the Four, the commentary of al-Battānī, is complete, with the praise of God and divine help (?), on the thirteenth of the blessed month of Shawwāl of the year nine hundred and thirty-nine. Praise be to God, and peace.
@n The colophon is the copyist's. «Thirty-» is supplied by the editor (ثلاث⟨ين⟩); 13 Shawwāl 939 of the Hijra is 8 May 1533 in the arithmetical calendar. «Divine help» guesses at وسنة. The Berlin manuscript ends: «The Book of the Four, the commentary of al-Battānī, is complete, by the praise and the grace of God. Aḥmad ibn Tamīm Aḥmad copied it, praising God for the divine blessings and invoking blessing and peace on the Prophet Muḥammad and the Prophet's family; God is our sufficiency, and how excellent a guardian.»
