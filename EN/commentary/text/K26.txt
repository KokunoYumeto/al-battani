⟨26⟩ On the illnesses [and] diseases that befall the native's body
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.12.
@p AB03-F052r-L18..AB03-F052v-L12
In this place, when we wish to know this matter as a whole, we must look at the two cardines of the horizon, the ascendant and the setting point, and in particular at the setting point itself and at the place before the setting point, which is the house of illness; and we must examine the two maleficent planets and see how we find their condition relative to these places. For when, compared with the degrees that rise to the places we have mentioned, both of them or one of them is found on the place itself, or in its quadrature, or in its opposition, one should suppose that injuries and illnesses will befall the native and enter the body; especially if both luminaries or one of them are on the cardines in the manner we have mentioned, either on one cardine or on two opposite cardines. For then a maleficent planet is not strong only when it rises to them after the luminaries, with the other maleficent planet on the cardine, signifying various events among the injuries and illnesses we have mentioned, like the injuries and illnesses signified by the places of the horizon, the places of the signs, the maleficent and the afflicted planets and the planets that share in a configuration with them; it may also be strong for that when it rises before the luminaries. For the parts of each of the signs that surround the two parts of the horizon that receive the harm signify the part of the body in which that event occurs, and whether what occurs in that limb is an injury, an illness or both.
@n «Itself» and «not» are written in the margin; «in which» follows the Berlin manuscript (الذي). The place before the setting point is the sixth, which Ptolemy calls disjunct from the ascendant.
@p AB03-F052v-L12..AB03-F052v-L19
The natures of the planets produce the kinds of the accidents and their causes. For of the principal parts of the human being, Saturn has the right ear, the spleen, the bladder and the phlegm; Jupiter, touch, the lung, the ribs, the arteries and the semen; Mars, the left ear, the kidneys, the veins and the genitals — in most copies «the small members» stands in place of «the genitals»; the sun, sight, the brain, the heart, the sinews and all the members of the right side; Venus, smell, the liver and the fat; Mercury, thought, speech, the tongue, the gall and the seat; and the moon, taste, swallowing, the stomach, the belly, the womb and all the members of the left side.
@n «Speech» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (النطق) for النظر, «sight». Ptolemy gives Saturn the bones as well, and Jupiter no ribs.
@p AB03-F052v-L19..AB03-F053r-L02
In general, an injury occurs for the most part when the maleficent planets that are its cause are eastern, and illnesses occur when these planets are western. For there is a difference between these two things: an injury befalls once, and its pain does not last, while diseases befall either continually or in bouts.
@p AB03-F053r-L03..AB03-F053r-L21
For the things by which the particular accidents are grasped, conditions and configurations that signify injury and illness have been found, when they were examined and observed with particular observation; and this was known from the accidents that follow and attach, for the most part, to similar positions of the planets. Loss of sight in one of the eyes occurs when the moon alone is on the cardines mentioned before, at the time of the conjunction or of the opposition; and when it is in another of its configurations relative to the sun and applies to one of the cloud-like nebulous stars in the sphere of the signs, such as the cloudy star in the breast of Cancer, the Pleiades of Taurus, the sign of the Head (?), the sting of Scorpio, the parts of Leo around the Braid that are called the Locks, or the pitcher (*qalbīs*) of Aquarius — that is, the outpouring of the water and the spout of the bucket, which is the handle (?). [It occurs too] when the moon is on one of the cardines and is western, and Mars and Saturn are eastern and moving towards it from behind; or when the sun is on one of the cardines and these two planets rise and ascend before them (?). If they share in a configuration with both luminaries, the luminaries being together in one sign or opposite on the diameter, as we have said, and in their sharing [with the sun] are attributed to the mornings, and in their sharing with the moon to the evenings, the event is in both eyes. From Mars comes what we have mentioned through a blow that falls, a collision, iron or burning; if Mercury shares in the configuration, it comes about through wrestling, exercise, or what wicked people do. From Saturn it comes about through water descending into the eyes, through cold, through glaucoma, or the like.
@n The copyist repeated «by which the accidents are grasped» and struck it out. «The Braid» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (الضفيرة) for الصغيرة, «the small one», and «pitcher» its القلبيس, the Greek *kalpis*, for القليسين; the Locks are Coma Berenices. Where the Arabic has «the sign of the Head», Ptolemy has the arrow point of Sagittarius. The transcription has «or» before «Mars»; the Berlin manuscript omits it, and Ptolemy has Mars or Saturn. Water descending into the eyes is the cataract.
@p AB03-F053r-L21..AB03-F053v-L13
Again, if Venus is on the cardines mentioned before, especially on the setting cardine, and is with Saturn or shares a configuration with it, or the two have exchanged their places in the ascendant and the setting point, and Mars is above them or opposite them on the diameter, it happens from that to the men that no children are born to them, and to the women miscarriage or birth before its time; and the cutting of the foetus may also befall them; that happens especially in Cancer, Virgo and Capricorn. If the moon applies to Mars from the east, as though increasing in the ascendant (?), and Venus too shares with Mercury in signifying this condition with Saturn, and Mars is above them or opposite them on the diameter, the native is a eunuch or a hermaphrodite, or has the passage and the outlet closed. When these things are so, and the sun too shares in the configuration, and the luminaries and Venus are masculine, the moon western and the maleficent planets in the degrees that follow, a male native is castrated or injured in the genitals, especially in Aries, Leo, Scorpio, Capricorn and Aquarius; the females have no children and are barren; and sometimes these do not escape an injury to the sight.
@n «No» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (لا); Ptolemy has sterile men. Where the Arabic has Venus, Ptolemy has the moon bearing to Mercury the same aspect as Saturn. «These things» follows the Berlin manuscript (الأشياء) for {الأمر}. «Castrated» reads مجبوبًا for مجنونًا, «mad»; Ptolemy has deprived of the sexual organs. The copyist repeated «an injury» and struck it out.
@p AB03-F053v-L13..AB03-F053v-L17
Natives whose tongues are tied, who lisp, or the like, are those who have Saturn and Mercury with the sun on the cardines we have mentioned, especially when Mercury too is western and both share a configuration with the moon. If Mars happens to be with these two planets, it is in its nature for the most part to loosen the tying of the tongue from the time when the moon meets it.
@n For «loosen» (يحلّ) the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «make» (يجعل).
@p AB03-F053v-L17..AB03-F054r-L08
Again, if both luminaries move towards the afflicted and maleficent planets on the cardines, or the maleficent planets face the luminaries on the diameter, especially if the moon is in one of the two nodes, or in the bending (?) — the place of the turning at the limit of the latitude, as it is seen, for the *qasmūn* (?) is the bent (?) thing — or in the afflicted signs, like Aries, Taurus, Cancer, Scorpio and Capricorn, an injury befalls the body: a hump, a chronic disability, lameness, dislocation or paralysis. When the maleficent planets are with the luminaries, these injuries befall from the very time of the birth; if they are in the middle of the sky, or above the luminaries, with their course in longitude with them — that is, above them in latitude — these injuries come from great and dangerous accidents, like falls from cliffs and from other places, falling into the hands of robbers, or four-footed beasts. If Mars is the one that prevails, these injuries come from burning by fire, from bad heat, or from falling into the hands of highwaymen and robbers; if the one that prevails is Saturn, they come from a fall, from drowning in water, or from cramp.
@n The word for the moon's bendings is read {القسون} and القسمون, and «bent» renders the doubtful {المنحرث}; Ptolemy speaks of the nodes or the bendings of the moon. «Accidents» reads أعراض for أعرض. For «bad heat» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «harmful abscesses» (الخراجات المؤذيّة); Ptolemy has fire, wounds, bilious attacks or robberies.
@p AB03-F054r-L09..AB03-F054r-L13
Injuries befall for the most part when the moon is on the points of the two solstices and the two equinoxes. When it is on the point of the spring equinox, the injury that befalls is in particular white leprosy; when the moon is on the point of the summer solstice, what befalls is lichens (?); when it is on the point of the autumn equinox, what befalls is leprosy; and when it is on the point of the winter solstice, what befalls for the most part is the marks called lentils, and the like.
@n «Spring» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (الربيعيّ) for الذي يبقى, «the remaining»; «white leprosy» reads البهق for إليهنّ, «to them». «Lichens» renders the doubtful {القوابيّ}.
@p AB03-F054r-L13..AB03-F054v-L03
Accidents and illnesses are apt to befall when the maleficent planets are in the condition we have mentioned in their sharing in a configuration with the sun or the moon, but the other way round: I mean that when the maleficent planets share with the moon, they are attributed to the mornings. What comes of the diseases in general is what I describe. Saturn makes the temperament of the native's belly cold; the native has much phlegm, with matters flowing down to the limbs, is lean and sickly, with jaundice, ulceration of the bowels, cough, spitting of blood, colic and elephantiasis; and women have besides pains of the womb. Mars makes the native spit blood, with melancholic obsessions, pneumonia and scabies; and besides, harm befalls the native through lancing or cautery used for illnesses of the hidden places, like fistulas, haemorrhoids, what sprouts and swells in the body, hot and fiery ulcers and spreading ulcers; and women have besides miscarriage of the foetus, its cutting, or its corrosion.
@n Ptolemy has the maleficent planets evening stars to the sun and morning stars to the moon.
@p AB03-F054v-L03..AB03-F054v-L10
The natures that we have said belong to the planets sharing in the configuration may also produce particular illnesses in the members of the body. What helps them in governing these things is Mercury in particular: for it helps Saturn, or agrees with it in the governing, especially in producing the flowing down of matters into the limbs and the harm from moistures, above all those that descend to the palate, the chest and the stomach; and it helps Mars in the matter of drying and what comes of it, like the crusts of ulcers, eschars, abscesses, the swellings known as erysipelas, malignant lichens, melancholy, madness, epilepsy and the like.
@n «Matters» follows the Berlin manuscript (الموادّ) for المولد.
@p AB03-F054v-L10..AB03-F054v-L19
Illnesses may also have properties according to the difference of the signs in which the sharing of the configuration mentioned before, on the two cardines, takes place. Cancer, Capricorn and Pisces, and all the signs whose figures resemble land animals or have the figure of a fish, produce in particular spreading illnesses, lichens, gout, scrofula, fistulas, elephantiasis and the like. Sagittarius and Gemini produce the illnesses in which falling and epilepsy occur. When the planets are in the last parts of the signs, the illnesses and injuries that occur are in the extremities of the body in particular; they befall there through a corruption that strikes them and a flowing down of superfluities, from which elephantiasis comes, and for the most part gout in the hands and the feet.
@n «Land» reads البرّيّ for البريء, «innocent»; «spreading» reads الساعية for السعاعيّة. Where the Arabic has gout among the illnesses of these signs, Ptolemy has scales.
@p AB03-F054v-L19..AB03-F055r-L11
When things are in this condition, if the beneficent planets share no configuration with the maleficent planets that are the cause of the action, nor with the luminaries when these are on the cardines, the injuries and illnesses that befall are harmful and inescapable; and likewise when they share a configuration with them but the maleficent planets are above the beneficent, or strong. If the beneficent planets are in their noble configurations and are above the maleficent planets that are the cause of the action, the injuries are then not ugly nor of a kind that brings shame, and the illnesses are mild and quick to subside, and sometimes easy to change too; that is when the beneficent planets are rising. For it is in the nature of Jupiter to hide the injuries and to calm the illnesses through the help that those who have the illnesses and the injuries find from people, because of their wealth or their ranks; and when it is with Mercury, the illnesses are calmed by drugs or by the treatment of good physicians. Venus makes the injuries somewhat comely, by causes that come from God, mighty and glorious, or from revelation, and calms the illnesses by treatments whose causes come from God the Exalted. If Mercury is with it, that comes with help and gain that reach the one who has the injury and the illness, from the very thing they suffer.
@n «People» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (بالناس) for بالبأس, «by force»; «mild» reads خفيفة for خفّيّة, «hidden». For «noble» the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has «eastern» (الشرقيّة). Ptolemy adds that with Saturn present the healing comes with the display and confession of the disease.
