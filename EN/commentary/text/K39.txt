⟨39⟩ [The place of a day]
@p AB03-F079r-L18..AB03-F079v-L03
If you wish to know the place of the day you want in the month you are in, enter with the number of the complete days that have passed of the month of birth, or of the month of the revolution, into the table of the increments for the days, and take what stands under it, the parts and minutes. Add what results to the haylāj in the sign of the month, and cast what results from the sign of the month, thirty degrees to each sign: where it ends among the signs is the place of the beginning of the day you wanted, in the sign in which that number falls, for each of the five haylājs. Then look at the planet among the wandering stars that is in that place, or the one that shares a configuration with it, and at the planet that governs that sign.
@n «Or the one» follows the Berlin manuscript (أو الذي). Thirty degrees to a sign of two days and a sixth make an increment of about 13° 53′ a day.
