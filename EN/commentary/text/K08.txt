⟨8⟩ On the planets' becoming masculine and feminine relative to the sun, and also relative to the horizon
@n *Tetrabiblos* I.6.
@p AB03-F022v-L17..AB03-F023r-L02
They hold that the planets become masculine and feminine according to the configurations they have relative to the sun: when they are eastern, preceding the sun, they are masculine, and when they are western, following it, they are feminine. This also happens relative to the horizon: when they are in the positions from the east to the middle of the sky, or from the west to the cardine of the earth, they are masculine, because these two quarters are masculine; the first of them is called «the swift» and the second «the middle of speed». When they are between the middle of the sky and the west, or between the cardine of the earth and the ascendant, they are feminine, because these two quarters are feminine; the first of them is called «the middle of slowness» (?), and the second, between the ascendant and the cardine of the earth, «the slow and heavy».
@n The words from «they are masculine, because» to «or between the cardine of the earth» are written in the margin. «The swift» reads السريع for الشربع, and «the slow» البطيء for البطن, «the belly».
