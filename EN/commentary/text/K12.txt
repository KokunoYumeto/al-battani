⟨12⟩ On the colours of eclipses and the stars with tails
@n *Tetrabiblos* II.9.
@p AB03-F030v-L07..AB03-F030v-L17
One should also consider the colours that appear at the times of eclipses, I mean the colours of the luminaries themselves and the colours of the things around them, when such arise around them, like those called rods, staffs, haloes and the like. If they are black, dense and inclining to green, they signify the things that we have said are of the nature of Saturn; if white, the things of the nature of Jupiter; if inclining to red, the things of the nature of Mars; if tawny and golden, the things of the nature of Venus; and if of varied colours, the things of the nature of Mercury. If the colour is over the whole body of the luminaries, or in all the places around them, the event will be in most parts of the countries; if it is in one part of the luminaries or of what is around them, whatever part it may be, the event befalls only the region that faces the part in which the colour is.
@p AB03-F030v-L17..AB03-F031r-L05
You should also look at the stars with tails that appear at the times of eclipses or at other times, and at the other general phenomena that arise, like those called staffs, trumpets, jars and the like; and take their natures, in what they produce, to be the same as what we said the nature of Mars and the nature of Mercury produce: wars, burning, severe heat, earthquakes and the accidents that come from them. The places in which the events occur are inferred from their positions in the sphere of the signs, or from the places that the direction of the tail of the stars faces. From the form of the phenomenon that appears one infers the kind of event that occurs; the class in which the event occurs, from the positions in the sphere of the signs in which that thing appears; the duration of the events, from the time for which the phenomenon that appears lasts; and the time of the beginning of the event, from its relation to the sun. For, for the most part, when they appear while attributed to the mornings, the event from them is swift, and when they appear while attributed to the evenings, the event from them is slow.
@n «Like those called» follows the Berlin manuscript and the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (مثل); for the jars (T: الخوابئ) the transcription has والحرّاي. The words from «to the mornings» to «while attributed» are written in the margin.
