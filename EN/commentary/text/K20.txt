⟨20⟩ On males and females
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.6.
@p AB03-F041r-L02..AB03-F041r-L14
Knowledge of the male and the female sex of those born comes from both luminaries, from the ascendant and from the planets that have shares in them. It is known especially from the condition at the time of the falling of the seed, and in general from the condition at the time of the birth too. One should examine these places that we have mentioned and the planets that govern them: if they are all, or most of them, masculine, the child is male; if feminine, female. One should distinguish the masculine and the feminine places and planets in the manner that we mentioned at the beginning of this book: I mean from the nature of the signs in which they are, from the nature of the planets themselves, and from their condition relative to the world — for they become masculine when they are eastern of the horizon and feminine when they are western — and also from their condition relative to the sun, for they become masculine when they are attributed to the mornings and feminine when attributed to the evenings; and from all of that one conjectures which sex will for the most part prevail in the child.
