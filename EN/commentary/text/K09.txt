⟨9⟩ On facing, illumination and the like
@n *Tetrabiblos* I.23, where Robbins translates Ptolemy's terms «proper face» and «chariots».
@p AB03-F023r-L03..AB03-F023r-L12
It is said that the planets have a proper facing when each of them shares in a configuration with the sun and the moon in this way: that there is between it and the sun or the moon the same distance as between its house and the house of the sun or of the moon, except that it is western of the sun, I mean in the following signs, and eastern of the moon, in the preceding signs. For example, Venus in sextile to one of the luminaries, but in the third sign from the sign of the sun in the order of the signs, or in the eleventh sign from the sign in which the moon is; or Mercury in the second sign from the sign of the sun, or in the twelfth from the sign of the moon. For the other planets reckon their houses in the same way. When the planets are in this condition with respect to the luminaries, they are facing them.
@n The manuscript first wrote «or in the twelfth from the sign of the moon, and the other planets», struck it out, and wrote the correct passage in the margin, from «in the order of the signs» to «or in the twelfth».
@p AB03-F023r-L12..AB03-F023r-L19
As for illumination, it is said [to be] when the planets are in their own domain and in places like it that agree with them; and when one of them has two or more of its dignities, as when it is in its house, its exaltation, its triplicity and its term and the like, [it is said] to be in the place of its joy and honour, and then its power increases in its actions that resemble the signs that belong to it and are akin to them in nature. It is also said that they rejoice when they are in the dignities of planets of their own domain; this is weaker than the first, because it is below it in power, but they share in it in this way all the same: just as, when they are found in foreign places or in a domain contrary to their own, their proper power weakens, and the contrariety of their mixture to the mixture of the signs in which they are becomes a cause for an action compounded of the two different natures to arise from them.
@n «Domain», حيّز, *ḥayyiz*; Ptolemy speaks here of the «sect» of the planets.
