⟨32⟩ On marriage
@n *Tetrabiblos* IV.5. For «marriage» (التأمّل, a slip for التأهّل) the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* has التأهّل wherever the word occurs in this chapter.
@p AB03-F067r-L04..AB03-F067v-L06
Since the account of marriage follows what we have described, our consideration of the lawful marriage of men and of women should be as I describe. For the marriage of men we should look at the condition of the moon at the time of their birth. We look first at the moon: if it is in the two eastern quarters, the man marries in youth, or marries a young woman after growing old; if it is in the two western quarters, the man is slow to marry, or marries an old woman; and if it is under the rays and shares a configuration with Saturn, the man does not marry at all. After that we look whether the moon is in a sign of one form, or also applies to one planet: if it is so, the man marries one woman; if it is in a bicorporeal sign or in a sign of many forms, and applies to more than one planet in one and the same sign, the man marries often. If the planets to which it applies, either by conjunction or by witness, are beneficent, the women whom the man marries are good; if they are maleficent, the women are the opposite. When the planet to which it applies is Saturn, the wife is hard-working and of rough character; if it applies to Jupiter, chaste and a good manager; if to Mars, bold and unsubmissive; if to Venus, beautiful, dutiful and cheerful; if to Mercury, the wife is intelligent and inventive. Again, if Venus is with Jupiter, or with Saturn, or with Mercury, the wife is useful in the matter of livelihood and loving to husband and children; if it is with Mars, the wife is corrupt, without steadiness or understanding. Instead of the words «if Venus is with Jupiter» one may say «when the moon applies to Jupiter together with Venus».
@n «Witness» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (الشهادة) for السعادة, «good fortune», and «cheerful» its باشّة for سارّة, «gladdening».
@p AB03-F067v-L06..AB03-F068r-L05
For the marriage of women we should look at the condition of the sun at the time of their birth. When the sun is in the two eastern quarters, the woman marries in youth, or marries a young man after growing old; when it is in the two western quarters, the woman marries [late], or marries an old man while still young. If it is in a sign of one form, and you find in it one planet attributed to the mornings, the woman marries one man; if it is in bicorporeal signs or in signs of many forms, or more than one of the planets attributed to the mornings shares a configuration with it, the woman marries often. Likewise, when Saturn shares a configuration with the sun, the husband is difficult, without affection and unsubmissive; if Venus shares with them, the husband is pure and handsome; if Mercury shares with them, useful in the matter of livelihood and hard-working. If Venus is with [Saturn], the husband is sluggish and wary of intercourse; if Venus is with Mars, the husband is of a hot temperament and absorbed in intercourse; and if it is with Mercury, tender towards their father (?). What is meant here by the two eastern quarters is as we mentioned at the beginning of the book: for the sun, the two quarters that precede the rising point and the setting point of the ecliptic; for the moon, the two quarters from the conjunction and from the full moon until the moon is half lit on either side of the full moon. The two western quarters are the two quarters opposite these two.
@n Something is lost after «Saturn shares a configuration with the sun»: Ptolemy gives Saturn sedate and industrious husbands and Jupiter dignified ones, and gives Mars the difficult, unaffectionate and unruly husbands that the Arabic gives to Saturn. «Pure» reads طاهرًا for ظاهرًا, «manifest». For «tender towards their father» Ptolemy has infatuated with boys.
@p AB03-F068r-L05..AB03-F068v-L01
Marriage is for the most part steady and lasting when the luminaries share in a configuration in both nativities, I mean the man's nativity and the woman's, with an agreeing sharing, that is, in sextile or trine; especially when that happens by exchange, and above all when the moon in the man's nativity shares a configuration with the sun in the woman's nativity (?). The marriage is dissolved completely, parting for the slightest cause, when the places we have mentioned of the luminaries are in disjunct signs, or opposite on the diameter, or in quadrature. If the beneficent planets see the luminaries that agree in sharing a configuration, the marriage remains pleasant, loved and useful; if the maleficent see them, it lasts with quarrelling, dislike and loss. The matter goes in the same way in the conditions that do not agree: when the beneficent planets witness to the luminaries, the marriage does not part or dissolve once and for all, but the parting is one in which there are returns, remembrance and kindness, through which love and familiarity last. If the maleficent planets witness to them, the couple part with evil between them, and with force; if Mercury comes to be with them, the parting comes with notoriety (?), blame and reproach; and if Venus is with them, that comes about through adultery, sorcery and the like.
@n «Is dissolved» reads وينحلّ for ولم نحل, «and we did not dissolve»; «dislike» reads كراهة for كرامة, «honour»; «notoriety» reads شهرة for شهوة, «desire»; Ptolemy has notoriety.
@p AB03-F068v-L02..AB03-F069r-L08
Pairings that come about in any other way whatever we should distinguish and know by looking at Venus, Mars and Saturn. If they are with the luminaries in a familiar condition, the marriage is suitable and lawful; for between Venus and each of these two planets there is a relation. Its relation to Mars signifies a pairing with a young person, and the relation between them arises because the exaltation of each of them is in a sign of the other's triplicity; its relation to Saturn signifies a marriage with an old person, and the relation between them arises because the exaltation of each of them is in a sign of the other's triplicity. For that reason Venus with Mars produces conditions of love simply; if Mercury is with them, it is notorious too; and if they are in signs common to them, like Capricorn and Pisces, it signifies that the intercourse is among siblings and kin. If the moon is present in the nativities of men, the man is one who goes with two sisters, or with two women related to each other; if Jupiter is present in the nativities of women, the woman is one whom two brothers, or two men related to each other, take. Again, if Venus is with Saturn, the marriage is in general pleasant and lasts well; if Mercury is with them besides, it is useful; and if Mars is with it, the marriage is unsteady and harmful, with jealousy. If the condition of Venus is like theirs, the pairing is between a woman and someone of the same age; if it is more eastern than they, the woman marries someone older and the man an older woman. If Venus and Saturn are in signs common to them, like Capricorn and Libra, the pairing falls among kin. If the moon is in the condition we have mentioned, and this condition is in the ascendant or in the middle of the sky, a male native marries their mother, their mother's sister or their father's wife, and a female native has intercourse with their son, their sister's son or their daughter's husband. If the sun is in the condition we have mentioned, or the planets are setting, a male native marries their daughter, their brother's daughter or their son's wife, and a female native has intercourse with their father, their father's brother or their mother's husband.
@n «The luminaries» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (النيّرين) for «the two planets». Ptolemy has persons of the same age for Mars, and says that for Saturn it is the houses that are in trine. «Jealousy» reads غيرة for غيره, «others». «More eastern» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (تشريقًا) for تشريفًا, «more honoured», and «someone» the Berlin manuscript (من هو); Ptolemy has younger partners when Venus is further east and older ones when it is further west, and the Arabic has only the older. «Son's wife» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (ابنه) for أبيه, «father's».
@p AB03-F069r-L08..AB03-F069v-L04
If this condition that we have mentioned is not in loving signs — the loving signs are those of one kind, meaning the masculine and the feminine, since each of these is a kind; and it is likelier that what is meant here is the masculine — when, then, it is not in the loving signs mentioned, and is in feminine (?) places, the native is absorbed in intercourse, finding it easy to do it and to have it done to them in every way; and in some figures, when this condition is in them, the native is besides impure in the matter of intercourse. These figures are the fore parts of Leo and Aries; the one called in Greek after its likeness to the letter lambda, the *hyades* (?), which ends in the stars of al-Dabarān; the one called in Greek *kalpis*; and the end of Leo and the face of Capricorn. If this condition is on the first two cardines, I mean the eastern and the southern, the native avows these acts openly and displays them in the gatherings of people; if it is on the other two cardines, I mean the western and the northern, the native is a eunuch, or sterile, or without an opening. If Mars is with that besides, a male native has the genitals cut off, and a female native is a tribade.
@n «The fore parts» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (المقاديم) for معادلة; Ptolemy has the fore and hind parts of Aries. The Hyades, a V like an inverted lambda, are written هداعر; *kalpis*, the Pitcher, is written فالفس. «Avows openly» reads مجاهرًا for مجاهدًا, «striving», and «these acts» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (بأفعاله) for نافعًا له; «is» follows it too (كانت) for {كلفت}.
@p AB03-F069v-L04..AB03-F070r-L04
We should examine the condition of male natives in matters of intercourse from Mars, in this way. When it separates from Venus and Saturn and Jupiter witnesses to it, the native is pure and chaste in matters of intercourse, aiming in it only at what nature needs. If it happens to be with Saturn alone, the native is wary of intercourse, incapable of it and cold in it. If Venus and Jupiter share a configuration with it, the native is quickly moved to it and of strong desire for it, but holds back, keeps guard and avoids foul things. If it is with Venus alone, or Jupiter is with it too without Saturn being with them, the native is greedy and lazy, contriving pleasures from every side. If one of the two planets, I mean Mars and Venus, is attributed to the evenings and the other to the mornings, the native inclines to males and to females alike, not inclining to one of the two kinds more than to the other. If both are attributed to the evenings, the native is absorbed in intercourse with females only, and if the signs are feminine, allows it to be done to them; if both are attributed to the mornings, the native inclines to males only, and if the signs are masculine, is given over to intercourse with males of any stature whatever. If Venus is more western, the native is one who has intercourse with the lowly, or with slave women, or with others foreign in kind, such as Black Africans, Khazars, Byzantines, Turks and the like, or with those who are not kin; if Mars is more western, one who has intercourse with those nobler than themselves, like one who commits adultery with a married woman or with the mistress of the house.
@n «Greedy» reads شرهًا for شرّها. «Stature» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (قامة) for قائمة; Ptolemy has males of any age. The list of peoples is the commentary's own; Ptolemy has foreigners.
@p AB03-F070r-L04..AB03-F070r-L16
In the nativities of women we should look at Venus. When it is allied with Jupiter and Mercury in configuration, the woman is chaste and pure in matters of intercourse; if Saturn is not there, and it is allied with Mercury, the woman is easily moved to intercourse and lustful, but for the most part wary and cautious, avoiding foul things. If Venus is with Mars alone, or shares a configuration with it, the woman is greedy, absorbed in intercourse and eager for it. If Jupiter is with them and Mars is under the rays, the woman has intercourse with slaves, with the lowly and with strangers to the tribe (?); if Venus is under the rays, with nobles or with masters, by way of fornication and debauchery. If these planets are feminine in their places or in their configurations, the woman is also absorbed in intercourse with women. If Saturn is allied with these conditions described and is feminine as well, it is a cause of impurity and filth; and if it is eastern and masculine, the woman is one who looks down from the roofs, or one who falls in love with those who look down from the roofs (?). Jupiter always helps to make these afflictions seemly, and Mercury helps towards their notoriety and their ugliness.
@n «Greedy» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (شرهة) for شهمة, «spirited». Ptolemy has the passive part when these planets are feminine, and intercourse with women when they are masculine; where the Arabic has looking down from the roofs, Ptolemy has objects of censure or lovers of such. «Notoriety» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (شهرتها) for شهوتها, «desire».
