⟨22⟩ On the malformed
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.8.
@p AB03-F041v-L18..AB03-F042r-L11
Not outside this subject is the account of the malformed. We say first that in such cases the luminaries are for the most part found declining, or not joined to the ascendant, and the maleficent planets occupy the cardines. When we find the matter in this condition — since it is something that often happens in lowly nativities of ill fortune, even if those nativities are not of the malformed — we should look at the preceding conjunction and opposition, and find the planet that governs the place of either of them, and those that govern the place of the luminaries at the time of the birth. If their places in the nativity, and the place of the moon or the place of the ascendant, all or most of them, are not joined to the place of the conjunction or the opposition, the child will be of changed form. If these things are so, and we find the luminaries besides in the four-footed signs or in signs of wild beasts, and the planets on the cardines are maleficent, the child will not be of a human being.
@n «Something», «governs» and «those that govern» follow the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* and the Berlin manuscript (شيئًا، المدبّر، مدبّري).
@p AB03-F042r-L11..AB03-F042v-L11
If none of the beneficent planets witnesses to the luminaries, and the maleficent witness to them, the child will be untamed, and its nature one of the harmful wild natures. If either Jupiter or Venus witnesses to the luminaries, the child will be of the animals that are familiar with men, like dogs, ichneumons and the like. If Mercury witnesses, the child will be of the animals that men need and use, like pigs, fowls, cattle, goats and the like. If the luminaries are in signs whose figures have the form of men, and all the other things are likewise, the child will be of ⟨mankind⟩ or of what is near to men, except that it will be malformed and changed. The knowledge of its condition, here especially too, comes from the forms of the signs in which are the maleficent planets that surround the luminaries or the cardines. If here too none of the beneficent planets witnesses to any of the places we have mentioned, the child will be without speech, and its form truly and completely changed. If Jupiter or Venus witnesses, the property of the child that has the defect is that it is honoured and respected: for from this arises the form called Hermaphroditos, which means the form attributed to Mercury and Venus, and the form called Harpocratiac, and the like. If Mercury witnesses too, the child, with these things, will be an interpreter of dreams, and his earnings and resourcefulness will come from these things; but he will be toothless and deaf, though in other things he will be of good nature, a man of learning and of cunning.
@n Ptolemy has dogs and cats among the sacred animals; the Arabic has النموس, ichneumons. The manuscript leaves a space where the editor supplies ⟨mankind⟩ from the Arabic *Tetrabiblos*. The names Hermaphroditos and Harpocratiac are read from the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (ارمامرويطس، ارقوقراطاقوى) for ارماطروديطس and ادفوفراطيافوا; Hermes and Aphrodite are Mercury and Venus. «An interpreter of dreams», «surround» and «of learning and of cunning» follow the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* and the Berlin manuscript (مفسّر الأحلام، المحفوفة، أدب، مكر); «his earnings» is written in the margin. Ptolemy speaks here of prophets who make money by it.
