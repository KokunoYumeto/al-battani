⟨18⟩ On the parents
@n *Tetrabiblos* III.4.
@p AB03-F036v-L20..AB03-F037r-L19
The sun and Saturn are by nature akin to the fathers, and the moon and Venus akin [to the mothers]; and as the condition of these planets is found to be with respect to one another and to the others, so we must suppose the condition of the parents will be. For one should consider their fortune (?) and their possessions from the attendance of the planets on the luminaries. When the beneficent planets of their own domain surround them, those planets being either in the signs of the luminaries themselves or in the signs that follow them, they signify that the affairs of the parents will be splendid and noble; especially when the planets attending the sun are attributed to the mornings and those attending the moon to the evenings, that is, when they rise before the sun and after the moon, so that their position is praiseworthy for that reason. If, besides, Saturn and Venus are eastern, or in the facing proper to them, or on the cardines, we should suppose from that a manifest good fortune of the parents, according to what suits each of them. If the matter is otherwise, and the luminaries proceed alone with no planet attending them, they signify the low station and meanness of the parents, especially when the position of Venus and Saturn is not praiseworthy. If they are attended by maleficent planets not of their domain, as happens when Mars rises after the sun and Saturn after the moon, and the position of the beneficent planets relative to the luminaries is not praiseworthy and they are not of their domain, one should infer from that the meanness of the parents' living and the unsettledness of their condition. If the lot that signifies fortune, which is the lot of good fortune that we shall mention, agrees in the nativity, in a praiseworthy agreement, with the planets attending the sun or the moon, the possessions of the parents remain safe. If the lot is not as we have said and the matter is otherwise, and then none of the beneficent planets attends the luminaries, or those that attend them are maleficent, there is no benefit from the possessions of the parents, and they become harmful to them.
@n «Will be» is written between the lines. «Suits» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (يلائم).
@p AB03-F037r-L19..AB03-F037v-L15
The length and the shortness of the parents' life are considered from the other sharings of the planets in configurations. As for the father's life: if Jupiter and Venus share in a configuration with the sun or with Saturn, in whatever way, or if Saturn too shares with the sun, its sharing with the sun being one of agreement — either being with it, or in its trine, or in its sextile — and both are strong, it signifies long life for the father. If they are not strong, it is not so; but they do not necessarily signify short life. If the matter is not so, and Mars is above the sun or above Saturn, or follows them — that is, it rises after them — and Saturn is not in agreement with the sun but in its quadrature or in opposition to it on the diameter, or both are declining from the cardines, weakness of the fathers comes from them. If they are on the cardines, or where they rise to the cardines, they make the fathers short-lived or bring an injury upon them: shortness of life they bring about if they are on the ascendant or on the middle of the sky, and injury if they are on the two remaining cardines, I mean the setting point and the cardine of the earth, or where they rise. For when Mars looks at the sun in the way that I mentioned at the beginning of the book — that is, the parts that we said look at one another and are equal in power — the father dies suddenly, or an injury to the eyes befalls the father. If Mars's look is towards Saturn, there befalls the father either death, or fever with shivering, or an injury from cutting or burning. Saturn too, when its configuration with the sun is bad, brings about the death of the fathers by diseases and by illnesses that befall them from harmful humours.
@n Robbins takes Ptolemy's «in the way described» as quadrature, opposition or overcoming; al-Battānī refers it to the signs that look at one another and are of equal power (*Tetrabiblos* I.15; chapter 6 above).
@p AB03-F037v-L15..AB03-F038r-L11
As for the mother's life: if Jupiter shares in a configuration with the moon or Venus, in whatever way, or Venus is in agreement with the moon, either being with it, or in its sextile or its trine, and both are strong, they signify long life for the mother. As for Mars, if it looks at the moon, or follows Venus, or is in quadrature or in opposition to it, or Saturn is in this condition with the moon: if they are decreasing in motion, retrograde, or declining from the cardines, they bring upon the mothers only a falling into illnesses and sicknesses; and if they are increasing in motion or on the cardines, they shorten the mothers' lives and bring [injury] upon them. They shorten life when they are on the two eastern cardines or where they rise to them, and bring about injury when they are on the two western cardines or where they come to them. For when Mars looks at the moon in the way we have mentioned, and the moon is eastern, sudden death of the mothers comes of it, or an injury that befalls them in the eyes; if the moon is western, their death comes of it from miscarriage or the like, or an injury from cutting or burning. Instead of the word «western» one may also say «declining». If Mars looks at Venus, their death by fevers comes of it, and illnesses that befall them from darkness of sight and from matter that flows down suddenly. When Saturn looks at the moon, death and illnesses befall them: if the moon is eastern, their death is from fevers with shivering; if it is western, it is from spreading tumours and ulcers in the womb.
@n For «eastern» and «western» of the moon Ptolemy has «waxing» and «waning». «Shivering» reads نافض for ناقص.
@p AB03-F038r-L11..AB03-F038v-L06
In knowing the kinds of the particular injuries and illnesses, or the death that befalls the parents, we should use, besides what we have mentioned, the conditions of the signs in which the planets that bring about the particular condition are found; we shall mention the conditions of the signs and explain them when we speak of the native. And we should examine by day in particular the sun and Venus, and by night in particular Saturn and the moon. What befits us, and is required of us, in examining the remaining particular matters of the parents is to set up the place of the father or of the mother of the [proper] domain in the place of the ascendant, and to consider all their other matters as is done in the nativities of the parents themselves, according to the methods, accidental and active, that are found in the general kinds, which we shall explain later. The ways in which the causes combine with one another we must remember here and in all the places that come later in the matters of nativities: if the planets that are the lords of the places sought are not of one kind but of different kinds, producing opposites, we look which of the planets that are strongest in power, dominion and prevalence we find to have the most shares, so that it has the best claim to what happens; and in examining them we always keep to the natures of these. If the shares are of equal weight, then if the planets are [together] (?), we arrive, with what we have described, at knowledge of what comes together from the mixture of these different natures with ease; and if they are apart, we give each of them the accidents that suit it according to the particular times, giving first to those of them attributed to the evenings.
@n «Of the domain» reads للحيّز for للخير, «of the good»: Ptolemy has «the paternal or maternal place of the sect» (*Tetrabiblos* III.4). «Causes», «lords» and «dominion» follow the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (الأسباب، أرباب، استيلاء) for الأشياء، أرباع، استعلاء; «we arrive» likewise (وصلنا). «Together» is a guess for the transcription's «the high» (العالية): Ptolemy has «when the rulers are together», and gives the events first to the more eastern planets, not to those attributed to the evenings.
@p AB03-F038v-L06..AB03-F039r-L02
Others than Ptolemy have said the like about the indications of the parents, except that they added to it, by night and by day, the lot of the father and of the mother and the sign of the parents, and considered the strength and weakness of all the significators, their positions relative to the cardines, and the lords of these places. If they found them strong, they inferred the excellence of the parents and their good condition; if they found them fallen and weak, they inferred their meanness. In this they also used the lords of the triplicities of the significators, and said: if, with the corruption of the significators, these are afflicted and corrupted with them, they signify chronic illness of the parents. If a beneficent planet is found in the house of the parents, and the lords of the houses and of the terms, and the terms of the luminaries, look at it, they judged the parents to be noble; if the maleficent planets look at the house of the parents from quadrature or opposition, or are in it, and the beneficent do not look at it, they judged that they are slaves or like slaves. If the sign of the parents is the house of the sun, the indication is for the father; if it is in the house of the moon, the indication is for the mother. If you find the lord of the twelfth house with the moon in the thirteenth (?) from the cardine, the native's mother is a slave-woman; if this is the condition of the sun, the native's father is a slave. If the lords of the triplicities of someone's significator are in their own dignities, in praiseworthy places of the sphere, free from the maleficent planets, with the beneficent looking at them, they signify gain and rank in what is to come; especially when the lot of good fortune and its lord are fortunate together with the lot of the parents, for that signifies the continuance of the matter and its inheritance by the sons. If the matter is the opposite, the parents have no benefit from their possessions, and [the significators] signify the fall of their rank.
@n «Significators» reads الأدلّاء for الأولاد, «the children», and «fortunate» مسعودين for منحوس سعودين.
@p AB03-F039r-L02..AB03-F039r-L14
They considered the agreement of the parents with the native and their love for the native. One looks at the planet that prevails over the ascendant and the one that prevails over the matter of the father by the number of shares: if they agree and suit each other, they love one another; if the matter is the opposite, they hate one another. Likewise, if the sun is in the term of Mars by day, or of Saturn by night, especially when the maleficent planets look at the sun and the beneficent do not, it signifies mutual hatred, or the native is deprived (?) of rank and of wealth. If the moon is as I have described of the condition of the sun, the mother meets with affliction from the child. [The parent] whose lot is found in the twelfth [place] from it, the native will be an enemy to that parent. When the sun is in a cardine and Mars looks at it from opposition, the father hates the child; likewise, when the moon is in this condition, the mother hates the child. If Jupiter and Saturn are in opposition and overlook one another, it signifies mutual hatred, and if they are in quadrature likewise; from the sextile and the trine it signifies love. If the lord of the house of the parents does not look at the parents, nor the lord of the sun at it, nor the lord of the lot of the parents at the lot, the native [will have] sorrow (?) and hardship. If the sun is afflicted by day from opposition and quadrature and is in a bad place, the father hates the child and drives the child away (?).
@n «Sorrow» guesses at the transcription's {سعود}; the Berlin manuscript seems to have لغمر.
@p AB03-F039r-L14..AB03-F039v-L04
They said about the life of the parents: if the sun looks at the ascendant by day, it is directed for the life of the father; if Saturn looks at the ascendant, the direction is from it, if the sun is fallen; if Saturn is fallen, the degree of the middle of the sky by day, and by night the degree of the cardine of the earth; and, by night and by day, the lot of the parents. Compare what comes out of the direction with the years of the planet that has the most shares in the place that you direct for the father: if it agrees with them, or is near their number, it signifies the death of the father. Use the like for the significators of the mother. Some of them looked by day and began with the sun for the father: if it is joined to the ascendant, with one of the lords of the shares that follow its place looking at it, they used it for the life of the father; if one did not find it so, one looked at Saturn; if one did not find it in this condition, one looked at the middle of the cardine of the sky, provided that the lot of the father does not have this condition. By night you begin with Saturn, then the sun, then the lot of the father, then the cardine of the earth; and you make the planet that has a share and a look a helper of the significator of the moon (?), according to its years. Use the like for the significators of the mothers.
@n «The death» reads هلاك for هلال, «the new moon».
