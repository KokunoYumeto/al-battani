⟨7⟩ On the masculine and feminine signs, their other conditions and the names given to them
@n *Tetrabiblos* I.12. Ptolemy declines to list the epithets that al-Battānī gives below.
@p AB03-F022r-L10..AB03-F022r-L18
The signs feminine and masculine by nature we have explained in mentioning the triplicities. Some people have used another way of ordering the masculine and feminine signs, from the sign of the ascendant: they counted one masculine and one feminine, and made the beginning from the masculine; just as some people take the solstitial signs beginning from the sign of the moon, because the moon, through the speed of its motion, turns more than the other stars. Some of them divided the signs into four parts and held that the signs attributed to the mornings, the masculine ones, are those from the ascendant to the middle of the sky and those opposite them, from the setting point to the cardine of the earth; and they held that the two remaining quarters are attributed to the evenings and are feminine, so they made the signs that fall in these two quarters feminine and attributed them to the evenings.
@p AB03-F022r-L18..AB03-F022v-L16
They have given the signs many epithets, taken from the figures to which they are attributed: they called some of them four-footed, some wild, some governing, domestic, of much sowing, of little sowing and the like. They said that the domestic signs are Aries and Taurus; the wild, Leo and Sagittarius; the speaking signs, which resemble human beings, Gemini, Aquarius, Libra, Virgo and the first half of Sagittarius; the winged, Virgo, Sagittarius, Gemini and Pisces; the *maʿqūma*, Aries, Taurus, Scorpio, Capricorn and Cancer; those of few offspring and little sowing, Aries, Taurus, Libra, Sagittarius, Capricorn and Aquarius; those of many offspring, Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces; the barren, Gemini, Leo and Virgo; those with mutilated limbs, Aries, Taurus, Leo and Scorpio; those of much anger, Aries and Leo; those that signify beauty and comeliness, Gemini, Libra, Virgo, Scorpio, Sagittarius and Pisces; those that gather and fill, Aries and its triplicity; those that give great wealth, Capricorn and its triplicity; those that pour out and empty, Libra and its triplicity; and those that grasp and take, Cancer and its triplicity. The signs of a strong voice are Gemini, Virgo and Libra; the moderate, which have half a voice, Aries, Taurus, Leo and Sagittarius; those of a weak voice, Capricorn and Aquarius; and those with no voice, Cancer, Pisces and Scorpio. Those that signify great care are Leo, Scorpio and Capricorn; those of little care, Virgo and Libra, for their darkness (?); and those of cunning and deceit, Leo, Sagittarius, Capricorn and Pisces. And they said that Aries, Taurus and Gemini, when their lords are found in them, ⟨are⟩ moving; Cancer, Leo and Virgo, when their lords are found [in them], are at rest; Libra, Scorpio and Sagittarius likewise, when their lords are found in them, are moving; and Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces likewise, when their lords are found in them, are at rest.
@n The meaning of المعقومة, *maʿqūma*, from the root of barrenness, is not clear; the barren signs follow under their usual name, العقيمة.
