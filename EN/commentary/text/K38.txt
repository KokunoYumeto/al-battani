⟨38⟩ [The beginning of a month of the revolution]
@n With this chapter the commentary turns from the *Tetrabiblos* to al-Battānī's own procedures for the months and days of chapter 37; the tables they use follow on ff. 80r–80v. The year of the revolution is treated in chapter 53 of the *Zīj*.
@p AB03-F079r-L07..AB03-F079r-L18
If you wish to know the beginning of the month you want among the months of the revolution, look in the table of the months for the number of complete months that you have, and take what stands under it, the number of days, hours and fractions; add what comes out to the days and hours that have passed from the time of birth to the time of the ascendant of the revolution. Look in the table of the months for the number of complete months that you have, and take what stands under it, the number of days, hours and fractions; add what comes out to the days and hours that have passed of the month of birth, at the time of birth or at the time of the revolution, whichever you wish to work from. Cast what results from that month, taking for each month its number of days in the Roman months with which you work in the revolutions of the years: what remains of the equal days and hours that have passed of the Roman month at which it ends, to which those days belong, is the beginning of the month you wanted among the months of the revolution. Then count from the sign at which that year ends, from the place of each of the haylājs — the ascendant, the sun, the moon, the lot of good fortune and the middle of the sky — in the order of the signs, one sign for each month: the sign at which it ends is the governor of that month, and the place of each of the haylājs in the root nativity is the place of that month in that sign.
@n «If you wish» stands twice. The instruction to look in the table of the months stands twice, the second time with «the month of birth». «Complete» reads التامّة for الثلاثة, «three», as in the repetition. «Count» follows the Berlin manuscript (بعد) for تبعد; «month» follows it (الشهر) for الشكل, «configuration», both times. The Roman months are the Syrian months of the Julian calendar used in the *Zīj*.
