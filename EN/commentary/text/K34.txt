⟨34⟩ On friends, enemies and slaves
@n *Tetrabiblos* IV.7.
@p AB03-F071r-L07..AB03-F071v-L03
The conditions of love and hatred — of which the greater and longer-lasting are called friendship and enmity, and the lesser and shorter-lived acquaintance and estrangement — we should examine in this way. For the great ones, examine the places that have the best claim to rule in both nativities, I mean the nativity of the native and the nativity of the one about whom you wish to know how matters will stand between the two in friendship and enmity: the place of the sun, the place of the moon, the place of the ascendant and the place of the lot of good fortune. If these are in one and the same sign in the two nativities, or in exchanged places, all of them or most of them — especially when the two ascendants are about seventeen degrees apart — a firm friendship comes of them that does not dissolve or grow troubled. If they are in disjunct places, or in places opposite on the diameter, there arise from them severe enmity and opposition of long duration. «Exchange» means that the sun of one of the two natives is in the place of the other's moon and the moon in the place of the sun, or that this condition holds in the lot of good fortune and in the ascendant with them. If it is in neither of the two ways we have mentioned, but they are only in signs that share in configurations, then if they are in those that share in trine or sextile, the friendship is less than the first, and if in those that share in quadrature, the enmity is less; so that at times, in friendship, speech is broken off between the two friends and their converse grows scant, which happens when the maleficent planets pass the place of the configuration; and in enmity there come peace and reconciliation when the beneficent planets come to the places of the configurations.
@n «Or most of them» reads أو for وأمّا; «the two ascendants are apart» reads [ما بين] الطالعين for من الطالعين.
@p AB03-F071v-L03..AB03-F071v-L18
For the kinds of love and hatred are three: those who are in these two conditions come to them through inclination and opinion, or because of benefit or harm, or because of pleasure or pain. When all or some of these places mentioned are familiar with one another, the love is compounded of these kinds, and when they are not familiar with one another, enmity comes of them. If the familiarity is only between the places of the two luminaries, the love is by choice and opinion — this is the excellent and trustworthy affection — and the enmity is of the utmost badness and does not dissolve. Likewise, when the familiarity or its opposite is between the two places of the lot of good fortune ⟨…⟩ what comes of it is because of pleasure or pain. We should also examine the places in which the configurations are shared, and see what overpowers them and which of the planets see them. In the nativities in which the planets that overpower the places of the shared configuration are in one and the same sign, approximately, or in the one that rises and moves to it, the friendship and the enmity in their strength, steadiness and soundness should be attributed to those planets; and in the nativities in which the regard of the planets is excellent and useful for doing good and for strength, the benefit of the friendship and the good outcome of the enmity should be attributed to the planets that see.
@n «Inclination» reads الهوى for الهواء, «air»; «pain» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (غمّ) for «something else», and «not familiar» the Berlin manuscript (لا يلائم) for {لا يلادم}. «Does not dissolve» reads ولا تنحلّ for ولم ينحل. A line seems to have been skipped after «the lot of good fortune» (PAL, comparing the Arabic *Tetrabiblos*): Ptolemy assigns friendship through need to the lots of fortune, and through pleasure or pain to the ascendants.
@p AB03-F071v-L18..AB03-F072r-L20
As for acquaintance and estrangement, which last a time at some periods and then end, we should look for them at the planets in each of the two nativities, I mean at what times the direction of the planets of one of the two natives reaches the places of the planets of the other. According to that there may be partial love and hatred, lasting at least until the end of that [direction] and at most until another of the counted planets catches up. When one of Saturn and Jupiter passes the place of the other, they produce friendship through farming or inheritance; when one of Saturn and Mars passes the place of the other, it produces quarrel and disturbance by choice; when one of Saturn and Venus passes the other, they produce connection and familiarity through kin, but it ends quickly. Saturn and Mercury produce partnership in livelihood, dealings, giving and taking, trade and secrets; Jupiter and Mars, companionship in matters of favour and of management; Jupiter and Venus, friendship through women, through worship and the temples, through revelation and the like; Jupiter and Mercury, companionship through logic, the sciences and philosophy, by choice. Mars and Venus produce connection through passion, debauchery and fornication, but it is not to be trusted and does not last long; Mars and Mercury produce enmity, desire (?) and quarrel through charms or sorcery; and Venus and Mercury produce partnership in livelihood through the art of music, through letters or through women. The increase and decrease in the strength of acquaintance and estrangement should be known from the condition of the places taken, when they are compared with the first four chief places: when they are on the cardines, or with the lot of good fortune, or with the luminaries, what they signify is more evident and clearer, and when they are far from them, it is not evident. Their harm and their benefit are known from the particular condition, for good and for evil, of the planets that see the places mentioned before.
@n «Direction» stands for الهيلاج, the haylāj; Ptolemy has the prorogations of the planets. «Hatred» reads وبغض for وبعض, «and some»; «passes» reads مرّ for أمر, «commanded»; «the place» follows the Berlin manuscript (بموضع) for بمكان. «Passion» reads العشق for العنق, «the neck». Ptolemy has noisy disputes and lawsuits for Mars and Mercury. «Taken» follows the Arabic *Tetrabiblos* (تؤخذ) for توجد, «found».

The account of slaves, and of their masters' love or hatred for them, is known in particular from the signs in the place of misery, which is the twelfth from the ascendant, and from the natural agreement of the planets that see this place at the time of the birth itself, at the time when they come to it, or at the opposition; especially when the planets that dominate the signs either agree with the chief places of the masters' nativities by sharing a configuration with them, or disagree with them in that.
@n «Hatred» reads بغضهم for بعضهم, «some of them».
