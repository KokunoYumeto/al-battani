In the Escorial codex the tables follow the chapters. Of them Nallino printed in Part III only those that contain words, the chronological and the geographical tables and the names of the fixed stars, as the codex has them; the tables of numbers only are in his Latin version (Part II). The English below translates the words of these tables and gives their numbers in figures, read from the abjad letters with their Western values. Where Nallino corrects a number of the codex in Part II, the English gives his value, marked *, and the codex's reading in the notes; where Part II differs from Part III without a note of his, the English keeps the value of Part III, marked †, and gives that of Part II in the notes. In the star catalogue the longitudes are counted in degrees from the beginning of the Ram through the whole circle of the signs, and N or S gives the side of the ecliptic on which the latitude lies; a magnitude «greater» or «lesser» is a little brighter or fainter than its number. The column «Star» gives Nallino's identification in the modern designation. Where his translation corrects the wording of the Arabic, the English follows it and the notes give the Arabic. Names of persons and places are transliterated as they are written, with Nallino's reading of them beside them. The pages of Part III are given in the margin.
