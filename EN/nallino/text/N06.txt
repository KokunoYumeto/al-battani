p. 59, n. 3 (*Terrae in K et M tangant*): The ends of the diameters AC, KM, EG are not the points of contact of the tangents; for these cannot be perpendicular to the diameters. In fact, however, the difference becomes insensible because of the smallness of the angles ENG, MSK. Ptolemy, *Almag.* V, 15 (ed. Halma, vol. I, p. 344), therefore says more accurately that the straight lines passing through the points of contact are «equal to the diameters *to the senses* (πρὸς αἴσθησιν)».
