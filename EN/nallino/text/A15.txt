@p 0278b016..0278b017
To find the *meridian altitude* of the sun from the geographical latitude of the given city and the declination of the sun.
@p 0278b018..0278b024
Al-Battānī gives the very well known formulas in two ways; when the sun is in the northern signs of the zodiac,
\[ [[(α)]] \text{merid. alt.} = 90^\circ - (\text{latit.} - \text{declin.}) \]
\[ [[(α′)]] \text{merid. alt.} = \text{complement of the latit.} + \text{declin.}; \]
when the sun is in the southern signs,
\[ [[(β)]] \text{merid. alt.} = 90^\circ - (\text{latit.} + \text{declin.}) \]
\[ [[(β′)]] \text{merid. alt.} = \text{complement of the latit.} - \text{declin.} \]
