On pp. 57–58: the tables of the declination of the sun
@p 0670b008
They are mentioned in vol. I, p. 13, ch. IV.
@p 0670b009..0670b014
The tables of Ptolemy, I, 12 (ed. Halma, vol. I, p. 59), and of Theon are computed for the obliquity 23° 51′ 20″; so Schiaparelli could correct al-Battānī's tables only by computations made with our logarithmic tables, neglecting discrepancies of 1″ or 2″. With logarithms it is enough to compute the declination for the longitude 1° ($\sin\delta = \sin L \sin\varepsilon$); the other terms are derived by adding to the first logarithm, one after another, the logarithmic differences of the sines for the single degrees; see M. Delambre's *notes* in Halma, vol. I, notes pp. 5–6.
@p 0670b015..0670b019
**P. 58**, *the orders of the declination*. The small table is mentioned at the end of appendix G (vol. I, p. 150); on these orders see ch. IV, vol. I, p. 13, and also my notes, vol. I, p. 13, note 2, and p. LXXI. In the figure of the codex the words *ascent* and *descent* are wrongly interchanged, so that near both sides of the circle the copyist wrote منكوس «to be inverted»; the Spanish version (f. 43v) has the same errors, and renders the word *mankūs* by «conuerso» (turned round).
@p 0670b020..0670b025
**P. 58**, *the right ascensions of the tens of degrees and the corresponding sines*. They are mentioned in appendix G, vol. I, p. 150. The ascensions are counted from the beginning of Aries, not from the beginning of Capricorn as in the tables of vol. II, pp. 61–64 (see the note in vol. I, pp. 163–64, and the Addenda). In the heading of the third column the codex wrongly has ازمان ..... المنصّفة «halved times» instead of اوتار ..... المنصّفة «halved chords», that is, sines. — Ptolemy too, II, 8 (ed. Halma, vol. I, p. 103), gives a table of the right ascensions for the tens of degrees, but without the sines and for another obliquity.
