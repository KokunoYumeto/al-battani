{ar:سهم} — {ar:سَهْم دائرة الشمس} *the sagitta of the solar disc*, that is, the sagitta of the eclipsed part of the disc, 164$_{21,\,22}$; {ar:سهم دائرة القمر} *the sagitta of the lunar disc*, 165$_{2}$; {ar:سهم الظِّلّ} *the sagitta of the shadow* in eclipses, 150 several times. See I, 276. — {ar:سَهْمُ السَّعَادَة} ὁ κλῆρος τῆς Τύχης, *the Part of Fortune*; see I, 314, n. 1, where I gave the true definition, such as is gathered from the doctrines of Ptolemy[^787.2] and of the greater part of the Arab astrologers. They do not distinguish diurnal nativities from nocturnal ones. Nor do the precepts of Nechepso and Petosiris differ from Ptolemy's method, although they lay down that the Part of Fortune is to be found in two ways, a diurnal and a nocturnal. For by day they add the interval from ⊙ to ☾, taken in the order of the signs, to the ascendant «in the *left* direction» (that is, in the order of the signs); by night they add the interval from ☾ to ⊙, that is, the complement of the diurnal interval to 360°, to the ascendant «in the *right* direction». What is gathered by either way is always one and the same, and agrees with Ptolemy's method. Vettius Valens, however, whom Firmicus sometimes follows, bids that the addition always be made in the left direction; for which reason the diurnal place of the Part of Fortune differs from the nocturnal[^788.1]. The definition of al-Tahānawī, *Dictionary*, p. 698 (Constantinople ed. 772), unless it is mutilated, follows the opinion of Vettius Valens: «*By day* they take [the interval of longitude] from ⊙ to the degree of ☾ and add to it the degree of the ascendant; then they cast off the sum by thirty degrees from the ascendant, and where the subtraction ends, there will be the place of the Part of Fortune. *By night* they take [the interval] from ☾ to the degree of ⊙ and add to it the degree of the ascendant». A diurnal example follows: let the ascendant be in 10° of Aries, ⊙ in 20° of Leo, ☾ in 15° of Libra; then ⊙ − ☾ = 55°, to which the 10° of the ascendant are to be added. They make 65°. When 30° are given to Aries and 30° to Taurus, 5° are left for Gemini; the place of the Part of Fortune will therefore be in 5° of Gemini.
