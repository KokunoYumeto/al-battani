The first circle [serves] for the *terms*; the one that follows it, for the *triplicities*; the one that follows both, for the *faces*; the one that follows those three, for the *domiciles*[^748.3]; the one that follows them all, and is the innermost, for the *exaltations*[^748.4]. Nor is [this figure] from the archetype.[^n]
@n In the figure the print has «Inpiter» (in the terms of Gemini) and «Iupitor» (Cancer) for Iupiter, and «Verus» (Virgo) and «Venns» (Pisces) for Venus; the English figure gives the names of the planets. The exaltation of Capricorn is printed «♂ 28», without the sign of degrees.
