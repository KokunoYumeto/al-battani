@h 0808b001
Index geographicus (index of places)[^n]
@n The headwords stand as printed, in Nallino's Latin and his transcriptions, and in his order; where a Latin headword is not the English name, the English follows in square brackets. Cross-references keep the headword as printed. Italic numbers are the places of al-Battānī's text, upright numbers those of Nallino's notes; numbers after II are pages of Part II, the others of Part I.
@p 0808b002
The numbers printed in italic type indicate the places of al-Battānī, the others my notes.
@p 0808b003..0808b004
The abbreviations are these: lin. = line. n. = note, at the foot of the pages. nr. = number. add. = with the additions. add. t. II = with the additions placed at the beginning of vol. II. emend. = with the corrections.
@p 0808b005..0808b007
Long after the tables were printed there appeared the excellent book of G. Le Strange, *The lands of the Eastern Caliphate, Mesopotamia, Persia, and Central Asia from the Moslem conquest to the time of Timur*, Cambridge 1905, which can now be consulted for a good many of the names.
