To find the *geographical latitude* φ of a given place on the earth from the meridian altitude of the sun.
@p 0278b008..0278b013
The rules are very well known; when the sun (⊙) is north of the celestial equator,
\[ [[(α)]] \varphi = 90^\circ - (\text{merid. alt. of }\odot - \text{declin. of }\odot); \]
when the sun is in the southern signs of the zodiac,
\[ [[(β)]] \varphi = 90^\circ - (\text{merid. alt. of }\odot + \text{declin. of }\odot). \]
We write them briefly
\[ \varphi = \text{complement of the merid. alt.} \pm \text{declin.} \]
