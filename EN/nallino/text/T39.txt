On p. 303: the signs of the Copts
@p 0765b018..0765b024
What this Coptic era is, provided with fixed or Julian years, is not quite clear. From the sign of the first month, Thoth (*Tūt*), it appears that the beginning of the era was a sixth weekday, or Friday; this fits both the era of Augustus mentioned by al-Battānī (vol. I, pp. 244–45) and the era of Diocletian, still in force among the Copts today, whose epoch is Friday, 29 August AD 284. It is likely, however, that the author (Maslama?) had in mind the era of *Diocletian*, because this is the one indicated in the following table. Nor must it be forgotten that the era of Augustus was of almost no use among the Arabs (vol. I, p. 245).
