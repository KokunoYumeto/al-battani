I have easily corrected the errors of the codex (74 in 180 numbers of minutes and seconds!) by comparing al-Battānī's sines with the chords of the *Almagest* (I, 9, ed. Halma; vol. I, pp. 38–45), as already corrected by Delambre (see ed. Halma, notes, p. 2). To make the correction more certain I also used the table of sines computed for every 15′ of arc by Aboul Hhassan, pp. 103–114; wherever any doubt arose, I did the computations again with our logarithmic tables. — Everything agrees very well with Ptolemy; there are occasional discrepancies of +1″ from Aboul Hhassan, in whose table, among the sines of the whole and half degrees (which alone I examined), the following must be corrected:
