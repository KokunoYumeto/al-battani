@h 0822b001
Index historicus et rerum (index of persons and subjects)[^n]
@n As in the index of places, the headwords stand as printed, in Nallino's order; where a Latin headword is not the English name, the English follows in square brackets.
@p 0822b002..0822b005
The names of the kings and caliphs that the tables II, 1-6 contain are left out, except those discussed in the notes. Likewise the pages of the glossary on which the technical words are explained are almost never given. The initials of first names are those used in the men's own native language; so Iohannes is designated by the letter *G.* (Giovanni) if the man is an Italian, by the letter *J.* (John) if he is an Englishman, etc.
@p 0822b006..0822b009
To the abbreviations already given on p. 359 these are to be added: adn. = the longer notes at the end of the translation of the chapters or of the tables. b. = ibn (that is, son). em. = corrected, correction. emend. = to be corrected according to ... l. = read. Muḥ. = Muḥammad. plur. = several times. v. = see. Vh. = the Spanish version.[^n]
@n In this translation adn., plur., v. and l. (read) are written out as «note», «several times», «see» and «read»; the other abbreviations are kept.
