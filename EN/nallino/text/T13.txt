@p 0670b032..0670b035
They are cotangents computed to a radius of 12 parts; with logarithmic tables I could easily heal the errors of the codex. The first two shadows are 687ᵖ 26′ and 663ᵖ 39′ in the codex (خصج[^n] for سمج 343ᵖ); but perhaps these small discrepancies in the minutes come from al-Battānī himself, who may have derived those minutes by dividing sines computed only to the seconds of a radius of 60 parts.
@n The print has حصج, the first letter without its dot. In the western numerals of the codex خصج is 663 (خ 600, ص 60, ج 3) and سمج is 343.
