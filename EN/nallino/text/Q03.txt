How the tables were constructed I have set out very fully in the notes. I should often have been briefer had I found among our scholars the intricate doctrines of the Almagest clearly told and the constructions of Ptolemy's tables rightly explained; but in this matter even Delambre is sometimes of no help.
@p 0427b010..0427b013
This volume was in the press for a very long time; the greatest part of the tables had already been printed in 1902. Hence it came about that the addenda turned out fuller than they should be; in them, moreover, I had to set out some things that would have had a less suitable place in the notes themselves.
