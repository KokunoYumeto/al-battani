*Taqiyūs* in the older Arab writers (al-Yaʿqūbī, al-Muqaddasī, Yāqūt, the author of the book *al-bayān al-mughrib*, al-Edrisī, Ibn Khaldūn; yet *Daqyūs* in ʿAbd al-Wāḥid al-Marrākushī); today *Daqāsh*, or, as it is commonly pronounced, *Dgāsh* (on French maps *Deguèche*), about 2 km from the northern shore of the Shaṭṭ al-Jerīd, 11 km NE of the town of Tūzer, 8 km ESE of al-Ḥāmma.
