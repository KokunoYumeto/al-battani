On pp. 4–6: the table of the caliphs
@p 0646b019..0646b036
The same table[^646.8], carried down to the death of al-Muṭīʿ (336 H), is in al-Masʿūdī, *Prairies*, vol. IX, pp. 38–49, who acknowledges that he followed al-Battānī above all; the same, carried down to the death of al-Muktafī (334 H), but with the sums of the years left out and not without errors, is also in Ḥamza, pp. 156–58 of the text. It is worth quoting some things from al-Masʿūdī, loc. cit., pp. 38–39: «In an earlier part of this book we had already set up a special chapter for the chronology of the kings … down to this time, derived from computation and from what is told in the books of biographies and histories and of those who have devoted themselves to the history of the caliphs and kings. But in that place we had not set out what the astronomers (*aṣḥāb al-nujūm*) tell in the astronomical works (*fī kutub al-zījāt*), relying on their own reckoning of the times. In this chapter, then, we shall record everything that they have laid down in the astronomical works from the hijra to our time, so that the usefulness of the book may be increased and the discrepancy between the historians and the writers on heavenly things may be known». Then, pp. 48–49: «Between this and the chronology of the historians and biographers there is a discrepancy (*tafāwut*) because of the increments (*ziyādāt*) of months and days. In setting out the chronology from the hijra to this time we have used what we found in the astronomical works; for those who devote themselves to this [heavenly] art keep those times accurately (*yurāʿūn*) and attain a knowledge of them most exactly defined (*ʿalā l-taḥdīd*). What we have reported we have taken from the astronomical work of Abū ʿAbd Allāh Muḥammad ibn Jābir al-Battānī[^646.9] and from other astronomical works». — On the chronological tables see also al-Masʿūdī, *al-Tanbīh*, p. 329.

@p 0647b001..0647b002
The years and months of this table are lunar, according to the Muslim calendar. Of the hijra fixed on a Thursday I shall speak in the note on the table of p. 7.
@p 0647b003..0647b006
The name «*just caliphs*» I have kept in the headings, as I had found it in the codex. But mostly only the first four caliphs are so called; and it is not impossible that the name, which al-Battānī put before those four only, was given to all the others too through an error of the copyists.
@p 0647b007..0647b008
The *Banū Hāshim*, mentioned at the beginning of p. 5, are the tribe or family from which the ʿAbbāsid caliphs descended.

@p 0647b010..0647b013
All those who are named after *al-Muktafī* (printed in italic, or sloping, letters), since they lived later than al-Battānī, were plainly added by some reader, who in the sum of the years of *al-Muqtadir* wrongly put 18 days instead of 11, and so vitiated the remaining sums too. Nor are the discrepancies from al-Masʿūdī small in these interpolated entries.
