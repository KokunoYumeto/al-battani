On pp. 102–106: the mean motions of the planets for the Roman calendar
@p 0687b002..0687b006
The Roman year here is the *fictitious* year counted from 1 Ādhār (March) of the chronological year already begun, of which I spoke above, on p. 227. Why the mean motion of the anomaly has been left out for the superior planets and the mean motion in longitude for the inferior ones I have already explained on p. 204. — All the motions of these tables differ a little from Ptolemy's[^687.1] over long intervals of time; see vol. I, pp. 239 and 242.
