@p 0203nl003..0203nl009
p. 114, n. 2 (*planetarum in epicyclo*): So the codex and Plato; but he undoubtedly means the apogees of the planets on the eccentric. The apogee of a planet on the epicycle occurs whenever the anomaly becomes zero, and that can happen at any place of the ecliptic. The greatest distance on the epicycle must therefore be understood as that of the apogee, that is, the longitude of that apogee of the epicycle which is had when the centre of the epicycle is at the apogee of the eccentric.
