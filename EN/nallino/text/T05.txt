Two pages of the codex (ff. 163v and 164r), which carry the concordance down to the year 720, I thought should be left out; for they are without doubt *spurious*. They swarm with mistakes, which arise not from the carelessness of the copyists but from evident errors of computation, as happens whenever an inept interpolator tries to add something to al-Battānī. To carry the concordance down to 600 H, that is, for more than 300 years from the epoch of the tables, was enough for al-Battānī. — In the Spanish version, ff. 30r–35v, these tables are found unfinished; for in the right-hand section of f. 35v the years of the hijra are written down to 488 (the last 7 lines being left empty), but the names of the first day of al-Muḥarram and the years of Dhū l-Qarnayn (the last of which is 1492) only down to the line of the year 477 H, and the days and Roman months only down to the line of the year 470 H. I think the copyist did not finish the table because on this page he had noticed that he had wrongly written 400 for 500 everywhere in the hundreds of the years of the hijra, and had moreover put, in place of the omitted sign of al-Muḥarram of the year 452 (read 552), the sign of the year 453 (read 553), and had set the following signs out of their places.
