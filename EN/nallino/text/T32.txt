@p 0744b005..0744b007
By «whole years» (*sinūn tāmma*) he means not the Roman, or Julian, years of the calendar but the *tropical* years, which according to al-Battānī's observations comprise 365ᵈ 5ʰ 46ᵐ 24ˢ. The name «of the Romans» seems to me an inept interpolation.
@p 0744b008..0744b012
The small table of p. 188 gives the angle that the sun completes with its daily motion about the earth in a tropical year beyond 365 whole daily revolutions, that is, the revolution of the sphere in those 5ʰ 46ᵐ 24ˢ by which the tropical year exceeds 365 whole days. These 5ʰ 46ᵐ 24ˢ, converted into degrees («times of ascensions»), make 86° 36′, which are found written under year 1. So the small table serves to compute the amount of the daily motion for any number of tropical years.
