@p 0653b029..0653b030
The columns of p. 24 are headed in the Arabic as on pp. 25–26; somewhat differently in the translation, as I pointed out in the note on pp. 19–23. — Pp. 27 and 28 necessarily do not differ from pp. 106 and 105.
@p 0653b031..0653b036
In his tables Ptolemy, IX, 4 (ed. Halma, vol. II, pp. 126–55), adds the anomalies of the superior planets and the mean motions in longitude of the inferior ones. These al-Battānī leaves out, rightly observing, vol. I, p. 113 (ch. XLV), that in the Ptolemaic system the mean motion of the anomaly of the three superior planets is the same as the mean motion in longitude of the sun lessened by the mean motion of the anomaly[^n] of these planets themselves, and that the mean motion in longitude of Venus and Mercury does not differ from the mean motion of the sun.
@n So the print; in the Ptolemaic system the mean motion in anomaly of a superior planet is the mean motion of the sun less the planet's mean motion in longitude.
