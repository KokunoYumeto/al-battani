On pp. 65–66: the oblique ascensions for the single climates
@p 0671b008
The tables are mentioned in chs. XIII and XXXV, vol. I, pp. 27–28, 29 and 73.
@p 0671b009..0671b015
They are counted from the beginning of *Aries*, not from Capricorn like the right ascensions. They differ from *Ptolemy's*, II, 8 (ed. Halma, vol. I, pp. 104–108), which are also computed for the tens of degrees, because of the different obliquity of the ecliptic and hence the different latitudes of the climates. — There is a certain proportion in the increase of the ascensions answering to an interval of a quarter of an hour in the length of the day, so that it was not hard to heal the errors of the codex. In some places where there was doubt I made the computations with al-Battānī's tables of the declinations and of the right ascensions and with the formula $\sin m = \tan\varphi \tan\delta$.
