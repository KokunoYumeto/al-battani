On p. 300: the signs of the years and months of the Arabs
@p 0763b008..0763b016
The table covers only the first 210 years, because after 210 years the signs repeat in their former order; see vol. I, p. 323. In constructing this table Maslama used a device different from al-Battānī's: he deliberately diminished by one the signs of the years, both single and collected, and increased by one the signs of the months. So one must proceed thus: if you seek the beginning of any month of any year of the hijra, add the sign of the month to the sign of the year; but if you seek the beginning of the year, add to its sign the sign of the first month (al-Muḥarram), that is, 1. — To give an example, I seek the beginning of the year 680. 680 = (210 × 3) + 50; so I enter the table with 50, to which the sign 1 answers. When 1, the sign of al-Muḥarram, is added, 2 results; therefore the beginning of the year 680 was a second weekday, or Monday.

It would have been simpler, since Maslama has a year 0, to take nothing from the signs of the years and to add nothing to the signs of the months. — A table equal to this one stands at the beginning of the Spanish version of the tables of al-Zarqālī (*Libro de las tablas del Zarquiel*), f. 94r of the same codex that contains the Spanish version of al-Battānī.
