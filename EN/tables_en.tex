\clearpage\pdfbookmark[0]{The tables of Part III}{tables}{\Large\bfseries The tables of Part III\par}\medskip
In the Escorial codex the tables follow the chapters. Of them Nallino printed in Part III only those that contain words, the chronological and the geographical tables and the names of the fixed stars, as the codex has them; the tables of numbers only are in his Latin version (Part II). The English below translates the words of these tables and gives their numbers in figures, read from the abjad letters with their Western values. Where Nallino corrects a number of the codex in Part II, the English gives his value, marked *, and the codex's reading in the notes; where Part II differs from Part III without a note of his, the English keeps the value of Part III, marked †, and gives that of Part II in the notes. Names of persons and places are transliterated as they are written, with Nallino's reading of them beside them. The pages of Part III are given in the margin.
\par\needspace{8\baselineskip}\bigskip\pdfbookmark[1]{Notice}{tab-notice}{\large\bfseries Notice\par}\ArPage{227}\nobreak\medskip
Carlo Nallino, who has taken care of establishing and correcting the text of this book, says: All the chapters are complete, and in the Escorial copy the tables follow them. We, however, have taken from them only what concerns chronology, geography and the names of the fixed stars, and have printed it without correcting the errors and the corruptions in the abjad letters — and, by God, how many these mistakes are! Whoever wishes to correct them should consult our Latin translation of this book, in which we have also printed the rest of the tables, which contain numbers only. The reader must also be told that the abjad letters have the same values in the tables as in the Escorial copy, that is, by the usage of the people of the West: so Ṣ stands for 60, Ḍ for 90, S for 300 and Ẓ for 800.\par{\centering End, end.\par}\par{\centering End.\par}
