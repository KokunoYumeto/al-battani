Chapter 37. On knowing the position of the northern node, which is called the head of the jawzahar
@n *Jawzahar*, from the Persian *gawzahr*, means the orbit of the moon, its nodes, and above all its ascending node (Nallino).
@p AB01-PDF1039-L03..AB01-PDF1039-L06
He said: If you wish to know the position of the northern node, which is called the head, find its mean position for the time sought in the way given for finding the mean positions, and always subtract the result from 360 degrees: the remainder is the position of the northern node on the ecliptic. Cast it from the beginning of Aries in the usual way. As for the position of the southern node, which is called the tail, it lies on the diameter of its sphere: it is the degree opposite the degree of the head, degree for degree, and does not depart from it.
@n The nodes move backwards, against the order of the signs, so the motion taken from the tables is subtracted from 360 (Nallino).
