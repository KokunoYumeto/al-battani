Chapter 23. On finding the azimuth of any star from its altitude and its place in the sphere
@p AB01-PDF1099-L17..AB01-PDF1098-L02
Al-Battānī said: If you wish to find the azimuth of any star you like on the horizon circle, find the altitude of the star at that time, the distance of the star from the equator and the latitude of the locality. Then proceed with them in the way that I described to you in the chapter on finding the azimuth of the altitude and of the shadow, leaving out nothing, except that you use the distance of the star from the equator in place of the declination of the degree of the sun. If you wish to find the azimuth of the moon, do this with its visible distance from the equator, so that you have the azimuth of the moon on the circle of altitude correct; and likewise for every star whose azimuth on the horizon circle you wish to find, if God wills.
