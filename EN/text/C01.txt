Chapter 1. The opening of the book
@p AB01-PDF1146-L03..AB01-PDF1146-L04
Al-Battānī said: The first thing with which every matter is begun and every discourse is opened is the praise of God, exalted be God's mention, thanks to God for the divine benefits, and the blessing on the Seal of God's messengers and prophets — upon them be peace, and the mercy and blessings of God.
@n The Seal of the messengers and prophets is Muḥammad (cf. Qurʾān 33:40).
@p AB01-PDF1146-L04..AB01-PDF1146-L07
Praise be to God, who created the creatures by divine power, ordered all things by divine will and perfected them by divine wisdom; who «encompasses all things in knowledge» and «has counted all things in number»; from whom «not the weight of an atom in the heavens or on the earth escapes, nor anything smaller than that or greater, but it is in a clear book».
@n Qurʾān 65:12, 72:28 and 34:3. The «clear book» is the book that is with God.
@p AB01-PDF1146-L07..AB01-PDF1146-L10
I bear witness that there is no god but God alone, who has no partner, and I bear witness that Muḥammad is God's servant and messenger, whom God «sent with the guidance and the religion of truth, to make it prevail over all religion, though the idolaters dislike it». Through the Prophet God guided the believers and «cut off the last of the unbelievers», and made the Prophet a proof for all the worlds. God bless the Prophet, the Prophet's good family, the chosen companions and those who follow the Prophet's practice, until the Day of Judgement.
@n Qurʾān 9:33 and 61:9 (Nallino's note has XLI, 9, a misprint for LXI, 9); for «cut off the last of the unbelievers», cf. 7:72 and 8:7.
@p AB01-PDF1146-L10..AB01-PDF1146-L16
To proceed. After the knowledge of the laws and the practice of religion, of which no one may be ignorant, the science of the art of the stars is among the sciences that are highest in rank and most exalted in standing, finest in adornment, that cling most to the heart and shine most in the soul, and that do most to sharpen thought and reflection, to quicken understanding and to train the mind. For there is a great share of good in it and great benefit: the knowledge of the length of the years and months, of the appointed times, of the seasons, of the lengthening and shortening of the day and the night, of the places of the two luminaries and their eclipses, of the courses of the stars in their direct and retrograde motion, of the changes in their configurations, of the order of their spheres and of all their other relations; and beyond this, what is attained through it by one who examines it closely and reflects on it constantly: the proof of the unity of God, and the knowledge of the essence of the Creator's greatness, of the breadth of divine wisdom, the majesty of divine power and the subtlety of divine handiwork.
@n The configurations of the stars are the nine configurations with the sun that chapter 49 explains (Nallino).
@p AB01-PDF1146-L16..AB01-PDF1145-L01
God has said — mighty is the Speaker: «In the creation of the heavens and the earth, and in the alternation of night and day, there are signs for those who have understanding.» God, blessed and exalted, has said: «Blessed is the One who has placed signs in the sky.» God, mighty and majestic, has said: «It is the One who has made the night and the day to follow one another.» God, to whom be glory, has said: «It is the One who has made the sun a radiance and the moon a light, and has measured out its stations, so that you may know the number of the years and the reckoning.» And God, whose mention be exalted, has said: «The sun and the moon run by a reckoning.» There is much more of this kind in the book of God, mighty and majestic, which it would take long to describe and much space to mention and cite.
@n Qurʾān 3:190, 25:61, 25:62, 10:5 and 55:5. The signs (*burūj*) of 25:61 are the word that al-Battānī uses for the signs of the zodiac throughout the book. In the third quotation the text prints «the night and the moon a light, in succession», a slip that mixes this verse with the next quotation; the Qurʾān (25:62) has «the night and the day», which Nallino translates and the translation follows.
@p AB01-PDF1145-L01..AB01-PDF1145-L11
When I had studied this science at length and reflected on it constantly, I came to know how the books composed on the motions of the stars disagree, and into what errors some of their authors fell in the procedures they set down in them as foundations and in what they built on these. I also came to know what has accumulated in the motions of the stars over long periods of time, when their observations are compared with the ancient observations; that the inclination of the ecliptic to the equator has been found to be smaller; and what has changed with this change in the various kinds of computation, in the lengths of the years, in the times of the seasons, and in the conjunctions of the two luminaries that are inferred from the times and hours of the eclipses. So I set about correcting all this and making it exact according to the method of Ptolemy in the book known as the Almagest, after close study, long reflection and consideration, following in Ptolemy's footsteps and keeping to what was laid down there. For Ptolemy had investigated these matters from every side and had shown their causes and the reasons that occur in them by a geometrical and numerical proof whose soundness cannot be rejected and whose truth cannot be doubted. After that, Ptolemy ordered that they be tested and examined by those who came after, and said that Ptolemy's own observations might be improved upon in the course of time, just as Ptolemy had improved upon Hipparchus and other peers; for the art is a lofty one, and, being heavenly and vast, it can be grasped only approximately.
@n «Has been found to be smaller» renders *mā wujida … min al-taqārub*, literally «the drawing together that was found»: al-Battānī's observations gave a smaller obliquity of the ecliptic than the Greek value (chapter 4). Nallino stresses that the words do not show knowledge of a continuous decrease of the obliquity. For Ptolemy's remark see the Almagest, III 1 (in Heiberg's numbering; Nallino cites Halma's III, 2).
@p AB01-PDF1145-L11..AB01-PDF1145-L17
I have composed a book on this, in which I have made clear what was obscure, opened what was closed and explained what was difficult in the principles of this science and out of the way in its branches. With it I have made the path of guidance easy for those who follow it and work by it in the art of the stars. In it I have corrected the motions of the stars and their places on the belt of the ecliptic, as I found them by observation and by the computation of the two kinds of eclipse, together with all the other procedures that are needed, and I have added other things that are needed. I have arranged that the motions of the stars are found from the tables for noon of the day for which the computation is made, at the city of al-Raqqa, where the observation and the testing that establish all this took place — if God, the Exalted, wills. Success comes from God.
