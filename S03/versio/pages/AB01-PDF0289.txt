@obs body L1-L3: the method is right: in the triangle ♈HC, right-angled at H, with the angle ε at the first point of Aries, tg ♈H = tg ♈C cos ε and sin HC = sin ♈C sin ε; so ♈H, entered as a right ascension in the table of right ascensions, gives ♈C as the corresponding arc of the ecliptic, and the declination of that arc in the table of the declination of the Sun is HC = β′. The 90° are added because the table counts the right ascensions from the beginning of Capricorn (note 1).
