@obs body L1: rule (α) is right: the meridian altitude of a star is 90° − φ ± δ, the declination added when it is northern and subtracted when it is southern. For the equator (δ = 0) it is 90° − φ, as the figure shows: the arc φ from the horizon to the pole, the quadrant from the pole to the equator and the arc alt. merid. from the equator to the horizon make up the half circle above the horizon.
@obs body L6: the dash of «al–Battānī» is a long dash (5.3 pt).
@obs body L7: rule (β) is rule (α) of chapter 16 (p. 190) and is right: with R = 1 and H the meridian altitude, 1 − cos P = 1 + tg φ tg δ = sin H/(cos φ cos δ) and sin h = sin φ sin δ + cos φ cos δ cos t give (1 − cos P)(1 − sin h/sin H) = 1 − cos t. The minus sign is printed as a long dash (9.7 pt); it is set as a minus sign.
