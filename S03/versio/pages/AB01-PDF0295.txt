@obs body L20: the dash of «indo–arischen» is a long dash.
@obs body (table): the table is boxed by double rules, the head separated from the rows by a single rule; the heads are in small capitals. The first row carries the signs of days, hours, minutes and seconds, the seconds' sign before the point; specks stand before «31.» in the second row and before «365» in the seventh.
