% Commentary on chapter 28, c) continued: al-Mas'ūdī's report that the Indians move the apogee of the Sun 1° in 100 years, and the very slow revolutions of the apogees in the Sūrya-siddhānta and in Brahmagupta's Brahmasphuṭa-siddhānta; the Arabs first gave the apogee the motion of precession. The proper motion of the apogee: az-Zarqālī, after Aboul Hhassan, 1° in 299 years, a motion back and forth in a small circle (begun). Notes on al-Bīrūnī; on Thibaut's Pañchasiddhântikâ and the Pulisa-siddhānta; Schiaparelli on the slow revolutions; on Arzachel.
