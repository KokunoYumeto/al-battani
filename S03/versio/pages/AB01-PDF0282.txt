@obs body L1-L11: Schiaparelli's derivation is checked. Menelaus' theorem (L3) in the triangle QPS cut by the equator, with QZ = 90° + β′, SV = δ, PY = 90°, QY = 90° + ε, SZ = β + β′ and PV = 90°, gives cos β′ sin δ R = sin (β + β′) cos ε R, the rule of p. 192.
@obs body L4-L11: the figure stands at the left of lines 4 to 11, which are set beside it in a narrow measure. @obs body L17-L18: the formulas are checked. With tg β′ = tg ε sin L, sin δ′ = sin ε sin L and sin d′ = sin ε cos L, cos β′ = cos ε / cos d′ holds as printed, but sin β′ = sin δ′ / cos d′, not sin δ′ / sin d′: for ε = 23° 30′ and L = 30°, β′ = 12° 16′ and sin β′ = 0.2125 = sin δ′ / cos d′, while sin δ′ / sin d′ = 0.577. Kept as printed.
@obs body L20-L30: the procedure by the tables is checked. In the triangle OTZ, right-angled at T, tg OZ = tg L / cos ε, so that OZ is the longitude whose right ascension is L, and sin TZ = sin OZ sin ε, the declination of that longitude, is β′; and cos ε = cos β′ sin Z gives sin Z = cos ε / cos β′, whence sin δ = sin (β + β′) sin Z.
@obs body L38: the minus sign is printed as a long dash (9.8 pt); it is set as a minus sign. «cotg C» is printed for cotg Z: in the triangle SVZ, right-angled at V, sin VZ = tg δ cotg Z with VZ = OZ − asc. recta. Kept as printed.
