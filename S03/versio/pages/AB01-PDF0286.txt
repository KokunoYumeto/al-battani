@obs body L3-L4: both rules give the right ascension counted from the beginning of Capricorn, that is, increased by 90°. When the star rises, the point of the equator rising with it has the right ascension α − P + 90° (α the right ascension of the star from the first point of Aries, P its semidiurnal arc), so the oblique ascension of the degree rising with the star, increased by P, is α + 90°. When the star sets, the point of the equator then rising has the right ascension α + P + 90°, so the second rule holds with the oblique ascension of the degree rising at that moment, the nadir of the degree setting with the star (the ascension of the nadir, as on p. 196), diminished by P; the oblique ascension of the setting degree itself differs from it by 180° and twice the ascensional difference of that degree.
