% Commentary on chapter 28, c) continued: III. The motion of the apogee of the Sun: al-Battānī gives the apogee only the motion of precession, as Delambre rightly says; why he did not compare his apogee with the 65° 30' of Hipparchus and Ptolemy, who held the apogee of the Sun immovable, as Theon did; the proper motion of 11",46 a year, 2° 22' 18" in 745 years; the Arabs first recognized the motion of the apogee: al-Mas'ūdī on Thābit ibn Qurrah, Ibn Yūnus on two Persian observations after Caussin, Ibn 'Ezrā. Notes on Ptolemy's Hypotheses and Proclus; on Thābit ibn Qurrah; on al-Bīrūnī; on Steinschneider; on Barhebraeus.
