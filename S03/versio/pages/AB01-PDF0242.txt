@obs notes_right L1-L2: Nallino's addenda et emendanda in Part II (p. XXI; AB01-PDF0438-body-L017) put + for the − in the numerator of the fraction, «+ crd q crd (180° — p)». With crd x = 2R sin (x/2) and crd (180° − x) = 2R cos (x/2), crd p crd (180° − q) + crd q crd (180° − p) = 4R² sin ((p + q)/2) = 2R crd (p + q), the rule of the sum of two arcs; with the printed −, the fraction gives crd (p − q). The printed − is kept.
