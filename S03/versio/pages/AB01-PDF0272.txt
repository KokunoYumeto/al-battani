@obs body L1: the head of the notes on chapter 11, «Ad» and «caput» in small capitals, in the larger type of the heads.
@obs body L6-L7, L27-L29: the rules are checked. In the projection on the meridian (first figure), E is the point of the equator on the meridian, EO = BP = φ, so that EZ = cos φ and ZC = sin φ (sines of the radius R); HK = sin h, and the right triangles EZC and HKG are similar, so that KG = sin h sin φ / cos φ, rule (α). In the horizon, CG is the sine of the amplitude ortiva and CK = CG ∓ KG; the foot of the Sun lies at R cos h from the centre, so that sin azimuth = R (sin amplit. ortivae ± sin diff. horiz.) / cos h, rule (β), the azimuth counted from the east or west point.
