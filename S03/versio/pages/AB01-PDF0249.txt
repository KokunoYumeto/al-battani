@obs body (table): the table is boxed by double rules, the head separated from the rows by a single heavier rule; the heads are set in bold small capitals, «Observatores» in the small capitals of the text; the rows are set in a smaller type.
@obs body (table): the asterisks that mark the certain years stand in the margin of the column of the years, before the numbers; in the column Error the minus signs are printed as long dashes.
@obs body (table): the first row carries the signs of degrees, minutes and seconds; in the column Obliquitas vera they are «23° 43′ 45.″ 01», the sign of the seconds after the point.
@obs body (table): recomputed with Bessel's formula of p. 159 (t = 1750 less the year), the true obliquities of the rows from 829 to 1437 agree with the print within 0.05″, except Ibn al-A‘lam and aṣ-Ṣūfī: for 960 and 965 the formula gives 23° 34′ 38.41″ and 23° 34′ 36.01″, and the printed values 37.92″ and 35.50″ are those of 961 and 966 (37.93″, 35.53″). For Eratosthenes the formula gives 23° 44′ 5.01″ (t = 1980), exactly 20″ more than the printed 23° 43′ 45.01″, as if the term 15′ 47.01″ had been added as 15′ 27.01″; for Hipparchus 23° 43′ 17.69″ (t = 1880), 21.07″ more than printed. Every error of the last column is the observed less the printed true obliquity, rounded to the second (Ibn ash-Shāṭir: −24.78″, printed −0′ 24″). The printed values are kept.
