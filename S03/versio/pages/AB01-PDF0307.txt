IV. The longitudes of the apogee of the Sun before and beside al-Battānī: the Babylonians, the Greeks, the Sūrya-siddhānta, the Romaka-siddhānta, Āryabhaṭa, the Sindhind, the Tables of the Shāh and the astronomers of al-Ma'mūn. Notes on Ricius and Levi ben Gerson; on Kugler; on the Brahmasphuṭa-siddhānta and al-Mas'ūdī; on the Tables of the Shāh (Schiaparelli on the Pahlavi tables); on the year of the hegira (begun).
