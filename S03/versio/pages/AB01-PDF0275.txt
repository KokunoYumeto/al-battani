@obs body L22-L27: the formulas are checked. The proportion EC : CG = EM : GF = CM : CF gives EM = GF × EC / CG; with EC = sin amplit. ortivae, GF = sin altit. merid. and CG = EC + EG = sin amplit. ortivae + cos altit. merid. it is rule (α). With R = 1, sin amplit. ortivae = sin δ / cos φ, sin altit. merid. = cos (φ − δ) and cos altit. merid. = sin (φ − δ), the denominator cos φ sin (φ − δ) + sin δ equals sin φ cos (φ − δ), so that the altitude on the prime vertical is sin δ / sin φ, as it must be.
