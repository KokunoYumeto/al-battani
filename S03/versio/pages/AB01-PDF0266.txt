@obs body L35: the formula is Menelaus' theorem for the spherical triangle THE cut by the meridian ABZ: sin AT : sin AE = (sin TZ : sin ZH) × (sin HB : sin BE), written with the chords of the double arcs; checked, the ratios are as printed. With AE = 90°, TZ = 90°, ZH = 90° − ε and HB = 90° − HE it gives cos HE = sin P cos ε.
@obs body L12-L25: Nallino's addenda et emendanda in Part II (pp. XXIII-XXIV; AB01-PDF0440-body-L028 to AB01-PDF0441-body-L019) give de Goeje's letter on pp. 175-177. His note on Qodāmah meant that in «المتصل بالبحر الاخضر» the Green Sea cannot be the Atlantic, though in what follows the name surely means it; he now thinks Qodāmah's text lacunose and corrupt and of no use for the question. Nallino's solution on this page he finds very ingenious, but uncertain while no independent witness gives the gulf of al-Qolzum the name of the Green Sea; Ibn Rosteh's «البحر الاخير» is as uncertain (de Goeje knows the name only from the Samaritan Chronicle, where it seems to mean the Mediterranean, or perhaps the gulf of Aylah). He cannot accept the reading «وعرض طريقه», which he holds a corruption of «طرفه» (or «طرفيه»): in two of its three places it is opposed to «عرضه في الاصل», and in the first, the Barbarian Gulf (Part III, p. 26, line 5; Part I, p. 17, line 31), Ibn Rosteh and al-Kharaqī both have «طرفه». In al-Mas‘ūdī «طرفيه» may be right, the sea having two extremities: that of Aylah, or ‘Aqabah, nearer to Syria («الطرف الادنى») and belonging to it, and that of al-Qolzum, which answers to the Yam ṣūf and belongs to Egypt; «cette dernière», he writes, may have had the name «البحر الاخير», and the «الاخضر» of al-Battānī and al-Kharaqī would then be a corruption of «الاخير», or the two have been confused. In al-Kharaqī's text, the fullest, «الى طرف» is to be read «الطرف», and Ibn Rosteh's «اعني» may be supplied. In a note Nallino adds that Plato and the Spanish translator had the reading «طريقه» («in parte commeabili», «enel logar do lo passan»), as has al-Kharaqī in two places.
