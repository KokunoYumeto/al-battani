@obs body L2-L12: the figure stands at the left of lines 2 to 12, which are set beside it in a narrow measure. It is redrawn from measurements of the print: the ecliptic AHDB, printed a little wider than high, as an ellipse about its pole F; the straight lines FB, FA and FC′ (through M, M′ and C) and the chords BA, GE and GA; the great circle DH through G and E and the great circle through G and A as arcs, each below its chord; the bold italic letters and the italic word ecliptica along the circle.
@obs body L3-L4: Schiaparelli's name is letter-spaced and divided between the lines.
@obs body L14-L18: the proportion and the rule are right: in the projection FG = FE = R cos β and FB = FA = R, GM and BC are the halves of the chords GE and BA, and the chord between the two stars, parallel to the ecliptic, keeps its length; so R sin (GE/2) : R sin ((L − L′)/2) = R cos β : R.

@obs body L34: the printed formula has 2 GB². The printed form is kept. The exponent of GA is printed broken.
