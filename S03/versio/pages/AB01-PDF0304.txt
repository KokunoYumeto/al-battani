The computation of the longitude of the apogee: Ptolemy's method from the equinoxes and the summer solstice, with the formulas for FS, ES, the eccentricity FE and the longitude of the apogee; al-Bīrūnī's account of the method of the later astronomers from the middles of the fixed signs and of Abū Naṣr Manṣūr ibn 'Irāq's method from three points of the ecliptic; Delambre's solution. Notes on Leverrier's formula; on al-muḥdathūn; on Abū Naṣr ibn 'Irāq and his treatise; on Sachau and Holetschek.
