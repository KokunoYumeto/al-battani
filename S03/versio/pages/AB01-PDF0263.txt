@obs body L1-L20: the Arabic text of al-Kharaqī's chapter, begun on p. 173, continued in the same type and set right to left, its lines justified across the measure; the note numbers 1 to 11 are raised Latin figures after the words they concern. The edition sets the lines in the Amiri type at the measured leading of the block.
@obs body L2, L15: «ثلثة» and «ثلثمائة» are written without the alif of the long vowel, as printed.
@obs body L7: in «المُنتهَى» the mīm stands at the foot of the lām, and the dot of the nūn is set above the two dots of the tā'.
@obs body L8, L22: note 5 asks for the negation before «المسلوكة» (the mountains not passable); the text keeps the reading of the manuscripts.
@obs body L21-L26: the readings of the manuscripts stand under a short rule, in the type of the notes and at a wider leading; the undotted letters of the manuscripts are printed so (the teeth as dotless beh, the qāf in note 2 without its dots), and the dots of the yā' in «سريره» (P) touch the rā' before it.
@obs body L21: in note 2 the qāf and the mīm of L «ڡمير» are printed in one ligature, the qāf above the mīm.
@obs body L23: «هِيرَقْلَس» in the quotation of Yāqūt carries a kasra under the hā', a fatha over the rā', a sukūn over the qāf and a fatha over the lām.
@obs body L23, L25: the names Yāqūt, Qāḍī–zādeh, Quṭb ad–dīn Maḥmūd ash–Shīrāzī are letter-spaced; their dashes measure about 4 pt, longer than the hyphen of «pri-» (2.6 pt).
