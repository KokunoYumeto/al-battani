% Printed p. 198: the beginning of chapter 55, on knowing the ascensions of the signs between the cardines, in the quarters of the sphere. He said: since what must follow what we have described of the amounts of the rays on the circle of the signs is the knowledge of the ascensions of the signs between the cardines, and since their ascensions are known only in the right sphere, which are their ascensions at the cardine of midheaven and the cardine of the earth, and in the climates, which are their risings and settings at the two horizons, the cardine of the ascendant and the cardine of the setting, of the circle of the horizon of every land, and all this that we have described is of different amounts, what remains of the kind of the ascensions is that you know the ascensions of the signs between these cardines in the regions of the sphere, so that you know in how many times of the equator one of the signs rises in every place of the sphere; by that you know the amount of the times of the equator that falls between the preceding degree of the sphere of the signs and the following degree, by the times of the passage of the preceding degree in that place. An example: the whole sign of Aries rises in midheaven with 27;53 of the times of the equator and passes the cardine of the earth with the like, and it rises in the fourth climate with 19;12 of the times of the equator and sets in this climate by the amount of the rising of Libra in it, which is 36;34. Between each of these cardines and the cardine that follows it are six seasonal hours, the hours of one of the quarters of the day and the night: what falls in the two quarters above the earth has day hours, and what is under the earth, in the two remaining quarters, has night hours. When the sign of Aries leaves one of these cardines, the amounts of its ascensions differ and exceed the amounts that we have mentioned or fall short of them, according to the number of seasonal hours by which the beginning of Aries is away from the cardine with which it is compared. Let us first suppose the beginning of Aries two seasonal hours away from midheaven toward the east: the ascensions of Aries there become less than its ascensions in midheaven by 2;54, which is a third of what lies between its ascensions in midheaven and its ascensions in the climate at the eastern horizon, as the two hours by which it is away from midheaven are a third of the six hours between midheaven and the ascendant. When the beginning of Aries is three seasonal hours away from midheaven on this side, its rising there is less than its rising in the right sphere by the amount (the sentence runs on).

@h2 في معرِفة مَطالِع البروج فيما بين الأوتاد في ارباع الفلك.{mL:5}
