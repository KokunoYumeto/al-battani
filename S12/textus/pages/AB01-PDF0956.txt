An example of the degrees that hear and obey: twenty degrees of Pisces hear and obey ten parts of Aries, because the increase of the day of ten of Aries is like the decrease of twenty of Pisces. These amounts of the two kinds may agree and fall in one of the figures: the beginning of Sagittarius is connected with the beginning of Aquarius and shares with it in the figure of sextile, and their distance from the beginning of Capricorn is one distance, so that it joins the two things; likewise the beginning of Pisces shares with the beginning of Taurus in the figure, and the beginning of Pisces hears the beginning of Taurus. The same may fall in trine, quartile and opposition: the middle of Taurus is at quartile of the middle of Leo, the middle of Aquarius at quartile of the middle of Taurus, the beginning of Taurus at trine of the beginning of Virgo, the beginning of Capricorn at trine of the beginning of Taurus, the head of Cancer at opposition of the head of Capricorn, and the distance of these parts from the point of the solstice and the point of the equinox is an equal distance; likewise the beginning of Aries faces the beginning of Libra. The wandering stars may be connected with the fixed stars when a distance of sextile, trine, quartile or opposition is between them; and the wandering and the fixed stars cast their ray on the circle of the sphere of the signs by different amounts, which increase and decrease by the amount of the difference of the latitudes, so that when the amount between the two stars is known, it is known whether they are in one of the figures of connection. For the fixed stars, because of the slowness of their motion, one does not work with the connection of the wandering stars with them nor with the casting of their rays on the circle of the signs, but one looks at the figures that they have with them at the cardines and at the bodily conjunction, especially with the sun. For the wandering stars one needs to know the distances of one from another and the amounts by which they cast the rays on the circle of the signs according to their latitudes, at nativities and in the direction from one to another. Opposition does not fall exactly unless the two stars are together on the circle of the signs, or the latitude of each of the two stars is equal to that of the other and they are in different directions; if one of the two stars is on the circle of the signs and the other inclined from it in latitude, the distance between them is less than the distance of opposition by the amount of the latitude of the star; if the latitude of the two stars is one latitude in one direction, the distance between them falls short of opposition by the amount of both latitudes. The quartile that falls from the other stars on the circle of the signs is always in one state: it neither exceeds nor falls short of ninety, whether the latitude is great or small, and that is plain on the sphere, where the circles fall on its two poles. As for sextile, when the star has a latitude, its ray is cast on the circle of the signs at less than sixty parts from the part in which it stands.
