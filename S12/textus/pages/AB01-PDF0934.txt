Title: «Chapter on knowing how to find the beginnings of the years of the Arabs and of their months, on which one works in the dating, by the tables.» He said: to know the sign of the year you are in of the years of the Arabs, the years of the Hijra, see by how much the years of the Hijra exceed two hundred and ten years, cast out two hundred and ten after two hundred and ten, and work with what is left under that, because they return every two hundred and ten years to the first pattern, always. With the years that result, together with the year you are in, even if only one day of it has entered, look for the like of the number in the lines of the tables of the collected years and take the sign of the years that stands beside it; if the number is not found there, look for the nearest one below it and take what stands under it of the signs of the years; then enter with the years that are left the line of the single years, take the sign under it and add it to the first sign: if the sum is more than seven, cast out seven, and what is left is the sign of the year sought. If the number entered was found whole in the collected years and nothing is left for the single years, add one, always, to what stands under the collected years.
