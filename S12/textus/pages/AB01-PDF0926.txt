We went that way in it so as to set it in a table for all the signs by differences of ten parts; in them is enough for what is needed of the knowledge of the single ascensions for the country and for what agrees with its latitude. This working is by the small table drawn under the square of the degrees of the declination. ﴿Chapter﴾ When you want to know the unequal motion of the Moon in an hour, for the reckoning of conjunction and opposition, take the excess between the Sun and the corrected Moon and learn what answers to it in the tables of the addition and the subtraction of the motion of the Moon in an hour, which is for every degree separately. The minutes that there are, take from the unequal motion of the Moon in an hour when the corrected anomaly of the Moon, whose motion in an hour you have learnt, is from 0 to 95 and from 265 to 360, and add them to that motion when that anomaly is from 95 to 265; what it comes to after the adding or the taking is the corrected motion. Take from it the motion of the Sun in an hour: what is left is the gain of the Moon.
