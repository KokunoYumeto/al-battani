[Then] Cancer, whose stars are 9, among them al-Nathra; then Leo, whose stars are 27, among them the Lock, al-Tarf and al-Jabha, which is the Heart of the Lion, al-Zubra and al-Sarfa; then Virgo, whose stars are 26, among them al-Awwa and the unarmed Simak: this is what is in the northern half of the figures of the zodiac. «As for those in the southern region» of them, I mean of the figures of the zodiac: Libra, whose stars are 8, among them al-Ghafr; then Scorpio, with 21 stars, among them the two Zubana, the Crown, the Heart and al-Shawla; then Sagittarius, with 31 stars, among them al-Naaim and al-Balda; then Capricornus, with 28 stars, among them Sad al-Dhabih and Sad Bula; then Aquarius, with 42 stars, among them Sad al-Suud and Sad al-Akhbiya; then Pisces, with 34 stars, among them the fore spout and the hind spout. So all the stars in the figures of the zodiac are three hundred and forty-six; all the stars in the eighteen northern figures named in the table set down are three hundred and sixty; and all that is in these fifteen southern figures is three hundred and sixteen stars. So all these counted stars are one thousand and twenty-two: fifteen of them of the first magnitude, 45 of the second, 208 of the third, 474 of the fourth, 217 of the fifth and 49 of the sixth; the cloudy ones, like mist, are five, and 9 stars are of the dark ones; and the stars of the Lock, the Solitary one and al-Mirzam. What we have set down of these stars is those for which we found a known temperament in the book of Ptolemy, of the figures outside the zodiac, above all the great ones among them; of those in the figures of the zodiac we have recorded their temperament and their force as like the force of the two luminaries and the planets. After that we set down, for those of the first magnitude, the second and some of the third, separate tables in which we recorded their distances from the equator, half their stay above the earth, their height in the middle of the sky, and with which degrees of the zodiac they rise, culminate and set where the height of the north pole above the horizon is thirty-six degrees, which is the latitude of the city of al-Raqqa; and we put these states of theirs that we have described at the year 1211 of the years of Dhu l-Qarnayn. In the tables before these we set down their places in longitude from the beginning of Aries. When you wish to know the place of any of these fixed stars that we have set down in the tables, take its motion in the years that are gathered from the year 1191 of the years of Dhu l-Qarnayn and add it to its place set down in the tables, and cast that off from the beginning of Aries; where the number ends with you, there is the place of the star in the sign at which it ends, and its latitude [the next page continues]. The notes give the readings of the codex for nine places, most of them numerals.
