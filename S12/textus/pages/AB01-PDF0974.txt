[The true motion of the planet is taken from] the true motion of the sun in the day: the remainder is the true corrected course; this for the three upper planets. As for Venus and Mercury, the course of one of them is added to the course of the sun if they are retrograde, and the difference between the two is taken if they are direct: the result is the corrected course of one of them. What comes out of the division: after the number of those days and hours it rises or sets, or the like of the number of those days and hours ago it rose or set. «I have set down for them» the amounts of the vision in degrees of the ecliptic in every clime, as their appearances were observed in every sign, and that was drawn at the heads of the signs, although it is not exact, because of what happens through the difference of their latitudes; therefore we have confined ourselves to setting down these amounts in one clime, so that the knowledge of that from this table be easy, approximately, and we have made it for the fourth clime. To know the visibility of the planet and its disappearance, what is under the sign in which the planet is, of the amounts of the vision and of the disappearance drawn in the table of the rising of the mornings and of the disappearance of the evenings for the three upper planets, is taken, and what is under the sign after it; then the difference between the two signs is taken and multiplied by the degrees of the planet in the sign in which it is, and the result is divided by thirty; what comes out is added to the amount of the vision or of the disappearance, whichever was reckoned for, that comes under the sign in which the planet is, if this is the lesser, and taken from it if it is the greater: the remainder is the arc of vision in that place in degrees of the ecliptic, and the arc of disappearance. If that is equal to what is between the sun and the planet in degrees of the ecliptic, on that day it rose or was hidden; if it differs, one follows in it what was drawn and explained in this chapter. It is known that working the visibility by reckoning, in the way drawn at first, is sounder and surer than what comes out of these tables. As for Venus and Mercury, the four states mentioned are taken for them in this way that we have described for the upper planets. The notes: the codex has «for the two of them» for «for them», and «their latitudes» with the dual pronoun.
