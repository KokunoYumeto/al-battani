## Conventions and checks

The conventions are those of [S10](../../S10/textus/README.md): the signs as printed, misprints kept as printed and described, a letter written with the dots that the master scan or the second copy of Part III (Internet Archive albattanisivealb00batt) shows, long joins not transcribed, abjad numerals and the letters of the constructions under the bars that are printed over them.

Printed forms that differ from the expected word are kept and described in `@obs`: p. 154: «القطربْن» (line 10), whose tooth after the rā' is printed with one dot (Nallino's corrigenda: «١٥٤,10, القطرَيْن»); p. 155: «نُدِير» (line 19), whose yā' is printed without its dots, with a kasra under the dāl; «دَوَائِر» (line 9) and «الدَّوَائِر» (line 20), whose tooth is printed bare, without a hamza and without dots, with a kasra under it; and «القمر» (line 4), the stem of whose lām is printed in two pieces; p. 156: «لدوائر» (line 2), whose tooth is printed bare, without a hamza, without dots and without a kasra; p. 157: the word for the anomaly, printed without the dot of the khā' (lines 19, 21 and 22 of Nallino's count); and «فاقسِمْه» (line 16 of that count), whose sīn is printed flat, without its teeth; p. 158: the word for the anomaly, printed without the dot of the khā' (lines 1, 3 twice and 6) and, in the second place of line 3, with a fatha alone over the ṣād, without the shadda («وحاصَته»); and «بمقدار» (lines 11 and 13), whose bā' is printed in two pieces before the mīm; p. 160: the word for the anomaly, printed without the dot of the khā' (lines 2, 7 and 9); and «والقمر» (line 13), whose rā' is printed apart from the mīm, as a low crescent under the line; p. 161: the note figure «4» printed after the closing square bracket of line 8, where note 6 is meant; the second «الابعد» of line 6, whose bā' and ʿayn are printed as a short tooth and a small knot; and the word for the anomaly, printed without the dot of the khā' (lines 3 and 22).

Each page is read from enlarged images of the master scan, with every vowel sign, hamza, sukun and bar measured. The reading is compared letter by letter with the scan's text layer and with Tesseract 5 (Arabic model), and every word that neither confirms is checked on the scan. Sweeps then compare what the transcription expects with what is measured on the page: the hamza sorts, the sukuns, the dots letter by letter, and the kasras under the lines; every place where they differ is looked at, the doubtful ones in both copies. Sheets set every signed word, every word that ends in yā' and every printed line beside the same word or line of the built edition. Nallino's corrigenda give readings for seven places on pp. 154–217 (p. 154, line 10; p. 176, line 12; p. 198, lines 8, 15, 16 and 22; p. 217, line 18); each is compared when its page is made, and the printed form is kept.
