@obs body L35-L36: in the variables of note 1, Ptolemy's lunar model (Almagest V: eccentric of radius 49p 41′, eccentricity 10p 19′, epicycle of 5p 15′, the mean apogee of the epicycle directed from the point opposite the centre of the eccentric) has no term in sin 2 (☾ − ⊙) alone: at a fixed elongation, as m runs once round, the Moon runs uniformly round the epicycle, which is symmetric about the line from the Earth to its centre, so the equation averages to zero; the rule of the tables, whose equations change sign with the corrected anomaly, has the same symmetry. Expanded numerically, the model gives terms of 6° 10′,6 in sin m and 1° 19′,6 in sin (2 [☾ − ⊙] − m), then 19′,9 in sin 2m and 17′,3 in sin (4 [☾ − ⊙] − m); the term 18′ sin 2 (☾ − ⊙) does not occur in these variables.
