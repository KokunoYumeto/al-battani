@obs body L1-L5: Schiaparelli's addition begun on p. 262 goes on. The nonagesimal lies in the meridian only when the meridian passes through the pole of the ecliptic, that is when a solstitial point culminates, as said. Its hour angle, computed with Theon's obliquity, reaches at most 0.7 h in the first of Theon's seven climates (latitude 16° 27′), 1.15 h in the third (30° 22′), 1.4 h at 36° and 2.1 h in the seventh (48° 32′): «1 horae vel magis» (L3) and «duas horas attingere potest in climatibus borealioribus» (L13), as printed.
