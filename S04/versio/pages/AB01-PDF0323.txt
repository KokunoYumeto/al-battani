@obs body L10: 42° and 52° are the true longitudes of the Sun less the apogee: 124° 1′ 32″ − 82° 17′ 42″ = 41° 43′ 50″ (p. 230) and 134° 36′ 13″ − 82° 33′ 12″ = 52° 3′ 1″ (p. 231).
@obs body L11-L19: AL = 5p,247 is 60p sin 5° 1′, al-Battānī's greatest equation of the Moon (p. 221). In the triangle AEL the angle at A is 180° less QAL; with QAL 114° 16′ and 111° 4′, EL is 58p,0407 and 58p,3199, and 60p less these 1p,96 and 1p,68; with the true anomalies of pp. 230 and 231, 114° 13′ 38″ and 111° 9′ 1″, EL would be 58p,044 and 58p,312.
@obs body (figure): the figure is not drawn to scale: AL is about an eighth of AE, where 5p,247 to 60p is about an eleventh, and the angle QAL is about 112°.
