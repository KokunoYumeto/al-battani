@obs body L1-L11: at the point of contact I the radius OI is perpendicular to TI, so the angle TOI is 90° − OTI and the arc MI from the highest point M is 90° + OTI; OTI, the greatest angle under which the epicycle is seen from T, is the greatest equation of the centre. Likewise MIK = 360° − (90° + KTO) = 270° − KTO. With al-Battānī's greatest equations (Part II, p. 81: 1° 59′ 10″ for the Sun, 5° 1′ 0″ for the Moon) the points of contact lie at 91° 59′ 10″ and 268° 0′ 50″, and at 95° 1′ and 264° 59′; with 7° 40′ at 97° 40′ and 262° 20′ (L14).
