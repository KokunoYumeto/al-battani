II. Al-Battānī mentions tubes (p. 91, l. 1, 5, 9, 11, 34), which keep off the diffused light of the sky: the oldest mention among the Arabs; the tube of Naṣīr ad-dīn aṭ-Ṭūsī's great sextant at Marāghah (1259); no tubes among the Greek astronomers, unless in Pseudo-Plutarch's De facie; Aristotle, De generatione animalium V, 1, on vision through a pipe; Polybius X, 43, the dioptra of Cleoxenus and Democletus; Rabbi Gamaliel's tube in the Talmud; Walafried in 825 at Reichenau; Wolf and Günther. The Shù-King on king Shun (2255-2205 B.C.), the jewelled sphere and tube; Nocentini's doubts and Ts‘ai C‘in's commentary. Note: the editions of the Shù-King by Legge, Gaubil-Pauthier and Zottoli.
