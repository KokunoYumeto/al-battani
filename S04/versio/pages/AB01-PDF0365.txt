@obs body L1-L12: the figure stands on the left of these lines and is redrawn from the print: the shadow about S (radius 79.8 pt) and the Moon about V (radius 38.9 pt), circles fitted to the print; the line ZA through their centres; the common chord BC, perpendicular to ZA within 0.2 degree; the radii SB, SC and VB, VC, which meet within 0.8 pt of the centres; the bold italic capitals, a, b, c in italic, and «umbra Terrae» in italic along the bottom of the shadow. On the figure SK = a and SN = b − c, so KA = c + (b − a) and KN = c − (b − a), the two factors of L16.
@obs body L2, L22, L26: «al–Battānī» (L2) and «Al–Battānī» (L22) are letter-spaced, with long dashes (5.5 and 5.4 pt); «al-Battānī» (L26) is letter-spaced, with a hyphen (3.5 pt).
@obs body L9-L20: Euclid, Elements II, 13: in a triangle the square on the side opposite an acute angle is less than the squares on the sides containing it by twice the rectangle of one side and the projection of the other on it; in SBV the projections of VB and SB on SV are LV = x and SL = y. The formulas are right: c² − (a − b)² = {c + (b − a)}{c − (b − a)} and a² − (b − c)² = {a + (b − c)}{a − (b − c)}, and ZN = a + (b − c), KN = a − (b − c).
@obs body L13-L16: a speck below the line after «ergo» (L13) is not a stop. The minus sign of «a — y» prints in two pieces, 5.5 and 3.0 pt, like «--» (L14); so do those of «a — y» and of «c — (b — a)» in L16. The minus signs are set as minus signs; the braces of L16 and L20 are as tall as the fractions.
@obs body L24, L27-L37: (β) holds because L lies between N and K; (γ) is the theorem of intersecting chords in the circle of the Moon, BL² = NL × LA, with LA = NA − NL; in L30 KV = SV − SK = c − KN, the semidiameter of the Moon less the corrected digits of the eclipse, NK; (δ) gives the eclipsed lune NBKC as the sum of the two segments, each a sector less a triangle. The dashes before «Quo» (L31), «Demum» (L34) and «Schiaparelli» (L37) measure 9.8, 9.5 and 8.0 pt; a heavy bracket closes the addition begun on p. 275, L35.
