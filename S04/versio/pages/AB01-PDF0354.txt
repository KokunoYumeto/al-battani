% Commentary on chapter 40, b) II. concluded (Schiaparelli): I. with E the centre of the Earth, F the observer, Z the zenith, L the Moon, ZFL = ζ the apparent zenith distance and FLE = p the parallax, LF produced to Q with EQ perpendicular: sin p = R EQ : dist., sin ζ = R EQ : a, so dist. × sin p = a sin ζ, and with a = 1 al-Battānī's first rule (α), dist. = sin ζ : sin p; figure redrawn. II. his second rule, mostly lost in the text: with z the true zenith distance ZEL and FQ perpendicular to EL, FL = a sin z : sin p, QL = FL nearly, EQ = a cos z : R, dist. = a sin z : sin p + a cos z : R, and with a = 1 and h the true altitude (β), dist. = sin z : sin p + sin h : R; figure redrawn. In p. 85, l. 23-24 the words «in semidiametrum multiplica, et productum» are to be struck out. Chapter 41, a) on p. 85, l. 37-39: the knowledge of the new moon among the Muslims, for the chronology and the fast of ramaḍān; the «day of doubt» (yawm ash-shakk); the witnesses accepted by the qāḍī (begun). Notes: the civil day begins at sunset; two witnesses for the end of ramaḍān.
