@obs body L1: the dash after «k)» measures 9.5 pt; «40—pag.» has a dash of 9.4 pt.
@obs body L2-L10: formula (α) is al-Battānī's rule of p. 80, L2-L7: the sine of the residue (the cosine of the distance of the Moon from the ascendant) times the sine of the altitude, divided by the sine of the distance, times R, divided by the sine of the zenith distance, that is the cosine of h. It is the rule of the right spherical triangle HET (the right angle at E): the cosine of the angle at H is tan HE : tan HT. «al-Battānī» (L9) has a hyphen (3.0 pt); the label «(α)» stands at the margin and the formula where it is printed.
@obs body L11-L19: the figure stands on the right of these lines and is redrawn from the print: the meridian ABGD (radius 54.2 pt), the horizon BETMD, the vertical circle AHEKG, the ecliptic ZHTL and the arc KLM of the great circle whose pole is H, as arcs of circles fitted to the print (radius 246.1, 264.6, 150.7 and 51.2 pt). The ecliptic ends 0.7 pt short of the meridian at N. The vertical prints broken in places between H and K, the ecliptic where it crosses the vertical at H and the arc KLM next to K; they are drawn whole. The letters are of two types, as far as the scan shows: B, E, T, M, K, N and G upright, A, Z, H, D and L italic; the A is printed without its bar.
@obs body L12, L14: «131–32» has a long dash (5.4 pt). «Ex II» is printed so, with two capitals I; the pole of the arc KLM is H (L13 and the figure).
@obs body L18-L20: L18 is the theorem of Menelaus for the triangle HKL cut by the horizon, which meets HK in E, HL in T and KL produced in M. With HK, HL and KM quadrants, EK = 90° − h, TL = 90° − HT and LM = 90° − KL, where KL measures the angle of latitude, it gives L20 and (α).
