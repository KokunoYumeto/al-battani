Schiaparelli: the new solution computes with AE for NE and corrects by NA, with the figure of the two epicycles redrawn: EG = EG′ − NA = (hypotenusa Lunae ad declivitatem epicycli aequata) − (portio duplae excentricitatis); the approximation suffices, since the angle EG′G never exceeds 5° 1′; NA nothing at the apogee of the eccentric, twice the eccentricity at the perigee. The trigonometric formula for NE (p. 78-79) after Almagest V, 13, with the figure of the eccentric redrawn: FL = ε sin 2(☾ − ⊙)/R, EL = ε cos 2(☾ − ⊙)/R. Note: the distance of the Moon from the centre of the Earth.

@obs body L9-L27: the figure stands on the right of these lines and is redrawn from the print: the two epicycles about A and about N (radius 30.9 and 31.0 pt) on the line from the Earth E to H′ (A and N 0.3 and 0.1 pt off it), the lines EG′, EG and G′G, the perpendiculars M′G′ and MG (89.8° and 89.7° to the line) and the radii AG′ and NG; the bold italic letters stand where they are printed. @obs body L33-L40: the angle FEL is the supplement of AEN, the double elongation, so that sin FEL = sin 2 (☾ − ⊙); in the right triangle FEL, FL and EL are ε sin and ε cos 2 (☾ − ⊙) on the radius R. On p. 79, L1-L4 al-Battānī takes the square of FL from 2468ᵖ 26′, the square of 49ᵖ 41′, and adds or subtracts EL: NE = NL ∓ EL. The bars between ☾ and ⊙ and after «180°» are minus signs; «ε» is printed upright.
