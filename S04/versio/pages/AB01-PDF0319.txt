@obs body L1: in the head the dash after «i)» measures 9.8 pt and is transcribed as a dash; that of «10-20» is a hyphen (3.1 pt).
@obs body L2-L3, L11: the dashes of «’l–qarnayn», «ar–Raqqah» and «’l–qar.» are long dashes (5.0 to 5.6 pt).
@obs body L2-L4: 23 Tammūz of the year 1194 of the era, which began on 1 October 882, is 23 July 883.
@obs body L5-L9: the Sun in Leo 4° 2′ is the true longitude 124° 1′ 32″ of L22; al-Battānī's equation of days of Leo 4° (Part II, p. 63) is 3° 4′, i. e. 12m 16s; 8h − 12m 16s = 7h 47m 44s.
@obs body L11-L19: the motions are those of al-Battānī's tables: the year 1191 of the era (Part II, p. 72), 3 years (p. 73), the end of ḥazīrān (p. 74), 23 days (p. 75), 7 hours (p. 76). For 55 minutes 11/12 of the hour's motions give 2′ 16″, 30′ 11″, 29′ 57″ and 7″: the «0 30 0» of the anomaly is 3″ more. The sums are right, and 242° 3′ 38″ + 308° 45′ 7″ = 190° 48′ 45″.
@obs body L20-L22: the apogee 82° 17′ 42″ adds 3 × 54″ to the 82° 15′ of 1191 (al-Battānī's table of the motion of the fixed stars, Part II, p. 107, has 2′ 44″ for 3 years); 125° 20′ 49″ − 82° 17′ 42″ = 43° 3′ 7″; his equation of the Sun (Part II, p. 79: 1° 19′ 12″ at 43°, 1° 20′ 43″ at 44°) gives the printed 1° 19′ 17″, and 125° 20′ 49″ − 1° 19′ 17″ = 124° 1′ 32″.
@obs body L21, L24-L25, L29, L31: the bars before «1 19 17», «4 43 43» and «0° 31′ 52″» and between ☾ and ⊙ are minus signs.
@obs body L23-L27: 308° 45′ 7″ − 125° 20′ 49″ = 183° 24′ 18″, doubled 6° 48′ 36″ beyond the full circle; 308° 45′ 7″ − 4° 43′ 43″ = 304° 1′ 24″, and the shadow, 180° away, stands in 124° 1′ 24″.
@obs body L24, L28-L31: for 6° 48′ 36″ al-Battānī's correction of the anomaly (Part II, p. 78: 0° 53′ at 6°, 1° 2′ at 7°) gives the printed 1° 0′ 17″, and 113° 13′ 21″ + 1° 0′ 17″ = 114° 13′ 38″; for this anomaly his simple equation (Part II, p. 81: 4° 44′ 6″ at 114°, 4° 42′ 24″ at 115°) gives the printed 4° 43′ 43″. 190° 48′ 45″ − 4° 43′ 43″ = 186° 5′ 2″, 6° 5′ 2″ beyond the descending node, for which his table of the latitude (Part II, p. 78: 0° 31′ 19″ at 6°, 0° 36′ 31″ at 7°) gives 0° 31′ 45″ south; the print has 0° 31′ 52″.
@obs body L34-L38: the corrupt numbers of Plato («51», «5») are printed in italics; the dots stand where Halley gives no number; the table goes on on p. 231.
