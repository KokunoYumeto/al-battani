% Commentary on chapter 54 d) I continued: since crd² x = 4 sin² x/2 = 4 (R² − R cos x)/2 = 2R² − 2R cos x, the formula found above, crd² x = R (crd² 60° − crd² β)/cos β, can also be written 2R² − 2R cos x = R ((2R² − 2R cos 60°) − (2R² − 2R cos β))/cos β; dividing all by 2R, R − cos x = R ((R − cos 60°) − (R − cos β))/cos β = − R cos 60°/cos β + R, whence cos x = R cos 60°/cos β. This rule, as answering al-Battānī's method, is given by the Libro de las taulas alfonsies in chapter LII, entitled «on projecting the rays of the planets on the ecliptic according to the opinion of al-Battānī» (note 1: the Spanish title, in the Libros del saber de astronomia del Rey D. Alfonso X de Castilla, ed. Rico y Sinobas, Madrid 1863-67, vol. IV, p. 181). The solution there is: «If the planet has latitude, divide 1800 (note 2: the edition has wrongly 180; for 1800 = 60p × 30p = R cos 60°) by the sine of the complement of the latitude of the planet; convert the quotient into an arc as sines are converted into arcs; the resulting arc [subtract from 90°, and what remains] (note 3: the words in brackets restored by a sure conjecture; the Spanish text of the whole place, with CLXXX [sic!]; this quantity is added to or subtracted from the longitude of the planet, giving the place of the left or the right radiation on the ecliptic) is the argument of the sextile». Likewise only the solution of the spherical triangle (note 4: whose sides are the latitude of the planet, the arc of 60° and the radiation sought) by various instruments is treated in other places of the same Libros del saber: vol. II, p. 65 (Libro de las armellas, book II, ch. XLVI); vol. III, p. 123 (Libro de la lámina universal, book II, part IV, ch. LXIV); vol. III, p. 206-207 (Segunda parte del libro de la açafeha, ch. LIX), each «according to the opinion of al-Battānī». The last place is the version of the book of Arzachel (az-Zarqālī) on the construction and use of the saphaea; the Arabic text is in the Escorial codex 962 (Casiri nr. 957), fol. 51 r.-52 r. Henry Bate understood al-Battānī's rules equally well (note 5: Magistralis compositio astrolabii, fol. d2 v.-d3 r., printed at the end of Abraham Iudaeus, de nativitatibus, Venice, Ratdolt, 1485; the work was completed at Malines on the fifth day before the ides of October 1274; Steinschneider, Orientalistische Litteratur-Zeitung 4, 15 November 1901, col. 444-45): after two other ways of projection, he has a chapter «on the aspects according to the common view of the astrologers and especially according to Albategni»: «There is a third way of taking the aspects ..... and it is by the equal degrees (note 6: on which Nallino spoke on p. 28, note 3) of the zodiac taken uniformly: and Albategni affirms this to be true in the fifty-fourth chapter of his book: unless the star whose aspect is sought has some latitude from the ecliptic line ..... (continued)».
