@obs body L1-L4: each formula stands where it is printed, «Qua re» and the label «(α)» at the margin. NL is the side of the right triangle FLN of p. 255: NL² = FN² − FL², with FN = FA = AE − FE = 60ᵖ − ε = 49ᵖ 41′, al-Battānī's radius of the eccentric (p. 78, L36-L37, its square 2468ᵖ 26′); the bars after «AE» and «60ᵖ» (L2) are minus signs.
@obs body L4: formula (α) is wrong in the sign of its last term. Since FEL = 180° − 2 (☾ − ⊙) (p. 255, L37-L38), EL = ε cos FEL/R = −ε cos 2 (☾ − ⊙)/R, not ε cos 2 (☾ − ⊙)/R as on p. 255, L40, and the distance is NE = √((60ᵖ − ε)² − (ε sin 2 (☾ − ⊙)/R)²) + ε cos 2 (☾ − ⊙)/R. This gives 60ᵖ at the syzygies, where the centre of the epicycle is in the apogee A, and 60ᵖ − 2ε = 39ᵖ 22′ at the quadratures, in the perigee; formula (α) as printed gives the reverse, 39ᵖ 22′ at the syzygies and 60ᵖ at the quadratures, and for the double elongation of 135.5° drawn on p. 255, 56ᵖ 31′ instead of 41ᵖ 48′. Al-Battānī's rule (p. 79, L1-L5) adds the product of the cosine below 90° and subtracts it above, and is right. Kept as printed.

@obs body L30-L34: L30 is the theorem of Menelaus for the triangle AZH cut by the horizon, which meets AZ produced in B, AH produced in E and ZH produced in T. With BA = EA = 90°, so that chord 2BA = chord 2EA = 2R, ZB the altitude of the culminating degree, ZT and TH its distance and that of the Moon from the ascendant and HE = h, it gives L32 and the rule (α), which is al-Battānī's of p. 79, L34-L38: the sine of the distance of the Moon from the ascendant times R, divided by the sine of the distance of the culminating degree from the ascendant, times the sine of the altitude of the culminating degree, divided by R.
