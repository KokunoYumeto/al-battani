% Commentary on chapter 30, the general remarks on the four eclipses continued: Ginzel's corrections of Oppolzer's Canon from ancient total solar eclipses and Schram's reduction tables; the reductions of al-Battānī's mean time to the modern one (half of the 32m between his and Ptolemy's, 16m) and of the meridians of Antioch (36° 9', Hartmann) and ar-Raqqah (39° 4', Chesney) to Greenwich; a boxed table of the true time of each syzygy after al-Battānī, the equation of days with the 16m, the difference of longitude, the mean time of the geocentric syzygy at Greenwich after al-Battānī and after Oppolzer-Ginzel, and their difference; the observed syzygies fall about a quarter of an hour later than the tables give, partly because the beginning of an eclipse is seen late. Notes: Ginzel's papers in the Sitzungsberichte of Vienna, 1882-1884; Schram's tables, 1889.
