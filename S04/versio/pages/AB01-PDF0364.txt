% Commentary on chapter 42, c) concluded (Schiaparelli): if the Moon is at A or B (or A′ or B′) the elongation depends on the equation of the Sun alone, at most 2°, so its 7/24 exceed half a degree a little (the end of the line is lost), and often do not reach half; the places about ab are those where neglecting the 7/24 gives the greatest error, about 1/8 degree or 1/4 hour in the relative motion of the two luminaries (the ends of these lines are lost in the scan). Chapter 43. a) p. 97, l. 35 (Schiaparelli): the word mora in several senses: O the centre of the shadow, b the centre of the Moon at mid-eclipse, a at the end of totality, c at its beginning; here and p. 98, l. 36, the minutes of delay are AK, by which the magnitude of a total eclipse exceeds 12 digits; p. 98, l. 6 and elsewhere, half the length bc; the hours or time of delay, the time from b to a, half the duration of totality; the whole delay, its greatest value in a central eclipse, half the difference of the diameters of the shadow and the Moon (figure redrawn). b) p. 98, l. 39 - p. 99, l. 3: the italic words corrupted by the copyists; the probable restitution with the figure (redrawn): from the latitudes BA or DE, the squares subtracted from the square of OB or OD, half the difference of the diameters; the square of the difference of the latitudes IH or IC added; the root IB or ID divided by the motion of the Moon relative to the Sun; the preceding rules of p. 98 likewise. c) p. 99, l. 22 - p. 100, l. 8: the computation of the eclipsed surface of the lunar disc (begun). Notes: p. 96, l. 6, 1/8 hour in the codex and Plato, to be corrected to 1/8 degree as in p. 96, l. 1; note a above.
