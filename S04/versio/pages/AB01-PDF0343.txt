@obs body L1-L2: the label «(β)» stands at the margin and the formula is centred where it is printed. Formula (β) gives the distance only if ψ is read as the anomaly less 90°, as in al-Battānī's rule (p. 78, L20-L24: «de eo 90° deme»); with ψ the anomaly itself, as defined on p. 253, (α) holds for every anomaly, √((60ᵖ + e cos ψ/R)² + (e sin ψ/R)²). For e = 5ᵖ 15′ and an anomaly of 120°, (α) and (β) with ψ = 30° both give 57ᵖ 33′; (β) with ψ = 120° would give 55ᵖ 31′.
