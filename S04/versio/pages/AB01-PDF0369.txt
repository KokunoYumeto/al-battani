% Commentary on chapter 44, Nallino quoting al-Khāzinī, az-Zīǵ as-Sanǵarī (continued): the first method, after Ptolemy: from the hours of the true conjunction, the altitude and the parallax of the Moon, less the parallax of the Sun, the angle of longitude and the first parallax in longitude; divided by the true praecessio it gives the hours of the first parallax, taken from or added to the hours of the true conjunction as the Moon is less or more than 90° from the ascendant: the hours of the second conjunction; with them the second parallax; if equal to the first, those are the hours of mid-eclipse; else the difference is equated: unknown quantity : difference = difference : smaller parallax, i.e. the square of the difference divided by the smaller parallax (al-ǵuz' al-maǵhūl), added to the difference if the second parallax is the greater, taken from it if the first; the equated difference with the first parallax gives the equated parallax, which divided by the true praecessio gives the hours of the apparent parallax and the exact equated hours (murattabah) of mid-eclipse. Second method, from tradition ('amaluhu mursalan), of Thābit, the authors of the Tabula Probata and the Indians: the first parallax divided by the true praecessio, the hours of the second conjunction and the apparent place of the Moon, repeated (third, fourth, fifth hours of the conjunction) until a number equals the one before. Third method, after al-Battānī (begun). Notes: (1) Thābit died A.H. 288, A.D. 901; Suter, pp. 34-38, no. 66; (2) the Tabula Probata, made by the astronomers of the caliph al-Ma'mūn.

@obs body L1-L43: every line of the quotation of al-Khāzinī opens with a guillemet, as printed; the quotation runs on from p. 279, L46.
@obs body L10-L16: if the parallax grows in proportion to the time from the true conjunction, the parallax sought, P = p₁ + (p₂ − p₁)·P/p₁ with p₁ and p₂ the first and the second parallax, is p₁²/(2p₁ − p₂) = p₁ + (p₂ − p₁) + (p₂ − p₁)²/p₁ + …, and the rule gives it to the second order. When the second parallax is the greater, the difference increased by its square over the smaller parallax is added to the first parallax, as printed. When the first is the greater, the parallax sought lies between the two, p₁ − d + d²/p₁ − … with d = p₁ − p₂, and the difference lessened by that quantity must be taken from the first parallax: «addita» (L16) holds for the first case only.
