@obs body L1-L6: the table goes on from p. 230; col. I repeats the results computed there (113° 13′ 21″, 114° 13′ 38″, 190° 48′ 45″, 186° 5′ 2″ and the southern latitude 0° 31′ 52″). The corrupt numbers of the codex («0», «9») and of Plato («93», «0», «94», «10») are printed in italics; the dots stand where Halley gives no number; after Halley's «8» in L2 stands a raised dot, kept as printed.
@obs body L7-L8: the numbers are written in Maghribi abjad: «يج» 13, «قيد» 114, «ط» 9, «يد» 14; the sign before 0′ in L7 is the zero sign of the Arabic tables. 114° 13′ is the true anomaly of col. I. The dash before «Schiaparelli» measures 10.1 pt and is transcribed as a dash.
@obs body L10: in the head the dash after «k)» measures 10.3 pt and is transcribed as a dash; that of «21–32», joined to the figures, is a long dash.
@obs body L11-L13: 2 Āb of the year 1212 of the era, which began on 1 October 900, is 2 August 901, one saros (6585 days 7½ hours) after the eclipse of 23 July 883 (p. 230). The dash after «I.» measures 10.1 pt and is transcribed as a dash.
@obs body L13, L15, L18: the dashes of «ar–Raqqah» (4.3 and 5.7 pt) and of «’l–qar.», joined to its letters, are long dashes.
@obs body L14-L16: the Sun in Leo 14° 36′ is the true longitude 134° 36′ 13″ of L29; al-Battānī's equation of days (Part II, p. 63: 3° 9′ at Leo 14°, 3° 10′ at Leo 15°) gives 3° 10′, i. e. 12m 40s; 15h 20m + 15m − 13m = 15h 22m.
@obs body L18-L26: the motions are those of al-Battānī's tables: the year 1211 of the era (Part II, p. 72), 1 year (p. 73), the end of tammūz (p. 74), 2 days (p. 75), 15 hours (p. 76); those of 22 minutes are 11/30 of the hour's motions, 54″, 12′ 4½″, 11′ 59″ and 3″. The sums are right, and 230° 45′ 30″ + 319° 25′ 40″ = 190° 11′ 10″.
@obs body L27-L29: the apogee 82° 33′ 12″ adds 18′ 12″, the motion of 20 years in al-Battānī's table of the fixed stars (Part II, p. 107: 18′ 11″), to the 82° 15′ of 1191 (p. 227, note 1); the year 1212 is 21 years after 1191, and p. 229 takes 82° 34′ for it, which would give the anomaly 53° 36′ 12″ and the equation 1° 33′ 58″. 136° 10′ 12″ − 82° 33′ 12″ = 53° 37′ 0″; al-Battānī's equation of the Sun (Part II, p. 79: 1° 33′ 13″ at 53°, 1° 34′ 28″ at 54°) gives the printed 1° 33′ 59″, and 136° 10′ 12″ − 1° 33′ 59″ = 134° 36′ 13″.
@obs body L28, L31, L35, L37: the bars before «1 33 59», «4 48 57» and «0° 28′ 9″» are minus signs; the cross before «0 57 39» is a plus sign.
@obs body L30-L33: 319° 25′ 40″ − 136° 10′ 12″ = 183° 15′ 28″, doubled 6° 30′ 56″ beyond the full circle; 319° 25′ 40″ − 4° 48′ 57″ = 314° 36′ 43″, 180° 0′ 30″ from the true Sun.
@obs body L33-L37: for 6° 30′ 56″ al-Battānī's correction of the anomaly (Part II, p. 78: 0° 53′ at 6°, 1° 2′ at 7°) gives 0° 57′ 38″, the print 0° 57′ 39″, and 110° 11′ 22″ + 0° 57′ 39″ = 111° 9′ 1″; for this anomaly his simple equation (Part II, p. 81: 4° 49′ 12″ at 111°, 4° 47′ 32″ at 112°) gives the printed 4° 48′ 57″. 190° 11′ 10″ − 4° 48′ 57″ = 185° 22′ 13″, 5° 22′ 13″ beyond the descending node, for which his table of the latitude (Part II, p. 78: 0° 26′ 7″ at 5°, 0° 31′ 19″ at 6°) gives 0° 28′ 3″ south; the print has 0° 28′ 9″.
