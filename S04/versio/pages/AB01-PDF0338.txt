@obs body L1-L4: with s the semidiurnal arc of the ascendant, the formulas put the cusps XI and XII s/3 and 2s/3 of right ascension after the midheaven, II and III one and two thirds of the seminocturnal arc 180° − s after the ascendant: (180° − s)/3 = 60° − s/3.
