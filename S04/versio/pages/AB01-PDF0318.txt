@obs body L1-L7: the motions in 20 hours and in 1 hour are those of al-Battānī's table (Part II, p. 76), those of a third of an hour a third of the hour's; with p. 228 they sum, for 21h 20m after noon of 22 kānūn II 1212, to 307° 10′ 11″, 312° 50′ 6″ and 124° 57′ 39″. The node sums to 220° 36′ 18″: the printed 220° 36′ 10″ leaves out the 8″ of the hour, while the motion in latitude, 220° 36′ 18″ + 312° 50′ 6″ = 173° 26′ 24″ (L7), is made with them.
@obs body L8-L10: the apogee 82° 34′ is the 82° 15′ of 1191 (p. 227, note 1) with 21 years at 1° in 66 years; 307° 10′ 11″ − 82° 34′ = 224° 36′ 11″; al-Battānī's equation of the Sun (Part II, p. 82: 1° 24′ 48″ at 224°, 1° 26′ 14″ at 225°) gives the printed 1° 25′ 40″, and 307° 10′ 11″ + 1° 25′ 40″ = 308° 35′ 51″.
@obs body L11-L14: 312° 50′ 6″ − 307° 10′ 11″ = 5° 39′ 55″, doubled 11° 19′ 50″; 312° 50′ 6″ − 4° 14′ = 308° 36′ 6″. The bars before «4 14 0» (L12, L16) and between ☾ and ⊙ (L13) are minus signs.
@obs body L12, L15-L17: for 11° 19′ 50″ al-Battānī's correction of the anomaly (Part II, p. 78: 1° 38′ at 11°, 1° 46′ at 12°) is 1° 40′ 38″, the printed 1° 41′ to the minute, and 124° 57′ 39″ + 1° 41′ = 126° 38′ 39″. For this true anomaly his simple equation (Part II, p. 82: 4° 16′ 6″ at 126°, 4° 13′ 6″ at 127°) gives 4° 14′ 10″, against the printed 4° 14′ 0″; at this elongation the table adds nothing for the distance of the epicycle. 173° 26′ 24″ − 4° 14′ = 169° 12′ 24″.
@obs body L18-L21: 180° − 169° 12′ 24″ = 10° 47′ 36″; 27′ 33″ is the motion in latitude in 50 minutes, 5/6 of 32′ 56″ + 8″; 10° 47′ 36″ + 27′ 33″ = 11° 15′ 9″, for which al-Battānī's table of the latitude (Part II, p. 78: 0° 57′ 9″ at 11°, 1° 2′ 16″ at 12°) gives 0° 58′ 26″.
@obs body L25, L40-L41: the dash of «Al–Battānī» is a long dash (5.5 pt); that before «Schiaparelli» measures 9.4 pt and is transcribed as a dash.
@obs body L29-L38: col. I gives these results, with 169° 12′ 24″ − 27′ 33″ = 168° 44′ 51″ in the middle of the eclipse; col. II takes al-Battānī's 308° 35′ for both true longitudes: the mean Sun 308° 35′ − 1° 25′ 40″ = 307° 9′ 20″, the mean Moon 308° 35′ + 4° 14′ = 312° 49′, 1′ 6″ less than in col. I, and so are the anomaly and the motions in latitude; for its 180° − 168° 43′ 45″ = 11° 16′ 15″ the table of the latitude gives 0° 58′ 32″, the print 0° 58′ 29″. The corrupt numbers of the codex and of Plato are printed in italics; the dots stand where Halley gives no number.
@obs body L39-L40: the numbers are written in Maghribi abjad, in which ص counts 60 and ض 90: «لب» 32, «لز» 37, «قضط» 199, «قصط» 169, «قضح» 198, «قصح» 168. The dot of ب in «لب» and that of ز in «لز» are not printed.
