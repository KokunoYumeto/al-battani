@obs body L1-L3: ATZ = ABZ − BZT and AHZ = ABZ + DZK hold in the plane triangles BZT and BZH, where an exterior angle is the sum of the two opposite interior angles; the bar in L1 (9.7 pt) is a minus sign. By p. 259, L36 ABZ is the angle of latitude, so ATZ and AHZ are corrected angles of latitude, not of longitude, here as in p. 260, L36: al-Battānī's rule (p. 82, L22-L26) takes the variation from the angle of latitude and adds it to the angle of longitude when the corrected zenith distance is the smaller, as for the northern Moon E; (α) and (β) of L8-L9 give the two corrections with ±.
