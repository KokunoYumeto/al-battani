Notes: the Tabula Probata in Ibn Yūnus, corrected after Ḥabash; the Alphonsine Tables and Peurbach after Riccioli; Leverrier's elements for 1850.

(♏ 14° 10′, ♍ 2° 9′, ♋ 16° 40′, ♉ 16° 10′, ♎ 1° 10′, given with the epochs of the planets in the Almagest, books IX–XI). For his own time the Almagest has them 8° 50′ greater (8° 51′ for Jupiter): ♏ 23°, ♍ 11°, ♋ 25° 30′, ♉ 25°, ♎ 10°, i. e. 233°, 161°, 115° 30′, 55° and 190°. Against these al-Battānī's apogees are greater by 11° 28′ for Saturn, Mars and Mercury, by 3° 28′ for Jupiter, exactly 8° less, as he says (p. 239, L17-L19), and by 27° 14′ for Venus. The differences of L4 are right; each is 8° 50′ (8° 51′ for Jupiter) more than these.
