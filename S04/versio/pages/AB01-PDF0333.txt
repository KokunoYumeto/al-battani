% Commentary on chapter 32, b) on p. 68, l. 23 sqq.: the era of the Copts: the three eras so called by the other Arabic writers (Nabonassar, Philip, Diocletian); al-Battānī's era has Julian years from Friday 29 August 25 B.C., 287 Julian years less 3 days after the era of Dhū 'l-qarnayn: a form of the era of Augustus or of Actium, whose epoch Ideler put at Sunday 31 August 30 B.C. with vague years; Theon on the return of 1 Thoth of the vague year to 1 Thoth of the fixed Alexandrian year after 1460 fixed years, in the fifth year of Augustus or five years after the beginning of his reign: the epoch 30 August 26 B.C. (Mommsen, Lepsius, Martin) or 29 August 25 B.C. (Halma, Biot, Letronne, al-Battānī); the Copts three days before the Graeco-Egyptians, two in every fourth year (begun). Notes: the eras in al-Farghānī, Ḥabash, Ibn Yūnus, al-Maqrīzī, al-Mas‘ūdī and the Āyīn-i Akbarī; Petavius and L'art de vérifier les dates; Ptolemy's Canopic inscription; Martin and Schram.
