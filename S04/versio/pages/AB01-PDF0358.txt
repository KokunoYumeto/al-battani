@obs body L5: the formula treats the latitude β of the Moon and the difference in longitude Δλ as the legs of a right triangle, with sines for arcs: sin luminis : R = sin Δλ : √(sin² β + sin² Δλ). In the right spherical triangle with these legs the sine of the hypotenuse, the elongation, has sin² = sin² β + sin² Δλ − sin² β sin² Δλ; without the last term, the arc found is the angle of that triangle at the Moon, opposite Δλ, which is 90° when β = 0.
