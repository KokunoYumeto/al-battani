@obs body L22-L26: the formulas are checked. In the triangle PAB the side PB is 90° − φ, the colatitude of Mekkah, and the angle P is λ − λ′, the difference of longitude; the rule of sines gives sin PAB = sin PB sin P : sin AB = sin (λ − λ′) cos φ : sin distantiae. AC is perpendicular to the meridian PA, so that PAB = PAC + CAB = 90° + azimuth and sin PAB = cos azimuth. The formula of t. I, p. 318, has φ′ in place of φ, as L26 says.
