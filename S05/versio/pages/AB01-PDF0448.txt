@obs body L1: «الاوسط» ends the correction to p. ٩٥, l. 16, begun at the foot of p. XXX: the line reads «يكون بقدر تعديله الأوْسَط». The first numeral is «١١٢»: its third digit has its flag on the right, as ٢. On p. ١١٢, l. 4 the edition prints «على حَدَّته»; the correction «حِدَته» has a kasra under the ḥā' and a fatḥa over the dāl, without a shadda. P. ١٣٧, l. 1 begins with «القمر», for which «سمت القمر» is read; p. ١٦١, l. 3 has «فخُذْ حاصَّة الشمس», for which «القمر» is read (L2).
@obs body L2: on p. ١٨١ the edition prints «المتأَخِّرْون», with a round sukūn over the rā', on the eighteenth line of the page as its margin numbers count (with the empty lines about the heading of chapter 50). The correction is printed «المتأَخرون», with the hamza and the fatḥa over the alif and no other sign; a dot (1.0 pt) high over the khā' is a speck. On p. ١٨٩, l. 7 the edition reads «ثمّ الحُوت فيها لد كوكبًا فيها الفَرْغ المُقَدَّم والفَرْغ المُؤَخَّر فجميع...»; «والرِّشَاء», al-Rishā', the twenty-eighth lunar mansion, is to be added after the two Farghs. «المؤَخَّر» has the hamza and fatḥa over the wāw and a shadda with a fatḥa over the khā'; «والرِّشَاء» a shadda with a kasra on the rā' and a fatḥa over the shīn. The stop after it prints small (0.7 pt) and raised.
@obs body L3: on p. ١٩١, l. 3 the edition has «ونَحْوَه», for which «او نحوه» is read. On p. ١٩٨ the words «في الأقاليم» stand on the fifth line of the text, the eighth of the page by its margin numbers.
@obs body L3-L5: on p. ٢٠٢, l. 3-4 the edition reads «...بهذا العَمَل الذي وصَفْتُ لك فما حصل...»; after «الذي وصفت لك» the correction restores «فما كان ٱضربه في ازمان ساعات الجزء التالي», whose alif in «ٱضربه» carries the waṣla, printed as in the Arabic text (for example «ٱعْمِدْ», p. ١٣٧, l. 1).
@obs body L5-L7: on p. ٢١٠, l. 2 the edition reads «...انتهَيْنا في هذا الكتاب الى هذا الموضِع فوصَفْنا الآلة...»; for «فوصفنا» the correction puts the reading of the codex. «كلّ» has a shadda; «يقـدَّم» a shadda with a fatḥa over the dāl, and its elongated join is transcribed with a tatweel; «ذكرُهُ» has a ḍamma over the rā' and over the hā'.
@obs body L7: on p. ٢١٢, l. 13 the edition reads «ثمّ نحيِّز ... حِيزًا»; the correction «نحُزّ ... حَزًّا» agrees with «ثمّ نحُزَّ ... حَزَّيْن», l. 20, and «ونحُزّ», p. ٢١٣, l. 10.
