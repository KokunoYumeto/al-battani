@obs body L4-L15: the incipit (L4-L8), the explicit (L8-L12) and the colophon (L12-L15) of the codex, set right to left inside the Latin lines as printed: «هذا كتاب هرمس» ends L4, L5-L14 hold Arabic only except the parentheses «(cod. sine punctis)» (L5), «(اصع cod.)» (L9), «(السنن cod.)» (L10), whose Latin abbreviation stands at the left of its Arabic word as printed, and «— Ita explicit:» at the left end of L8, after the stop that closes the incipit; L15 begins with «الهجرة النبوية (4)» followed by the Latin. @obs body L5, L6: «المفضَّل» has the shadda with the fatḥa over the ḍād; «يسمَّى» has the shadda with the fatḥa over the mīm; «يُعلم» has the ḍamma over the yā’. The Arabic commas (L6, L11) are printed as turned commas.
@obs body L5, L8-L10, L14, L18: «الا» at the head of «الاوتاد» and «الاربعة» (L8), «الابواب» (L10), «الاول» (L14 and L18) is printed as one sort: the strokes of the lām-alif cross like an X under a forked top, and no separate alif of the article stands before it; the lām-alif of «ثلاثة» (L5), «كلامي», «الكلام» (L9) and «انقلاب» (L10) has the ordinary form.
