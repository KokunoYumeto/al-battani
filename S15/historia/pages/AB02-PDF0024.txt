@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٢و (32r), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio.
@obs H01: the chart is a square of three by three cells whose four corner cells are halved by diagonals running from the outer corners to the central cell; it stands to the right of lines 1-6, which are set to its left, and the central cell is empty. The houses run from the ascendant, Sagittarius, in the top cell. Three entries are written vertically, reading downwards: «زحل ب ل» along the left side of the Scorpio cell, «عطارد يد ك» along the left side of the Aries cell and «الزهرة ح ي» along the right side of the Taurus cell. The numbers are letters (abjad): القوس و = 6, السنبلة يد = 14, الحوت يج = 13, الجوزاء و = 6; the Sun and the Moon و ه = 6;5, Saturn ب ل = 2;30, Jupiter ب = 2, Mars يد = 14, Venus ح ي = 8;10, Mercury يد ك = 14;20, the ascending node و ل = 6;30.
@obs H01, comparator: the commentary's table for Horoscope 1 (p. 89 of the article) gives the values of the text as ascendant 246°, midheaven 164°, Sun 36;5, Moon 214;5, Saturn 212;30, Jupiter 212, Mars 104, Venus 48;7 (marked as departing from the edition, which has 8;10 Taurus, 38;10, while the manuscript can be read as 18;7), Mercury 14;20 and node 36;30, and proposes to correct the Moon to 216;5. The printed chart agrees with these values except for the Moon: its first letter has the form of the و of «القوس و» and «الجوزاء و» (6;5, that is 216;5), where the table reads د (4;5).
@obs H01: the cusp of the lower midheaven, Pisces 13 (يج), is printed one degree short of the opposite point of the midheaven, Virgo 14 (يد).
@obs H01, L07: the chart agrees with the latitude of the Moon in the text: the Moon at Scorpio 6;5 and the ascending node at Taurus 6;30 give an argument of latitude of 179;35, and with an inclination of 5 degrees a latitude of 0;2,11 to the north, the «ب» of L07 (2 minutes); the Sun at Taurus 6;5 stands opposite the Moon.
@obs L08, L11, L12: the latitudes are written in letters: Saturn «د مه» 4;45, Jupiter «ا ن» 1;50, Mars «ا» (L09) 1, Venus «هـ ن» 5;50, Mercury «ا ن» 1;50; «هـ» is the letter ه with the tail by which the print marks it as a number.
@obs A02: the shadda with fatha of «تشاكلهنَّ» is printed after the nūn, above the full stop.
