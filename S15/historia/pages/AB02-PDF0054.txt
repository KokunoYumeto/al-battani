@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٩ظ (39v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio. «الشمسُ الطالعُ» is printed with dammas.
@obs L01, L03, L04, L07, L09, L13: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L03-L05: from 'Umar's death, 2 November 644 (p. 62), to 27 Haziran 967, which with the years counted from Tishrin I is 27 June 656, a Monday (28 Dhu l-Hijja of the year 35 in the arithmetical Hijra calendar), are 4255 days; 11 lunar years, 11 months and 19 days are 4242 days with mean values. The commentary explains the figure as the interval from 'Uthman's accession on 28 Dhu l-Hijja 23 to 'Ali's on 17 Dhu l-Hijja 35 (p. 120 of the article). The year ظسز, 967, is printed without a line above it.
@obs L07: «وكان القران الخامس» is set at the end of the line in the larger and heavier type of the headings; it begins the sentence that continues on L08.
@obs L08-L13: the positions of Horoscope 17 of the article: ascendant Capricorn without a degree, midheaven Libra 12, Sun Cancer 17;20, Moon Capricorn 17;20, Saturn Cancer 14 (the apparatus gives the manuscript's مد, 44), Jupiter Cancer 16;20, Mars «at the end of Virgo and the beginning of Libra», Venus Gemini 5;24, Mercury Leo 14;30; the terminus Gemini. The commentary gives these values (p. 121 of the article: 271 for the ascendant, 192, 107;20, 287;20, 104, 106;20, 180, 65;24, 134;30).
@obs L09: «the seventh of Tammuz» of the year 961 is 7 July 650; the commentary finds the lunar eclipse in Oppolzer's canon on 18 July 650 and recomputes the horoscope for 8 July 650, 17:28 hours after midnight (pp. 120-121 of the article).
@obs H17: the chart stands to the left of L12-L17, which are set to its right, the opposite of the charts on the preceding pages. The houses run from the ascendant, Capricorn, in the top cell, which also holds the Moon, Capricorn 17;20; the central cell is empty. Venus stands in the Libra cell, «الزهرة هـ كد», where the text has Gemini 5;24, as the apparatus notes. The Sun is printed «الشمس ز ك», Cancer 7;20, where the text (L10) has 17;20 (يز ك); the apparatus has no entry for it. Mars stands in the Virgo triangle without a degree; the Libra cell gives no degree for the midheaven. Saturn (Cancer 14), Jupiter (Cancer 16;20) and Mercury (Leo 14;30) agree with the text.
@obs H17, comparator: the commentary notes that the degree of the midheaven is not in the diagram and that the diagram has Venus at 185;24, in Libra (p. 121 of the article); it does not mention the Sun's ز ك.
