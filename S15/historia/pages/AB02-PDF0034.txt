@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٤ظ (34v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio.
@obs L01: the positions of Horoscope 4 end here: Saturn and Jupiter conjunct in Aquarius 22 (كب), Mars in Aries 11, Venus in Taurus 10, Mercury in Aries 17. The chart on p. 44 agrees for Mars, Venus and Mercury and places Saturn and Jupiter at 22 in Pisces, as the apparatus of p. 44 notes.
@obs L01-L17: the numbers are written in letters with a line above them: كب = 22, يا = 11, ي = 10, يز = 17, ظلجـ = 933.
@obs L04, L06, L07, L09, L10, L11, L13: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L05: «والمريخ» is printed without the shadda that the word has elsewhere on the page.
@obs L15: «الاثنين» is printed without the hamza that p. 40 gives it («الأثنين»).
@obs L15-L17: 20 September (Aylul) 622 of the Julian calendar was a Monday; in the arithmetical Hijra calendar whose first day is Friday 16 July 622 it is 8 Rabi' I of the year 1, as the text says. The commentary discusses the year 933 «which begins with Aylul» (p. 128 of the article).
@obs L17: «فكان نزوله قُباء» is printed without a preposition before قُباء; the sentence continues on p. 48.
