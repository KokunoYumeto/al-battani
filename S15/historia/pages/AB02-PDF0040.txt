@obs L01, L02, L09: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L06-L07: the heading is printed in two centred lines in the heavier type, and the editors count both: ١٠ stands in both margins beside L10. The year ظما, 941, has a line above it.
@obs L08: the line is printed with a paragraph indent.
@obs H08: the chart of Horoscope 8 stands to the right of L12-L17, which are set to its left. The houses run from the ascendant, Capricorn, in the top cell; the midheaven, Scorpio 3;5 in the text, falls in the eleventh cell, which has no degree. The chart gives the ascendant as 21 (كا), the text as 21;10 (كا ي); Mars stands in the Aquarius triangle without a degree, the text giving 13;30 (يجـ ل); Jupiter is given as Libra 27;30 (كز ل), the text giving 26;30 (كو ل): the apparatus notes these three. Venus, Libra 7;30 (ز ل), is given only in the chart. The Sun (Virgo 6;37), the Moon (Pisces 6;37), Saturn (Scorpio 3;30) and Mercury (Leo 27) agree with the text.
@obs H08, comparator: the commentary recomputes Horoscope 8 for Tuesday 28 August 630, 15:34 hours after midnight, and its column of the text's values gives ascendant 291;10, midheaven 213;5, Sun 156;37, Moon 336;37, Saturn 213;30, Jupiter 206;30, Mars 313;30, Venus 187;30, Mercury 147 and the terminus Libra (p. 104 of the article), the values of the text and, for Venus, of the chart.
@obs L11-L12: «برج الانتهاء / في الميزان»: the terminus sign is in Libra; the article's translation reads «The terminus sign was in Libra».
@obs L12-L17: the solar eclipse after the conjunction is Horoscope 9 of the article, which has no chart (p. 105 of the article: «no diagram»). Its positions begin here: Sun and Moon in Aquarius 27;27 (كز كز), Saturn in Scorpio 20 (ك), Jupiter in Scorpio 29;30 (كط ل), Mars in Taurus 11 (يا), Venus in Aquarius 15 (يه), Mercury in Pisces; the sentence continues on p. 54.
