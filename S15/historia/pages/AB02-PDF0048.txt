@obs L13-L15: an eighth of the Sun's lesser years, 19, is 2 years and 4;30 months; the text gives the caliphate as 2 years, 3 months and 8 days. From the Prophet's death on 25 May 632 (p. 58) to 10 Ab 945, which with the years counted from Tishrin I is 10 August 634 (9 Jumada II of the year 13 in the arithmetical Hijra calendar), are 807 days; 2 lunar years, 3 months and 8 days are 805 days with mean values. The commentary makes the same comparisons (p. 114 of the article).
