@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٤١و (41r), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio.
@obs L03, L05, L09, L13, L16: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L04-L05: from the eclipse of 'Ali's accession, 21 October 655 (p. 68), to his death in January 661 are five years; five signs from Aries, the sign of the eclipse (Aries 29;43, «about the beginning of Taurus»), reach Virgo, where Saturn stands in that eclipse (Virgo 23, p. 70). The commentary makes the same count (p. 125 of the article).
@obs L06: 24 Kanun II 972, with the years counted from Tishrin I, is 24 January 661 (17 Ramadan of the year 40 in the arithmetical Hijra calendar). From 16 June 656, 17 Dhu l-Hijja 35, the date of 'Ali's accession that the commentary gives (p. 124 of the article), to 24 January 661 are 1683 days; 4 lunar years and 9 months, the caliphate of p. 70 (L13), are 1683 days with mean values.
@obs L07: the heading is centred and set in the heavier type. L08 is printed with a paragraph indent.
@obs L09: 22 Kanun I 972 is 22 December 660, a Tuesday; the commentary gives the horoscope for Tuesday 22 December 660, 14:24 hours after midnight, and notes that the death followed the eclipse by about a month (p. 125 of the article).
@obs L10-L13: the positions of Horoscope 19: ascendant Taurus 20, midheaven Aquarius 8, Sun Capricorn 4;6, Moon Cancer 4;6, Saturn Scorpio 21, Jupiter Taurus 26, Mars Pisces 12, Venus Capricorn 29;30, Mercury Capricorn 10;30; the terminus Aries. The commentary gives these values for the text (p. 126 of the article: 50, 308, 274;6, 94;6, 231, 56, 342, 299;30, 280;30).
@obs H19: the chart stands to the left of L12-L17, which are set to its right. The houses run from the ascendant, Taurus 20, in the top cell; the central cell is empty. Jupiter, Taurus 26 in the text, is not in the chart. «عطارد ى ل» is written vertically, reading downwards, along the right side of the Capricorn triangle, with the yā' printed without its dots; the ل of «الزهرة كط ل» touches the diagonal, and the و of «القمر د و» stands on the left frame of the chart. The other numbers agree with the text.
@obs L13: «الحملل» is a misprint for «الحمل»; the apparatus has no entry for it, and the article's translation reads «Aries».
@obs L17: the line is set in a smaller type than the lines around it.
