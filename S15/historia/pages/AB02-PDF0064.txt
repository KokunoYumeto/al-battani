@obs H20: the chart of Horoscope 20, whose positions are given on p. 74 (L13-L15), stands at the head of the page, to the right of L01-L06, which are set to its left. The folio of the manuscript, ٤٢و (42r), is printed in the right margin beside the chart at the height of L01; the stroke at the head of L01 marks the beginning of the folio.
@obs H20: the houses run from the ascendant, Sagittarius 10, in the top cell; the central cell is empty. In the Capricorn triangle the م of «المرّيخ كط م» is raised beside the diagonal, and «عطارد يجـ مز» is written vertically, reading downwards, along the right side of the square. In the Aquarius triangle Saturn's number «كجـ لجـ» stands above «زحل», and «المشترى كز يد» is written vertically, reading upwards, along the left side of the square; no dots are visible under its final yā', which touches the frame. The chart gives Jupiter as Aquarius 27;14 (كز يد), where the text has 23;14 (كجـ يد), as the apparatus notes; the commentary notes the same (p. 128 of the article). Venus, Capricorn 0;5 in the text, is not in the chart. The other numbers agree with the text: midheaven Virgo 16, lower midheaven Pisces 16, descendant Gemini 10, Sun Capricorn 24;11, Moon Cancer 24;11, Saturn Aquarius 23;33, Mars Capricorn 29;40, Mercury Capricorn 13;47.
@obs L01: the sentence begins on p. 74: «وشارف المرّيخ / أوّل الدلو», Mars approached the beginning of Aquarius (Capricorn 29;40).
@obs L02: the square brackets of «[ فيه ]» are the editors', printed with spaces inside them; the apparatus has no entry for them.
@obs L09, L10: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L10-L11: nine signs on from Cancer, the sign of the eclipse of Horoscope 20 (the Moon in Cancer 24;11), the tenth year reaches Aries, where Mars stands in the eclipse of Mu'awiya's death (L16, Aries 20).
@obs L12: the heading is centred and set in the heavier type. L13 is printed with a paragraph indent.
@obs L13-L14: the year ظصا, 991; 22 Kanun I 991, with the years counted from Tishrin I, is 22 December 679. The commentary identifies the lunar eclipse of 23 December 679, 1:38 hours after midnight, in Oppolzer's canon and recomputes Horoscope 21 for 23 December 679, 4:01 hours after midnight (p. 130 of the article).
@obs L14-L17: the positions of Horoscope 21, which has no chart: ascendant «the end of Scorpio», midheaven «the beginning of Virgo», Sun Capricorn 4;5, Moon Cancer 4;5, Saturn Cancer 14;20, Jupiter Sagittarius 29;45, Mars Aries 20, Venus Capricorn 12, Mercury Capricorn 22; the terminus Scorpio. The commentary gives the text's values as 239, 150, 274;5, 94;5, 104;20, 269;45, 20, 282 and 292 and notes «no diagram» (p. 130 of the article).
