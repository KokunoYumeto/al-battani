@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٨ظ (38v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio. The heading «قيام عمر بن الخطّاب» is set at the head of the line in the larger and heavier type of the headings, followed by the text.
@obs L01, L02, L06, L08, L12, L13, L14: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L02-L05: the positions of Horoscope 14 of the article: ascendant Taurus 10, midheaven Aquarius 5, Sun Gemini 27;47, Moon Sagittarius 27;20, printed «كزك» without a space, Saturn Sagittarius 17;20, printed «يزك», Jupiter Pisces 14, Mars Aries 21;30, Venus Taurus 11;30, Mercury Gemini 10. The apparatus gives the manuscript's يز, 17, for the Moon's كز. The commentary says that the manuscript has 17;20 Sagittarius for the Moon in both the text and the diagram and that the edition corrected it to 27;20 in both, and that the diagram gives Mercury as 73 (p. 115 of the article). The commentary recomputes the eclipse for Friday 17 June 634, 2:21 hours after midnight (p. 115).
@obs H14: the chart stands to the right of L06-L11, which are set to its left; the number ١٠ in the right margin stands beside the chart at the height of L10. The houses run from the ascendant, Taurus 10, in the top cell; the central cell is empty. The chart gives Mercury as Gemini 13 (يجـ), where the text has 10 (ي), as the apparatus notes; Venus is not in the chart. The other numbers agree with the text; the lower midheaven is Leo 5 (الأسد هـ), opposite the midheaven Aquarius 5. The diagonal of the Gemini triangle runs through the end of «يجـ» and of «الجوزاء».
@obs L06-L07: the year's end from the eclipse falls in Aries, «the sign of the terminus from the ascendant of the Prophet»: from Capricorn 20 (p. 42), 63 years from April 571 to June 634 give five turns and three signs, Aries. The commentary makes the same count (p. 116 of the article).
@obs L09-L14: Saturn is in the seventh sign from the Sun counting both: Gemini 27;47 to Sagittarius 17;20; the opposition of Saturn, Gemini 17;20, the Sun has passed by 10;27. Half the lesser years of the Sun, 19, is 9;30 years, and half the lesser years of Jupiter, 12, taken as months, adds 6 months: 10 years; the text gives the caliphate as 10 years, 6 months and 19 days (تسعة عشر).
@obs L13-L15: from Abu Bakr's death, 10 August 634 (p. 60), to 2 Tishrin II 956, which with the years counted from Tishrin I is 2 November 644 (25 Dhu l-Hijja of the year 23 in the arithmetical Hijra calendar), are 3737 days; 10 lunar years, 6 months and 19 days are 3740 days with mean values, 17 days 3738. The commentary says that the number of days «can easily be read as 17», and the article's translation prints «[seven]teen days» (pp. 63 and 116 of the article).
