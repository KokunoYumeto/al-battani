@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٤٢ظ (42v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio. The sentence begins on p. 76: «والكسوف / موضع المرّيخ في الأصل», the eclipse (the Moon in Cancer 4;5) at the place of Mars in the original horoscope (Cancer 14, p. 36).
@obs L01, L02, L05, L06, L07, L08, L09: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L03: the line is printed with a paragraph indent.
@obs L05-L07: a fifth of the Sun's lesser years, 19, is 3 years and 9;36 months; the text gives Yazid's reign as three years and three months. From 30 April 680 (24 Rajab 60, the date the commentary takes for the end of Mu'awiya's reign, p. 74) to 11 November 683 are 1290 days; 3 lunar years and 3 months are 1152 days and 3 years and 8 months 1299 days with mean values. The commentary cites 3 years and 8 months from Battani's zij and suggests a confusion of the abjad letters for 3 and 8 behind the written number (p. 131 of the article).
@obs L07-L08: 11 Tishrin II 995, with the years counted from Tishrin I, is 11 November 683 (15 Rabi' I of the year 64 in the arithmetical Hijra calendar); the commentary gives the same equivalents (p. 131 of the article). The year ظصه, 995, has a line above it.
@obs L08: three signs on from Cancer, the sign of the eclipse of Yazid's accession (the Moon in Cancer 4;5, p. 76), reach Libra, opposite Mars in Aries 20.
@obs L12: the closing formula is centred, in the ordinary type and without a line number; it is the last line of the text. The space between L11 and L12 is about twice the distance between two lines.
