@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٩و (39r), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio.
@obs L01-L03: the solar eclipse half a lunar month before the eclipse of 'Umar's accession is Horoscope 15 of the article, without a chart: Sun and Moon in Gemini 12;3 (يب جـ), ascendant Sagittarius 4 (د), midheaven 23 (كجـ) without the name of a sign. The commentary gives these values and says that the rest of the horoscope is missing (pp. 117-118 of the article).
@obs L01, L02, L03, L06, L11: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L04: the heading is centred and set in the heavier type. L05 is printed with a paragraph indent; the square brackets of «[و]قيام» are the editors', without an entry in the apparatus.
@obs L06-L09: the positions of Horoscope 16: ascendant Leo 7;9, midheaven Taurus 18, Sun Gemini 7;41, Saturn Aries 23;45, Jupiter Capricorn 6;30, Mars Gemini 18;40, Venus Taurus 9, Mercury Gemini 22;20, whose number is divided between the lines: «كب» ends L08 and «ك» begins L09, each under its own line. The text gives no place for the Moon (the apparatus: «سقطت»).
@obs H16: the chart stands to the right of L08-L13, which are set to its left; the number ١٠ in the right margin stands beside the chart at the height of L10. The houses run from the ascendant in the top cell, which gives Leo 9 (ط) where the text has 7;9, as the apparatus notes; the central cell is empty. The Moon, Sagittarius 7;41, stands only in the chart, written across the diagonal of the Sagittarius triangle; Mars is not in the chart. In the Taurus cell the midheaven is printed «الثوريح», 18 joined to the name of the sign, followed by «و» on a line of its own and «الزهرة ط». The other numbers agree with the text; the lower midheaven is Scorpio 18 and the descendant Aquarius 9.
@obs H16, comparator: the commentary recomputes Horoscope 16 for Thursday 27 May 644, 11:08 hours after midnight; it notes the ascendant 9 Leo of the diagram, takes the Moon from the diagram and notes that Mars is not in it (p. 118 of the article), and finds the ascendant and the midheaven of the text incompatible at Mecca (p. 119).
@obs L12-L13: Saturn, Aries 23;45, is 43;56 behind the Sun, Gemini 7;41, and has not reached the sextile, 60 degrees.
@obs L14-L16: Mars, Gemini 18;40, follows the Sun, Gemini 7;41, by 10;59, «about eleven parts»; with the obliquity 23;35 their right ascensions differ by 11;47, «about eleven parts and two thirds of a degree». The commentary computes the same arc, 11;47, and reads it as the eleven and two thirds years of 'Uthman's caliphate (p. 119 of the article).
