@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٥ظ (35v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio.
@obs L01: the positions of Horoscope 7 (the transfer of the year of the nativity, begun on p. 48) end here: Jupiter in Pisces 1;55 (ا نه), Mars in Leo 3 (جـ), Venus in Aries 26;7 (كو ز), Mercury in Taurus 8;20 (ح ك). The ح of Mercury's degree is the letter ح (8), in the form of the text, not the flat sign printed for zero on pp. 42 and 48.
@obs H07: the chart stands to the right of L04-L08, which are set to its left. The houses run from the ascendant, Taurus, in the top cell. In that cell the Sun is written «افتاب», its Persian name, without a degree; Mercury is not in the chart. Jupiter is given in the chart as Pisces 11;15 (يا يه), in the text (L01) as 1;55 (ا نه); the apparatus has no entry for this. Saturn is given as Cancer 22;30 (كب ل), in the text (p. 48, L17) as 22;20 (كب ك), as the apparatus notes. The Moon (Gemini 13;30), Mars (Leo 3) and Venus (Aries 26;7) agree with the text; the ل of «القمر يجـ ل» touches the diagonal of its cell.
@obs H07, comparator: the commentary (p. 101 of the article) notes that the diagram gives the Sun by its Persian name aftāb without a longitude and omits Mercury; its column of the text's values gives Jupiter as 341;15 (Pisces 11;15), the value of the chart, «probably a scribal mistake for 1;15º or 1;55º».
@obs L05, L06, L08, L09, L10, L11, L12, L13, L16: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L10-L12: half the lesser years of the Sun, 19, is 9;30 years; Jupiter adds its lesser years, 12, as months, one year; together 10;30 years, «about ten and a half years» from the eclipse of Horoscope 6 (February 622, p. 48) to the Prophet's death, which L16 places in the year 11 of the Hijra.
@obs L17: the sentence continues on p. 52.
