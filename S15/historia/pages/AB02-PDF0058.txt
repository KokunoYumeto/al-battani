@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٤٠ظ (40v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio. No comma is printed between «يط ك» and «الزهرة».
@obs L01-L02: the positions of Horoscope 18 end here: Saturn Virgo 23 (the line ends «زحل في» on p. 68), Jupiter Sagittarius 10, Mars Leo 19;20, Venus Libra 6;20, Mercury Scorpio 6;20; the terminus Scorpio. The commentary gives the text's values as 184, 94, 209;43, 29;43, 173, 250, 139;20, 186;20 and 216;20 and recomputes the eclipse for Wednesday 21 October 655, 4:27 hours after midnight (p. 123 of the article).
@obs L02, L03, L04, L05, L09, L12, L13, L16, L17: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs H18: the chart stands to the right of L05-L10, which are set to its left. The houses run from the ascendant, Libra 4, in the top cell; the central cell is empty. The chart gives the Sun and the Moon as 29;13 (كط يجـ), where the text has 29;43 (كط مجـ), and Venus as Libra 10;20 (ي ك), where the text has 6;20 (و ك), as the apparatus notes. The other numbers agree with the text: midheaven Cancer 4, lower midheaven Capricorn 4, descendant Aries 4, Saturn Virgo 23, Jupiter Sagittarius 10, Mars Leo 19;20, Mercury Scorpio 6;20. The commentary notes the same three departures of the diagram (p. 123 of the article).
@obs L04: «وموضع القمر في الدلو»: the Moon is in Aries 29;43 in the text (p. 68, L17) and in the chart; the commentary finds the remark «difficult to understand» (p. 124 of the article).
@obs L11-L13: the commentary takes the years given here as Venus's lesser years, eight, and notes that they do not agree with the four years and nine months of 'Ali's caliphate; it finds the four years and nine months exact from 17 Dhu l-Hijja 35 to 17 Ramadan 40 (p. 124 of the article).
@obs L11: «وقرانَه» is printed with a fatha on the nūn.
@obs L14-L15: the number ١٥ stands in the right margin beside L15 and in the left margin beside L14.
@obs L17: the sentence continues on p. 72.
