@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٣و (33r), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio.
@obs L01, L04, L07, L09: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle or near the end of these lines.
@obs L11: «تدبيرالكسوف» is printed without the space between the two words.
@obs L13: the line is printed with a paragraph indent.
@obs L15: after «وشرحها .» the rest of the line, «وكان مولد النبيّ ، صلّى اللّه عليه وسلّم،», is set in the larger and heavier type of the headings; it begins the sentence that continues on L16. The article's translation gives it as a heading.
@obs L16-L17: six days before the eclipse of the night before Sunday 26 April 571 is Monday 20 April 571 of the Julian calendar; «the night whose morning was Monday, the twentieth night of Nisan» is the night before that Monday.
@obs A01: no separator is printed between the entries «١٦ - العشرون : العشرين» and «١٧ - ثمان ... وثمنين»; the lemma of the second is printed «وثمنين», while L17 reads «وثمانين».
@obs A02: «تحت الشطر» is printed with ش, for «تحت السطر» (under the line), as the apparatus has it on pp. 20 and 36; the article's translation says that the number is repeated under the word in Indian numerals.
