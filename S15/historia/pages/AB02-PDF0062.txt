@obs L04: «وخمسة وعشرين» stands without «يومًا»; the article's translation supplies «(days)».
@obs L04-L05: the reign ends «on the thirtieth of Nisan of the year ظعب», 972, the year of the eclipse of Mu'awiya's accession (p. 72); 30 Nisan 972, with the years counted from Tishrin I, is 30 April 661. The commentary says that the manuscript's year can be read as 972 or 992, takes the year 991 from Battani's zij and the further data, and gives 30 April 680 = 24 Rajab 60 (p. 127 of the article). 19 lunar years, 3 months and 25 days, 6847 days with mean values, counted back from 30 April 680 reach 1 August 661.
@obs L08: the line is printed with a paragraph indent.
@obs L11: «وكان القران السادس في أيّام معاوية» is set at the end of the line in the larger and heavier type of the headings; it begins the sentence that continues on L12.
@obs L12: the year ظف, 980; 12 Kanun II 980, with the years counted from Tishrin I, is 12 January 669. The commentary identifies the lunar eclipse of 12 January 670 and takes 980 to be a mistake for 981 (p. 128 of the article).
