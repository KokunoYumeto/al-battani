@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٣ظ (33v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio. The print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of the line.
@obs L04-L07, L12, L14, L16: the numbers of the text are written in letters (abjad) with a line above them: جـ = 3, ك = 20, ب = 2, يا ل = 11;30, ز = 7, ح = 8, سا = 61. The chart has no lines above its numbers.
@obs L05: the line above «{0} كه» covers a sign shaped like a small ح with a flat top, set before كه (25). The commentary reads the Sun at 0;25 Taurus, taking the sign as zero, and notes that the text can also be read as 5;25 Taurus (p. 93 of the article); 0;25 Taurus is the value of the nativity transfers of Horoscopes 7, 10 and 12. The sign is transcribed {0}; it differs in form from the ح (8) of «سهم السعادة في الثور ح» on L06.
@obs H02: the chart stands to the right of lines 8-13, which are set to its left. The houses run from the ascendant, Capricorn 20, in the top cell; the central cell reads «طالع مولد النبي صلعم». The entry «الشمس لو يجـ» is written vertically, reading downwards, along the left side of the Taurus cell. Its numerals print لو: the letter before و has the tall upright stroke of ل, which reaches the frame of the chart; لو يجـ is 36;13, not a degree within a sign. The commentary gives the value of the chart as 16;13 in Taurus, 46;13, reading يو (p. 93 of the article). The other numbers of the chart agree with the text: ascendant Capricorn 20, Saturn and Jupiter Scorpio 3, Mercury Aries 7, Venus Taurus 11;30, Moon Leo 2; Mars, Cancer 2 (ب), is given only in the chart.
@obs L10: the line is printed with a paragraph indent.
@obs L10-L17: two of the three directions recompute to about 61 degrees, the 61 years from the birth in 571 to the death in 632: the ascendant, Capricorn 20, to the quartile of Mars, Aries 2, is 60.6 degrees of oblique ascension at Mecca (latitude 21;40, obliquity 23;35; 60.7 degrees at 21;25); the degree of the conjunction, Scorpio 3, to the opposition of Mars, Capricorn 2, is 61.4 degrees of right ascension. The third, from the Moon by the descensions of the clime according to the Moon's place from the horizon, has not been recomputed.
@obs L16: the line is set in a smaller type than the lines around it, so that the whole sentence fits it; the words in square brackets on L15 and L16 are the editors' corrections, which the apparatus explains.
@obs A01: «پيغمبر» is printed with the Persian letter پ; the marginal note in Persian reads «the age of our Prophet: 61 (سا)»; «ما سا» is set in bold.
