@obs L16: the year is printed «طسز», with ط (9), in the text and in the apparatus, which gives the manuscript's طس. The year of 'Uthman's death on p. 66 (L05) is printed ظسز, with ظ (900): 967. 21 Tishrin I 967, with the years counted from Tishrin I, is 21 October 655; the article's translation reads «in year 967», and the commentary identifies the eclipse of 21 October 655 in Oppolzer's canon (p. 123 of the article).
