@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٦ظ (36v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio. «ط ل», 9;30, ends the positions of Horoscope 9 begun on p. 52: Mercury in Pisces 9;30. The square brackets of «شبا[ط]» are the editors'; the apparatus has no entry for them.
@obs L01: the date of the solar eclipse is the night of 22 Shubat of the year ظمب, 942; with the years counted from Tishrin I, the count with which the dates of pp. 46 and 58 agree (p. 58, L03), it is 22 February 631. The commentary gives the date as «22 Shubāṭ, 941 Byzantine = 22 February, 631», finds no solar eclipse on that day and recomputes the horoscope for 7 February 631 (p. 105 of the article).
@obs L02: «سنة س», the sixtieth year: the transfer of the year in April 630 (the commentary's date for Horoscope 10, Thursday 19 April 630, p. 106 of the article) falls 59 years after the nativity of 20 April 571 (Horoscope 2).
@obs L02, L03, L08, L10, L11, L16, L17: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L03: the Sun's number is the sign printed for zero and ك, 0;20, under one line; the zero sign touches the ر of «الثور».
@obs L04: Saturn's number is printed as the sign for zero followed by د, under one line; read as the numbers on pp. 42 and 48 and on L03 (degrees first), it is 0;4. The chart gives «زحل د», Scorpio 4, and the commentary's column of the text's values gives Saturn 214, Scorpio 4 (p. 107 of the article).
@obs H10: the chart stands to the right of L04-L09, which are set to its left; the number ٥ in the right margin stands beside the chart at the height of L05. The houses run from the ascendant, Aries 10, in the top cell; the central cell is empty. In the chart the Sun is «الشمس{0} ي», 0;10 Taurus, the sign for zero touching the س, where the text (L03) has 0;20; Venus is Aries 27;9 (كز ط), the text (L06) 27;20 (كز ك); the midheaven is Capricorn 7 (الجدي ز), the text (L03) 6 (الجدي و): the apparatus notes the last two. Mars, 24;40, stands with Jupiter, Libra 23;40, in the Libra cell.
@obs L05: the edition prints «المرّيخ في الجدي»; the apparatus gives الميزان as the reading of the manuscript, and the chart places Mars in Libra. L09-L10 put Jupiter, the lord of the terminus from the ascendant, «in the sign of the terminus from the midheaven of the nativity», Libra (L08), «with Mars». The commentary says that both the text and the diagram place Mars in Libra (204;40) instead of Capricorn and recomputes Mars at 292;09 and 293;38 (p. 107 of the article); the article's translation reads «Mars in Libra 24;40».
@obs L07-L09: the termini: from the ascendant of the nativity (Capricorn 20, p. 42) the terminus of the sixtieth year is Sagittarius, from its midheaven (Scorpio) Libra; 59 years complete four turns of twelve signs and eleven signs more, one sign short of five turns. The commentary gives the same count (p. 107 of the article).
@obs L12: «حالٍ» and «محمودةٍ» are printed with kasratayn.
