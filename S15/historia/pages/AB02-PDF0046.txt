@obs L01: the folio of the manuscript, ٣٧ظ (37v), is printed in the right margin; the stroke at the head of the line marks the beginning of the folio. The square brackets of «[أن]» are the editors'; the apparatus explains them.
@obs L01-L02: from the arrival at Medina, 20 September 622 (p. 46), to 25 May 632 (L03) are 3535 days; 9 lunar years, 11 months and 22 days are 3536 days with the mean year of 354;22 days and the mean month of 29;31,50 days. In the arithmetical Hijra calendar the two dates are 8 Rabi' I of the year 1 and 28 Safar of the year 11, 9 years, 11 months and 20 days apart.
@obs L03: 25 Ayyar 943, with the years counted from Tishrin I (the year 943 running from October 631 to September 632), is 25 May 632, a Monday, as the text says; the commentary recomputes Horoscope 11 for Monday 25 May 632 (p. 111 of the article). With the same count, 20 Aylul 933 (p. 46) is 20 September 622 in the last month of the year 933, and 22 Shubat 942 (p. 54) is 22 February 631. The words «ابتداؤها ايلول» on p. 46 make Aylul the first month of the year 933; counted so, with 20 Aylul 933 in 622, 25 Ayyar 943 would be 25 May 633, a Tuesday.
@obs L03-L05: the positions of the day of the death (Horoscope 11 of the article, without a chart): Sun Gemini 5;25 (هـ كه), Moon Gemini 11, Saturn Scorpio 27, Jupiter Capricorn 1;30, Mars Pisces 15;30, Venus Cancer 15;40, Mercury Cancer 1; the ascendant, Leo, is named on L16. The commentary gives the same values for the text (p. 111 of the article).
@obs L01, L02, L03, L04, L05, L10, L17: the print leaves a wide space after the full stop in the middle of these lines.
@obs L06-L08: the positions of Horoscope 12: ascendant Libra 4, midheaven Cancer 4, Sun Taurus 0;25 ({0}كه, the sign printed for zero touching the ر of «الثور»), Moon Aquarius 15;20, Saturn Scorpio 29;20, Jupiter Capricorn 3;20, Mars Aquarius 18;40, Venus Gemini 3;10, Mercury Aries 27. «سنة اثنتين وستين»: the transfer of April 632 falls 61 years after the nativity of April 571.
@obs H12: the chart stands to the right of L11-L16, which are set to its left; the number ١٥ in the right margin stands beside the chart at the height of L15. The houses run from the ascendant, Libra 4, in the top cell; the central cell is empty. The chart gives the Moon as Aquarius 18 (يح), Saturn as Scorpio 29;30 (كط ل), Jupiter as Capricorn 4;10 (د ي), Mars as Aquarius 14;30 (يد ل) and Venus as Gemini 14;10 (يد ي), where the text has 15;20, 29;20, 3;20, 18;40 and 3;10: the apparatus notes all five. The Sun, «الشمس{0} كه», 0;25 Taurus, has the sign for zero touching the س; the ascendant, the midheaven (Cancer 4) and Mercury (Aries 27) agree with the text. The ح of «القمر يح» touches the diagonal of its cell.
@obs H12, comparator: the commentary (pp. 111-112 of the article) gives the text's values as above and the diagram's as Moon 318, Saturn 239;30, Jupiter 274;10, Mars 314;30 and Venus 74;10; it notes that both in the text and in the diagram the Sun may also be read as 5;25 Taurus.
@obs L09: «برج الانتهاء من طالع التحويل الدلو»: Aquarius is the terminus of the sixty-second year counted from the ascendant of the nativity, Capricorn 20 (p. 42): 61 signs on, five turns and one sign; counted from the ascendant of the transfer, Libra, it would be Scorpio. The commentary takes the terminus as counted from the ascendant of the nativity (p. 112 of the article).
@obs L16: Leo is the eighth sign from Capricorn, the ascendant of the nativity, counting Capricorn as the first.
