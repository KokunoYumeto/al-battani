% Printed p. 129: the heading and the title of chapter 41, on the sighting of the new moon at the beginnings and the ends of the months, the azimuth of the place where it is seen, its height and lowness, the shape of its figure according to the light in it, and the levelness of its two horns and their inclination from the zodiac; the beginning of the chapter: the knowledge of the sighting of the new moon is among the most useful things, since the era of the Arabs and the beginnings of their months follow the sighting of the new moons; it is hard to know exactly, because of the moon's nearness to and distance from the sun and the earth, its northern and southern latitude, its parallax in longitude and latitude in every place, the shortness and length of the rising and setting times of the signs in the climates, and the amount of light in it; hence the error of some people of the author's time, who imagined that the distance of a star from the equator and its latitude are taken from one arc, and that the moon's parallax does not come from its difference in the circle of altitude; the ancients were not forced to this knowledge, since their era and their reckoning went by the years of the sun, and they mentioned it only in general terms, saying that the new moon cannot be seen at less than a day and a night; the amount of the sighting found by observations can be known only approximately. 19 lines, two notes in one line; the printer's signature 17 at the foot.

الشمس لأنّ اوائل الشهور القمريّة عندهم معلومة بأوقات الاجتماعات التي يدُلّ على حقيقتها الحِسَاب
ولذلك ما أَلْقَوْا ذِكْرَه مع كَثْرة ما يعرِض فيه ممّا ذكرنا إِلّا بالقَوْل المُطْلَق فإِنّهم ذكَروا أنّه
لا يُمْكِن أن يُرَى الهِلال لأَقَلَّ من يوم وليلة واذا تُقُصِّيَتْ أَسْبَاب الرُّؤْية وُجِدَ هذا{n:1} القَوْل هو
