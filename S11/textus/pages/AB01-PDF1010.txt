% Printed p. 142: chapter 42 continued: the hours of the true conjunction or opposition. The hours of the difference are added to the hours of the mean conjunction found from the tables if the excess is the sun's, and taken from them if it is the moon's: the result is the absolute hours of the true conjunction; if they are more than 24, twenty-four hours are taken away and a day is added to the elapsed days of the month; if the hours of the difference are to be taken away and are more than the hours of the mean conjunction, a day is taken from the elapsed days of the month, reckoned as 24 hours and added to the hours of the mean conjunction, and then the hours of the difference are taken away: the result is the equal hours elapsed after noon at the city of ar-Raqqa of the day found. The hours of the difference are multiplied by the motion of the sun and by the motion of the moon in an hour: if the excess is the sun's, the products are added to the place of the sun, to the place of the moon and to the motion in latitude, and to the latter the motion of the northern node in those hours as well; if the excess is the moon's, they are taken away instead: so the places of the sun and the moon are made right for that time. An approximate way: half a sixth of the difference between the sun and the moon is added to the sun, and the whole difference with half its sixth to the moon and to the motion in latitude, if the excess is the sun's, or taken from them if it is the moon's: they then come out in one minute; the whole difference and half its sixth divided by the moon's unequal motion in an hour gives the hours of the difference, used as before; but the first way is the more correct. The anomaly of the moon for the time of the conjunction or opposition must also be known: the hours of the difference are entered in the table of the hours, the motion of the moon's anomaly in them is taken and added to the corrected anomaly of the moon if the excess is the sun's, or taken from it if it is the moon's: the result is the corrected anomaly of the moon for the time of the conjunction, if the reckoning is for a conjunction, or for the time of the opposition [...]. 22 lines; two notes in one line.
