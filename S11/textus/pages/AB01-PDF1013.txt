% Printed p. 139: chapter 42 continued: the reckoning of the mean conjunctions and oppositions from the tables. The like of the years is sought in the row of the collected years of the tables of conjunction or of opposition; where the same number or the nearest smaller one is found, what stands beside it is taken from the four tables, for the days, the mean place of the sun and the moon, the anomaly of the moon and the motion in latitude; the years that remain over those found are entered in the row of the single years, and what stands beside them in the four tables is written down, each kind under its kind; then what stands beside the complete month before the month of the reckoning, of the days of the lunar months in the first of the four tables, is added to the days from the collected and the single years. If the sum is more than the number of the Rumi days written under that complete month and less than the days under the month of the reckoning, those days and what stands under them in the three other tables are written down; if it is more than the Rumi days under the month of the reckoning, the days beside the month before the complete month are taken with what stands under them in the three other tables. Each of the four tables is summed and written as before; then the Rumi days beside the complete month before the month of the reckoning are taken from the days of the three tables: what remains of days and minutes is the days elapsed of the month and the equal hours after noon of the elapsed day to the time of the mean conjunction or opposition of the sun and the moon [and from the three other tables come the mean places of the sun and the moon at that time], the anomaly of the moon and the motion in latitude; and at the time of opposition the mean place of the moon is of necessity opposite the mean place of the sun. Then, within ornamental brackets that open on line 20 and close on line 21: to reckon the conjunction and the opposition by the era of the Copts, the years of Dhu l-Qarnayn are taken with the current year, even if only one day of Aylul has entered; 287 is cast out of the years, and of the remainder a quarter is taken [...]. 22 lines; two notes in two lines.
