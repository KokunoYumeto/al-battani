*وقد تَبَيَّنَ بما قد ظهَر انّ بالمقدار{mR:f. 64,v.}
الذي به تكون دائرة القمر التي يُرَى فيها
الضَّوْء كاملًا خَمْسَةَ عَشَرَ فبه يكون كلّ
اثنَيْ عشر جزءًا من بُعْده عن الشمس
جزءًا واحدًا الى تمام {ov:قف} التي تستكمل{mL:5}
الخمس عشرة كلَّها واذا كانت دائرة القمر
{ov:يب} جزءًا كان كلّ خمسة عشر جزءًا من
البعد جزءًا واحدًا من اجزاء الضوء وعلى
هذه النِّسَب رَسَمْنا الدائرتَيْن وبَيَّنَّاهما
إِن شاء اللّٰه تعالى.{mL:10}

@obs Figure F01 (redrawn; its measures and the way they were taken are in the geometry). At the top the circle of the sun (radius 48.5 pt) with the word «الشمس» across the axis, its level diameter with ا at its left end and ج at its right end, both outside the circle, and ب under the diameter at the left of the centre. The upright axis runs from the top of the sun's circle down to the line ك ط of the lowest circle of the moon; on it, 146 pt above that circle's centre, the earth is a filled disc (radius 3.1 pt) with the word «الارض» at its left and the letter ي at its right below it. Ten circles of the moon (radius 24.6 to 25.1 pt) stand side by side, touching or within 0.7 pt of one another, their centres 20 degrees apart on the left half of a circle of 145.0 pt about the earth. In every circle the line ك ط bounds the dark half, which is hatched with thin upright lines and lies away from the sun; in the eight circles between the first and the last a second line, م ل, crosses the whole circle at right angles to the direction of the earth. A dashed arc joins the first circle to the last at the right of the axis, and along it, turned a quarter and read downward, stand the words «وحكم هذا النصف مثل النصف الآخر» (the madda of الآخر is printed; no other signs).
@obs Figure F01, its letters (59 letters and words; those in the dark halves are printed over the hatching). First circle, at the top (the conjunction): in the lit half ك, then ن at the right of the axis, then ط; in the dark half ل under the ك, the word «مظلم» across the axis and م under the ط. Circles 2 to 6: ك at the upper left end of the line ك ط (inside the second circle, outside the others), ط near its right end, د over the centre, م in the lit half by the line م ل and ل in the dark half. Circles 7 to 9: ك outside at the left, ط and د as before, and the two other letters changed about: ل in the lit half and م in the dark half. Last circle, at the foot (the opposition): in the lit half ك and the word «منير» across the axis, in the dark half ل under the ك and ز at the centre, where the axis ends; ط and م stand outside the circle at the right, the ط above the line ك ط and the م under it. The note on p. 93 says that the letters are wanting in the codex and in Plato's version and were restored by Nallino, and that the lines from ا to ك and from ج to ط are left out.
