% Printed p. 149: chapter 43 continued: the end of the exact times and the digits of the eclipse by reckoning. The minutes found are divided by the gain of the moon and, with half their sixth, taken from the motion in latitude for the time of the opposition and also added to it, so that the motion in latitude is known for the two times and from it the latitude of the moon in each; the latitude is taken from the half of the two diameters, the excess of the remainder over the diameter of the moon is multiplied by itself and taken from the whole minutes of the stay multiplied by themselves; to what remains is added the difference between the latitude of the moon for the middle of the eclipse and its latitude at that time, the root of the sum is divided by the gain of the moon: the result for the beginning is taken from the hours of the opposition and the result for the clearing added to them, which gives the hours of the beginning of the stay and of the beginning of the clearing. If the moon is not wholly eclipsed and the digits of the eclipse are to be counted by reckoning, that is the area of the part of the moon's circle that falls in the circle of the shadow, in the measure in which the area of the moon's circle is 12 parts, called digits: from the corrected half diameter of the moon 0;14,50, its half diameter at the farthest distance, is taken away, the remainder is multiplied by six and divided by that farthest half diameter, and the result is added to the six digits of the farthest half diameter: this is the corrected half diameter of the moon in digits; doubled it is the whole diameter, which multiplied by three parts, eight minutes and a half gives the circumference of the moon; half of this multiplied by the digits of the half diameter is the area of the circle of the moon. The excess of the corrected half diameter of the shadow over 0;38,30 is doubled, multiplied by 12 and divided by seventy-seven, the whole diameter of the shadow at the farthest distance; the resulting digits are added to thirty-one digits and a fifth, the least diameter of the shadow: this is the diameter of the shadow in digits; multiplied by three parts, eight minutes and a half it gives the circumference of the circle of the shadow, half of which multiplied by the digits of the half diameter of the shadow is the area of the circle of the shadow. The digits of the diameter of the shadow and of the diameter of the moon are added: the digits of the two diameters. The notes: Plato agrees with the codex in the passage on the two times, which Nallino holds to be corrupt and restores; the codex has 0;18,30 for 0;38,30.
