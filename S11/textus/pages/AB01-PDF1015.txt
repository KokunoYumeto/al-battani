The sighting of the new moon with an instrument: in a place with an open horizon a post or the like is set up, as high as a person's stature, its top level, balanced with the plumb line and parallel to the plane of the horizon; a circle of any size is drawn on it about a centre, with the directions of east, west, south and north marked as in the finding of the meridian line, and the quarter of the circle on the side of the new moon is divided into ninety parts; a straight ruler or a hollow tube is laid with its edge, or the middle of its bore, on the centre of the circle and on the distance of the azimuth of the new moon from the east or the west point; the astrolabe is hung from the hand with the end of its alidade at the apparent altitude of the moon that was found, and the end of the ruler or the tube next to the new moon is raised from the plane of the circle on a prop, without leaving the azimuth of the moon and the centre of the circle, until the sight passes through the two holes of the alidade along the edge of the ruler or the middle of the tube: at the time of the sighting the observer then sees the new moon along the ruler or through the tube; and this is the figure of what was described. Then a new paragraph, beside the figure: the circle of the horizon ABGD is drawn about the centre E, the place of the zenith in the plane; A is the south point, B the east point, G the north point and D the west point; the two lines AG and BD are drawn, and the moon is supposed in [...]. 22 lines; the figure (two circles, an arc, four lines, seventeen letters and words) stands at the left beside lines 14 to 22; three notes in two lines; the printer's signature 18 at the foot. In line 10 the print has «الدئراة» for «الدائرة» (corrected in Nallino's list of corrigenda): kept as printed.
