% is added to the centre) when the planet's corrected anomaly is less than 180 and taken from it when it is more
% than 180; what remains is the planet's distance in the sphere of the signs from the farthest point of the
% eccentric. Then, its opening words between the ornamental brackets, the cause of the retrogradation of the
% planets: in some places of the epicycle the change of the planet's equation in one day is more than its mean
% motion, the motion of the centre of the epicycle on the eccentric for a day, so that the planet's place in the
% sphere of the signs falls short of the place where it was before. This happens only in the nearer half of the
% epicycle, which is less than the upper half, for the arc of the eccentric bounds the two halves: the lower half
% is the arc ش و, the upper the arc و لا ي ش. When the planet, coming from the farthest point of the epicycle,
% reaches و, it is as if standing still, for it lies on the line from ه that touches the epicycle; no motion shows
% until it passes و; in the lower half its motion in the sphere of the signs is seen backward until it reaches ش,
% where it lies on the touching line a second time and stands as if still; when it rises from ش its motion is
% seen forward again as long as it is in the upper half. The planet itself has no retrogradation; it appears so to
% us from the difference of the centres of its spheres and its state on the epicycle. The sun and the moon show
% none, because the motion of each in a day is many times the change of its equation in the day. The page ends,
